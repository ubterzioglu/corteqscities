Westdale Park
15200 Westdale Drive
Bakersfield, CA 93314
Westdale Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 9.3 acres and includes the following amenities:
Softball diamond (backstop only)
Multi-purpose field
One basketball court
Volleyball courts
Tennis courts
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Group picnic shelter
Children’s play area
Open play area
Mature Shade
Directions to the park:
Take Jenkins Road south from Rosedale Highway. Turn west on Brimhall Road. Access Westdale Park by turning north onto Westdale Drive from Brimhall Road. The park site is located at the northwest corner of Westdale Drive and Brimhall Road immediately south of the Centennial Elementary School campus.
