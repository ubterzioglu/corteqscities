Where bold storytelling, local talent, and unforgettable nights come together on one stage.
The Ovation Theatre was built on a simple belief:
great stories deserve a great stage, and great communities deserve a place to experience them together.
What began as a vision to bring high-quality, accessible theatre to the Central Coast has grown into something much more. The Ovation is a home for performers, creatives, and audiences who believe in the power of live performance to connect, challenge, and inspire.
From bold musicals to intimate productions, every show is crafted with intention. Every seat in the house matters. And every audience member becomes part of the experience.
At The Ovation Theatre, you are not just watching a performance. You are part of something alive.

Our Vision
To unleash the power of theatre to show how live performance can reflect and impact our community.
Our Mission
The Ovation Repertory Theatre, is a semi-professional theater company with a mission to produce epic, moving, topical, and contemporary musicals and plays, reinvent classics, develop new work, and engage its community by offering a place where young artists can learn and hone their craft professionally.
Core Values
Organize a professional culture that encourages the rehearsal hall to be a place of experimentation and collaboration.
Value excellence in all aspects of our endeavor.
Achieve by building a healthy, dynamic and powerful artistic community.
Throughout the organization and within the community the Ovation will champion diversity.
Instill in the public a life‐long love and participation in the theater arts.
Operate in a way that strives to advance the field.
Nourish and engage, a diverse and responsive staff, audience and community.
The Ovation Theatre: Stages
The Mainstage
Our Mainstage is the heart of The Ovation Theatre, where stories come to life in a space designed for connection, clarity, and impact.
Thoughtfully configured to balance intimacy with scale, the Mainstage ensures that every seat in the house offers a strong visual and emotional connection to the performance. Whether you are seated in the front row or further back, the experience remains immersive, drawing you into the world on stage from the very first moment.
The design of the space allows performers and audiences to meet in a shared energy. Every note carries. Every line lands. Every subtle expression is visible and felt. It is a room built not just for viewing, but for experiencing live theatre in its fullest form.
From large-scale productions to character-driven performances, the Mainstage supports a wide range of storytelling while maintaining a consistent sense of closeness between artist and audience.

The Ovation Underground
Beneath the Mainstage, a different kind of theatre experience takes shape.
Ovation Underground is an intimate, lower-level performance space designed to bring audiences closer than ever to the work. Built around a three-quarter round black box, the room surrounds the action, creating a shared energy between performer and audience that is immediate and electric.
There is no distance here. No separation.
Actors move within arm’s reach. Every glance, every breath, every moment lands with clarity and impact. It’s a space built for bold storytelling, immersive performances, and creative risk.
Stage Left Bar
Before the curtain rises and after the final bow, the experience continues at Stage Left Lounge.
This welcoming space is designed for connection and conversation. Whether you’re enjoying a pre-show drink, meeting friends after a performance, or simply taking in the atmosphere, Stage Left Bar is an extension of the theatre experience.
It’s where the night begins. And often, where it lingers.

FAQs
Tickets & Purchasing
How do I purchase tickets?
Tickets can be purchased online by selecting your preferred performance date and time.
→ Visit our Tickets page for a step-by-step guide.
Can I buy tickets at the theatre?
Yes, tickets may be available at the door prior to the performance, subject to availability. We recommend purchasing in advance to guarantee your seat.
Can I choose my seats?
Yes. During checkout, you can select your preferred seats using our interactive seating map.
→ Learn more on our Tickets page.
What happens if a show is sold out?
If a performance is sold out, additional tickets may occasionally become available. We recommend checking back or selecting an alternate date.
→ View all available performances on our Shows page.
Do you offer discounts or group rates?
Discounted ticket options for students, seniors, and veterans may be available depending on the production. Group tickets are available for parties of 10 or more.
→ Please contact the Box Office for group inquiries.
How will I receive my tickets?
Tickets are delivered digitally via email after purchase. You can present your ticket on your mobile device or bring a printed copy to the theatre.
→ See full details on our Tickets page.
Attending a Show
What time should I arrive?
We recommend arriving 30 to 60 minutes before showtime to allow time for parking, check-in, and concessions. The Stage Left Lounge opens one hour before all performances.
→ Plan your visit on our About page.
What happens if I arrive late?
Late seating is handled at the discretion of the theatre and may be delayed until an appropriate break in the performance.
How long are performances?
Performance lengths vary by production.
→ Refer to the individual Show page for run time and intermission details.
Are there intermissions?
Many productions include a 20-minute intermission.
→ Details are listed on each Show page.
Are children allowed?
Some productions are family-friendly, while others are intended for mature audiences.
→ Please review the age recommendation on each Show page.
Seating & Accessibility
Is seating reserved?
Yes, seating is assigned at the time of purchase, allowing you to select your preferred seats.
Is the theatre accessible?
Ovation Theatre is committed to providing an accessible experience, with designated accessible seating available.
How do I request accommodations?
If you require specific accommodations, please contact us in advance so we can assist you.
→ Contact the Box Office.
Food, Drinks & Lounge
What is the Stage Left Bar?
The Stage Left Bar is our on-site gathering space where guests can enjoy drinks, conversation, and the atmosphere of the theatre before and after performances and during intermission.
Is food or drink available?
Yes. Beverages and light refreshments are available for purchase.
Can I bring drinks into the theatre?
Beverages purchased at Stage Left Bar may be brought into the theatre. We ask that cups be returned to the bar area after the performance.
Are outside food or drinks allowed?
Outside food and beverages are not permitted.
Location & Parking
Where is Ovation Theatre located?
Ovation Theatre is located in Downtown Bakersfield in the heart of the Arts District.
→ Get directions on our Contact page.
Where should I park?
Street parking and nearby public lots are available within walking distance of the theatre.
Is parking free?
Parking availability and pricing may vary depending on location and time. We recommend allowing extra time before your performance.
Are there places to visit nearby?
Yes. The Downtown Arts District offers restaurants, bars, and cultural destinations, making it easy to plan a full evening around your visit.
Dress Code & Etiquette
Is there a dress code?
There is no formal dress code. Guests are encouraged to dress comfortably or elevate their look for a night out.
Can I take photos or videos during the show?
Photography and video recording during performances are strictly prohibited due to rights restrictions and out of respect for performers and guests.
Should I silence my phone?
Yes. Please silence all devices before the performance begins.
Policies & Safety
What items are not allowed inside the theatre?
Prohibited items include outside food and beverages, recording devices, and anything that may disrupt the performance experience.
What happens in case of an emergency?
Our staff is trained to ensure guest safety. In the event of an emergency, please follow instructions from theatre personnel.
