Lengthwise brewery: Handcrafted in Bakersfield, California. Originated 2002.
Located just down the street from the original brewery of 1998 (faithfuls fondly remember our very humble beginning), the Brewery offers a 360 degree view of the brewing process. Lengthwise customers can enjoy a full menu of brew, grub, to-go beer, and fresh roasted coffee. If it’s a brewing day, (which fortunately most are!), customers can observe the brewers hard at work while they sip a Centennial and dine on Beer Bathed Fish n’ Chips. The Brewery houses a vintage motorcycle collection and art by local artists. The spacious pub is also available for large parties. The Brewery Banquet Room, nestled within the equipment, comfortably hosts private groups of 35. The popular Beer Garden, outdoors and cooly misted for summer, can also be reserved for groups of 200+. This location showcases what made Lengthwise Bakersfield’s favorite microbrewery; Great Beer, Great Food, Great Service and Good Friends.
Take a look around!
