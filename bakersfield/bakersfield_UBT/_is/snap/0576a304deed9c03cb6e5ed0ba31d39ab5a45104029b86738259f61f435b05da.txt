Portrait of a Warrior Gallery moving, will become Woolworth’s neighbor next year
Robert Price
2 months ago
BAKERSFIELD, Calif. (KGET) — The Portrait of a Warrior Gallery is moving.
The nonprofit gallery, which since its founding in 2018 has strived to honor the local fighting men and women who served and died for this country, is doubling its space in the process.
The Gallery is moving into the former home of the Mi Casa furniture store at 19th and K streets – directly across K from the historic Woolworth building and catty-corner from the Kress building.

The existing Portrait of a Warrior gallery, at 20th and Eye streets, is about 4,200 square feet.
The new gallery in the nearby Eastchester district, which could open as soon as February, is about 7,500 square feet, not including its ample basement.
