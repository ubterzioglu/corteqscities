Helen F. Chavez Elementary
Helen F. Chavez Elementary School will lead the Bakersfield City School District in academic excellence. It will be a safe and clean environment where all students are excited to be at school on time, every day, and ready to learn. Chavez Elementary School first opened in 1994. The school is named after Helen F. Chavez, the civil rights activist who fought for farm laborer’s rights in the Central California area and beyond.
