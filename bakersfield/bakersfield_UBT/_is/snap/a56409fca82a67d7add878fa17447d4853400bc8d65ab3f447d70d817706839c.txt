Volunteer Income Tax Assistance Program (VITA)
Volunteer Income Tax Assistance Program
Every tax season, usually beginning in the first week of February, community members, students, faculty, and staff will have the opportunity to take advantage of free income tax preparation services provided by California State University, Bakersfield accounting student volunteers. These students are fully trained and passed certification tests required by the IRS.
Free tax preparations provide not only external community members but also students, staff, and faculty, with complete tax returns in a timely fashion, enabling them to receive proper tax credits and refunds. Services are provided on campus and by appointment only, every Saturday from 10:00 am - 4:00 pm starting February through the end of March at the Business Development Center Room 218A. Those interested are encouraged to call (661) 654-3356 to make an appointment.
Refer to the “Important documents to Bring with You” below.
For additional information, please contact Dr. Benjamin Bae at bbae@csub.edu.

Schedule An Appointment
The CSUB Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) program provides free services to students, staff, faculty, and community members with W-2 income only to prepare individual income tax forms.
CSUB VITA staff meet every Saturday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM starting February in the Business Development Center (BDC) room 264A.
Alternatively, you may schedule an appointment by calling (661) 654-3356 (English and Spanish).
For more information, contact Dr. Benjamin Bae at bbae@csub.edu.
