Rasmussen Senior Center & Park
115 East Roberts Lane
Bakersfield, CA 93308
661.392.2030
The Rasmussen Senior Center is located in the Oildale Area of Bakersfield.
The center is on 4.4 acres and includes the following amenities:
Rasmussen Senior Center
Community center with activity rooms
Large main dining hall and kitchen
Open turf
Horseshoe Pits
Bocce Ball
Mature shade
Rasmussen Senior Center is located approximately one block east of North Chester at the intersection of Roberts Lane and Hart Street.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures
Community Center & Business Office Hours:
Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
