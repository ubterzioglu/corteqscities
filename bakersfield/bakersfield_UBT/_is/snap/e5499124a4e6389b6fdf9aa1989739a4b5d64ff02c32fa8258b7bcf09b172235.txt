Come see us on District Blvd.
We're in Suite 18 at 5880 District Blvd in southwest Bakersfield, just off Ashe Road. Free parking out front, no reservations needed, and the kitchen is open whenever we are.

Good to know
Parking
Free lot out front, with more spaces around the side when it fills up on event nights.
Kids
Welcome. There is a kids menu and the restrooms have changing tables.
Groups
Small groups can just walk in. For parties of about 15 or more, call ahead so we can set the room up.
Not a beer drinker?
We also pour wine by the glass, cider, cocktails, hard seltzers, nonalcoholic beer and soft drinks. Just ask.
Live music
Bands and leagues are booked in advance. Check the events calendar before you plan your night.
Food & dogs
Full kitchen open all hours we are. Dogs are welcome on the patio.
