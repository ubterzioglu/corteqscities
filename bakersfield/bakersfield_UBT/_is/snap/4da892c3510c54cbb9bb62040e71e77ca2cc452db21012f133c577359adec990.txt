BarrelHouse Bakersfield
We’ve wanted to open a taproom in Bakersfield for quite some time, but we were waiting for just the right location—and we finally found it. Our newest taproom is one of our biggest yet—featuring the largest indoor stage we’ve ever built, 30 taps of ice-cold craft beer, endless events, free concerts, a sprawling outdoor beer garden, beer-to-go, local food purveyors, and much, much more.
We’ve been grinding to bring BarrelHouse Bakersfield to life — and we’re ready to celebrate.
Join us for a Grand Opening Party open to the public, because this space deserves a proper first cheers!
You’ll find us at 4200 Resnik Ct, Bakersfield, CA 93313, the former Great Change Brewing Company. We’re completely revamping the space and doing it the BarrelHouse way—it takes a little time, but rest assured, we’re working hard to open as soon as possible. Here’s the official Press Release.
Keep an eye on this page for updates and timelines. Cheers—we can’t wait to raise a pint with you, Bakersfield!
