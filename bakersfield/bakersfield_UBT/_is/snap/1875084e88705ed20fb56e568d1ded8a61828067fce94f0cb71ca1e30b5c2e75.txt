North Rosedale Park
3635 Jewetta Avenue
Bakersfield, CA 93312
North Rosedale Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 14.4 acres and includes the following amenities:
Four lighted softball diamonds
Multi-purpose field
Basketball court
Tennis courts
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Group picnic shelter
Children’s play area
1 Volleyball Court
Open play area
Mature shade
Directions to the park:
North Rosedale Park is located on the northwest corner of Meacham Road and Jewetta Avenue. The park is immediately north of the Rosedale North Elementary School campus.
