Overview
Located in downtown Bakersfield, audience members will enjoy exceptional dinner service with an outstanding off-Broadway musical or comedy performance. With show runs of six weeks throughout the year there is always a reason to visit Stars. Past favorites include “Guys & Dolls,” “A Chorus Line,” “Funny Girl,” and “Annie Get your Gun.”
