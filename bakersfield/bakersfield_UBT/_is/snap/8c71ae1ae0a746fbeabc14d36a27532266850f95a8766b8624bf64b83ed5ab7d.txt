Interested in taking part in the Makers Market?
Are you a local artist or artisan interested in showcasing and selling your work at Maker’s Market? We’re always excited to welcome new creators into our community! Becoming a vendor at Maker’s Market is a great opportunity to share your unique creations, connect with art lovers, and earn money by selling your work. If you’re passionate about your craft and want to reach a wider audience, we’d love to hear from you. Simply click the button below to apply and start your journey as a vendor with us!
Requirements:
• Personal statement
• Three examples of your work
• The fee is 30% of sales. No sales, no fee.
• All items must be tagged with your business logo
• Vendor must provide their own merchandise bags

What is Second Saturday?
Second Saturday is part of a larger, city-wide celebration of local art and small business that takes place one Saturday each month in downtown Bakersfield. As part of this grander event, the Arts Council of Kern hosts our own vendor fair from 9 AM to 1 PM inside our building at 1020 18th Street.
It’s a fun and creative opportunity for artists, makers, and vendors to sell their work, meet the community, and even create live during the event. Since we’re not usually open on Saturdays, Second Saturday gives artists a special extra day each month to connect, showcase, and make sales in a festive setting.
