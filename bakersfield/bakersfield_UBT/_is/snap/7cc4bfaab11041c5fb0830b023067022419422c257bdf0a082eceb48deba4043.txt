Frequently Asked Questions
Helpful answers before you visit CALM Zoo.
Find quick answers about visiting, tickets, memberships, accessibility, food, events, animals, and general guest information.
What are CALM Zoo’s hours?
CALM Zoo is generally open Tuesday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Online ticket sales end at 3:00 PM.
Please visit our Plan Your Visit page for current hours, seasonal updates, and admission information.
Where is CALM Zoo located?
CALM Zoo is located at 10500 Alfred Harrell Hwy, Bakersfield, CA 93306.
Get directions to CALM Zoo .
Do you have a zoo map?
Yes. Guests may download the current CALM Zoo map before arriving.
Download the CALM Zoo Map
Can I purchase tickets online?
Yes. General admission tickets may be purchased online for the quickest entry experience. Online ticket sales end at 3:00 PM.
Buy admission tickets online .
Do you offer memberships?
Yes. CALM Zoo memberships are available and provide a great way to support the zoo while enjoying repeat visits throughout the year. A variety of memberships are available for individuals, families, and supporters.
Visit the Memberships page to learn more.
Can I bring outside food or drinks?
Yes. Guests are encouraged to bring their own picnic and enjoy the zoo grounds. Alcohol is prohibited.
CALM Zoo currently does not have a full food-service option other than snacks, so planning ahead is recommended.
Is drinking water available?
Yes. Filtered water is available for bottle fill-ups. Guests are encouraged to bring refillable water bottles, especially during warmer months.
Are strollers and wheelchairs available?
Strollers and wheelchairs are available to rent on a first-come, first-served basis. Availability may vary by day.
Is CALM Zoo accessible?
CALM Zoo works to provide a welcoming experience for all guests. Because the zoo is an outdoor facility, some paths and areas may vary depending on weather and grounds conditions.
For specific accessibility questions, please contact CALM Zoo before your visit at 661-546-8940.
Can I bring my pet?
Pets are not permitted inside CALM Zoo. Service animals are allowed in accordance with applicable guidelines. For questions about service animal access, please contact CALM Zoo before your visit.
Can I feed or touch the animals?
Guests may not feed, touch, or disturb zoo animals unless participating in an approved CALM Zoo experience supervised by staff or trained volunteers.
What should I wear when visiting?
CALM Zoo is primarily an outdoor experience. Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, weather-appropriate clothing, and water are recommended.
Do you host events?
Yes. CALM Zoo hosts seasonal events, fundraisers, member experiences, and family-friendly activities throughout the year.
Visit the Events page for current and upcoming events.
Can I host a private event at CALM Zoo?
Yes. CALM Zoo offers rental opportunities, including pavilion rentals and private events through The Willows at CALM.
Visit the Rentals page to learn more.
How can I schedule a field trip?
CALM Zoo offers educational opportunities for schools and groups. Visit the Education page to learn more about field trips and learning experiences.
How can I volunteer?
Visit the Volunteer page to learn more about available opportunities, orientation requirements, and how to get involved with CALM Zoo.
How can I support CALM Zoo?
You can support CALM Zoo through memberships, donations, event attendance, volunteering, sponsorships, and community involvement.
Visit Master Plan to learn more about CALM’s long-term vision.

Our team is happy to help with questions about visits, events, memberships, volunteering, donations, accessibility, and guest services.
