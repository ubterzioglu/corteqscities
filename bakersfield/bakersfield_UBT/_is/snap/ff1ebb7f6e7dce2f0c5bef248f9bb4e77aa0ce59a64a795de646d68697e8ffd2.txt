Operation Safe Park happening this weekend at Panorama Park
Post Date:02/17/2022 11:20 AM
Beginning Saturday, February 19, 2022, Kern County Park Rangers will conduct directed law enforcement at Panorama Park.
Operation Safe Park will last through 2 a.m. Monday, February 21, in an effort to mitigate illegal activity at one of the most beloved recreational areas in Kern County.
Law enforcement operations will be elevated at Panorama Park throughout this period, with a focus on maximum enforcement of County ordinance, parking, traffic, and California Penal Code violations, particularly between the hours of 10 p.m. and 2 a.m.
Panorama Park spans 38 acres and runs 1.5 miles along Panorama Drive in northeast Bakersfield. This location is part of the Kern River Parkway and is among the most popular County-owned parks for Bakersfield and Kern County residents.
Panorama Park is closed to the public from 10 p.m. to 5 a.m. every day, including the small parking lot located at Panorama Drive and River Boulevard. Parking along the length of the park on the north side of Panorama Drive between the hours of 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. is prohibited.
