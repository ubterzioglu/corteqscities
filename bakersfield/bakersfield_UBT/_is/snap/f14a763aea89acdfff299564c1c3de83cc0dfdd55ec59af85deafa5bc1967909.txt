Tutoring Services

Kern Literacy Council offers a comprehensive range of tutoring services designed to enhance literacy skills and promote academic success across all age groups and backgrounds.
Our services include:
One-on-One Tutoring: Tailored sessions provide personalized attention to address individual learning styles, strengths, and areas needing improvement.
Small Group Tutoring: Collaborative sessions under the guidance of knowledgeable tutors foster peer learning and a supportive environment.
Literacy Assessments: Thorough evaluations, including reading fluency, comprehension, writing skills, and vocabulary proficiency assessments, help identify students' current skill levels and areas for growth.
Customized Learning Plans: Based on assessment results, tutors develop personalized plans outlining specific goals, objectives, and strategies to address weaknesses and build on strengths.
Flexible Scheduling: We offer convenient scheduling options, including online, after school, evenings, and weekends, to accommodate students' busy schedules.
Access to Learning Resources: Our program provides access to a variety of educational materials, books, online resources, and technology tools to complement tutoring sessions and enhance the learning experience.
Progress Monitoring: Continuous monitoring, regular assessments, and feedback sessions allow us to track progress, celebrate achievements, and adjust learning plans as needed.
Community Partnerships: Collaborations with organizations such as the Kern County Library and community resource centers extend our reach and provide additional support to students, connecting them with resources beyond traditional tutoring settings.
Overall, Kern Literacy Council's tutoring services aim to empower individuals to reach their full potential through improved literacy skills and academic achievement. We are committed to fostering lifelong learning and a passion for reading and education in our community.

Evidence Based Individual Reading Inventory Assessments.
​Tutors utilize tailored learning plans based on the results of the Ekwoll Shakner assessment. These plans serve as roadmaps for tutoring sessions, focusing on specific areas where improvement is needed. In conjunction with Challenger Level workbooks, carefully selected to align with each student's learning plan, tutors provide targeted instruction to gradually challenge reading and comprehension skills. Additionally, study aids such as flashcards, reading guides, and vocabulary lists are offered to enhance understanding and retention.
The program also extends its impact beyond the tutoring sessions by providing students with books to take home. This practice encourages independent reading practice, reinforcing newly acquired skills and fostering a lifelong love of reading. Furthermore, by enrolling children aged 0 to 5 in the Dolly Parton Imagination Library, the program plants the seeds of literacy at an early age.
Burlington English
Kern Literacy Council tutoring services utilizes Burlington English Soft Skills course, contributing to the development of problem-solving, critical thinking, and study skills. The incorporation of technology, including the Learning Management System's full inventory of homework assignments, quizzes, and assessments, allows for detailed tracking of progress, enabling tutors and program coordinators to provide customized support aligned with each student's needs. In summary, Kern Literacy Council tutoring services assessment tools and materials create a dynamic and personalized learning environment, ensuring that each individual's educational journey is both effective and enriching.
Become a volunteer tutor
To become a tutor at Kern Literacy Council, individuals need to meet specific requirements to ensure they are well-equipped to support the educational needs of the community. The key requirements for prospective tutors include:
Educational Qualifications: Tutors are required to have a high school diploma or equivalent. This foundational educational background establishes a minimum level of academic proficiency.
Communication Skills: Strong verbal and written communication skills are essential for effective interaction with students. Tutors should be capable of articulating concepts clearly and providing guidance in a supportive manner.
Pass Background Check: All tutors working with minors in our Child Basic Education program must pass a background. check prior to being connected with a student.
Commitment to Community Engagement: Tutors should be dedicated to community service and have a genuine interest in contributing to the improvement of literacy and educational outcomes within the community.
Flexibility: The ability to adapt to the diverse needs of students is a valuable quality. Tutors may encounter students with varying learning styles and preferences, requiring flexibility in tutoring approaches.
Collaborative Spirit: Tutors often collaborate with the Education Services Coordinator and other volunteers. A collaborative spirit ensures effective teamwork and enhances the overall impact of the program.
Completion of Orientation: Prospective tutors are required to participate in tutor orientations conducted by the Kern Literacy Council. This orientation provides essential insights into the program's goals, processes, and expectations. Once a tutor has been onboarded with Kern Literacy Council the Education Services Coordinator will connect the tutor with their student and select a partner agency site for tutoring services.
These requirements collectively contribute to building a team of tutors who are not only academically qualified but also possess the interpersonal and organizational skills necessary for fostering a positive and supportive learning environment within the Kern Literacy Councils Education Services Porgam.
