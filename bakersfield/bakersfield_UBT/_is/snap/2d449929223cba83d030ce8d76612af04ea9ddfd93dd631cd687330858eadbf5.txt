Ready to get started?
Social Security uses Login.gov to allow you to sign in to your official my Social Security account safely and securely. The first step in creating your account is deciding which sign in option to use. If you are not sure which account to choose, continue with Login.gov. If you already have a Login.gov or ID.me account, use it to sign in to my Social Security.
