Ride Sharing
Lyft and Uber are the only ridesharing companies authorized to operate from BFL. To use Lyft or Uber, download their app on your mobile device. Arriving passengers should collect their bags prior to requesting a Lyft or Uber and then proceed to the arrivals curb.
