Madison Grove Park
10115 Norris Road
Bakersfield, CA 93312
Madison Grove Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 12 acres and includes the following amenities:
Open Play Area
Restrooms
Group picnic shelter
Family picnic pads
Children’s play area
Water play feature
Two tennis courts
Basketball court
Turf volleyball
Multi-use play field
Walking paths
Directions to the park:
Madison Grove Park is at 10115 Norris Road. From Calloway Drive, turn west on Norris Road. The park is on the west side of Norris Road where the road curves to the north.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures
Community Center & Business Office Hours:
Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
