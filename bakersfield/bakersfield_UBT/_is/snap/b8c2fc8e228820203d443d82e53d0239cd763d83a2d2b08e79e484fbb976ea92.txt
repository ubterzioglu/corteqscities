Plan Your Day
Make the most of your visit.
CALM Zoo currently sits on 14 acres and is an outdoor experience with native California animals, shaded areas, walking paths, and family-friendly habitats.
Allow Time to Explore
Plan enough time to walk the grounds, visit animal habitats, and enjoy the outdoor setting.
Dress for Weather
CALM Zoo is primarily outdoors, so comfortable shoes, sun protection, and water are recommended.
Family Friendly
Bring the family for a relaxed day of learning, exploring, and connecting with California wildlife.
Helpful Information
A few things to know before you arrive.
CALM Zoo is an outdoor experience. Planning ahead helps make your visit more comfortable and enjoyable.
Picnics
Bring Your Own Food
Guests are encouraged to bring a picnic and enjoy the zoo grounds. Alcohol is prohibited.
Water
Refill Stations
Filtered water is available for bottle fill-ups. Guests are encouraged to bring refillable water bottles.
Rentals
Strollers & Wheelchairs
Strollers and wheelchairs are available to rent on a first-come, first-served basis while supplies last.
