Gold Runner
San Francisco Bay Area / Sacramento
Bakersfield / Southern California
6 hours 15 minutes Multiple Departures Daily
The Gold Runner provides a great way to connect your travel throughout California and the San Joaquin Valley. The San Joaquin Valley is California's premier agricultural region, bordered on the west by the coastal mountain ranges and on the east by California's magnificent Yosemite, Kings Canyon, Sequoia and Death Valley National Parks. The Gold Runner runs multiple times daily between the San Francisco Bay Area (or Sacramento) and Bakersfield, where Amtrak Connection buses connect you to great Southern California destinations. Other stops along the way include Stockton, Modesto, Merced, Martinez and Fresno. Easy bus connections to San Francisco are made at Emeryville.

Travel to the doorstep of four of America’s greatest national parks. Gaze in wonder beneath the towering giants in Sequoia and Kings Canyon. Bask in the shadow of Yosemite’s El Capitan. Route Highlights
Sacramento, California
Before it was a bustling state capital, Sacramento was a stop on the Pony Express. Relive the city’s pioneer spirit in Old Town and Sutter’s Fort State Historic Park, or explore the Capitol building’s classic architecture.
Enrich your railroading adventure by exploring the beauty and brawn of the locomotives and coaches that forged the Golden State’s history at the California State Railroad Museum.
Route Highlights
Los Angeles, California
Los Angeles, a hub of culture, offering good eats, plenty of entertainment and some of the most stunning beaches. Enjoy the almost year-round sunshine while exploring cities like Venice, Hollywood and Santa Monica. Whether you’re looking to savor diverse cuisine or soak up the lively atmosphere, LA promises unforgettable experiences.
Route Highlights
Yosemite, California
Discover the magic of the Half Dome, amazing cliff views and vast meadows full of wildﬂowers. Yosemite’s landscapes promise an unforgettable experience in nature. Don’t forget to stop by the local eateries after a long day of outdoor adventure.
The best part is your shuttle and admission is included to Yosemite National Park when you take the Gold Runner.
Route Highlights
Martinez, California
More than just the birthplace of the martini, Martinez is the perfect walkable destination with streets lined with shops, breweries and local eateries.
The train ride from Fresno is about three hours, so make a day of it with friends and family in this historic landmark that is one of California’s ﬁrst towns and was home to John Muir.
Route Highlights
San Francisco, California
San Francisco has topped the list of just about every experience there is to be had from dinning to theater, museums, hotels and more. Create memories with friends and family that will last a lifetime by visiting the iconic city that has been one of the top tourist destinations in the world.
Pier 39, Union Square, and the Exploratorium are a must for your next trip to the Bay.
