Most Californians Can Apply

You can apply if you live in California and are a citizen or have a lawful immigration status. Plans are available with or without financial help, depending on your income.

How to Apply

You'll need to know what information to gather and how to submit your application.

What You Need

The following information can help you apply faster. You don't need to submit documents up front, since we check your details against government records and information you've already given us. If we need anything else to confirm your eligibility, we'll contact you with the specific documents to provide.

  • Social Security Numbers
    For applicants who have them.
  • Federal Tax Information
    For applicants who file taxes.
  • Immigration Documents
    For non-citizens.
  • Employer and Income Information
    For applicants who earn money.

Ways to Apply

Apply the way that works best for you. Each option is free and confidential:

  • Online: It takes about 30 minutes, and it’s the fastest way. Save your progress and finish anytime. Start your application now.
  • By phone: Get free local help, available in multiple languages. Call us at (800) 300-1506.
  • In-person: It takes about an hour, and it’s available in multiple languages, from a certified enroller or licensed insurance agent.
  • By mail: Paper applications are available in 12 languages — see the list below.

Open Enrollment vs. Special Enrollment

Whether you can apply right now depends on the time of year and your situation.

  • Open Enrollment

    Nov. 1 to Jan. 31

    The time every year when eligible Californians can enroll, renew or change their plan.

  • Special Enrollment

    Available Year-Round

    Open to Californians who recently experienced a major life change, like lost health insurance, got married, had a baby, moved or changed their immigration status.

What Happens After You Enroll

After you enroll and make your first month’s payment, you will receive a welcome letter and a brochure from Covered California. You will also get an enrollment package and membership ID card from your health insurance company.

Check Your Enrollment Status

Sign in to your account and view your Enrollment Dashboard, or you can call our service center at (800) 300-1506.

If you or a family member are eligible for Medi-Cal, visit BenefitsCal.com or contact your local county office.
