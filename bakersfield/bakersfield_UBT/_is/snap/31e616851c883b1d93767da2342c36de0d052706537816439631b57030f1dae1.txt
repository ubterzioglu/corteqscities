McMurtrey Aquatic Center
Share
Overview
The McMurtrey Aquatic Center is a swimming, diving, and recreational facility. Public recreational swim is offered June through August. McMurtrey Aquatic Center recreational swim fees: $4 per person or $13 for a group of 4 people. Lap swim and water walking are offered year round in the competition pool at McMurtrey Aquatic Center. Lane availability may vary based on scheduled activities in the pool.
