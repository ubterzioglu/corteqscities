The Frogs
by Aristophanes
directed by David Melendez
Thursday-Saturday October 22, 23, 24 at 7:30pm
Sunday October 25 at 2pm
Doré Theatre
Go back in time and meet the ancient Greeks in this comedy by Aristophanes. With no first-rate tragic poet in Athens, the god Dionysos embarks on a journey to the underworld in search of the greatest playwright. He stages a contest between renowned poets Euripedes and Aeschylus where they must debate their merits. Filled with humor and satire, The Frogs looks at how a society values artistic expressions.
