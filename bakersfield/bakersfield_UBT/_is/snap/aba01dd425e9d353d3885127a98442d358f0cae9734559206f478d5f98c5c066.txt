Pharmacy Services
Clinica Sierra Vista’s Pharmacies provide affordable medication access to our patients. Our pharmacy team partners directly with the clinic to manage your prescriptions and improve your overall health outcomes.
Our pharmacy services are designed to make medication management seamless, accessible, and supportive for every patient. Our pharmacists provide medication therapy management and education, ensuring that you understand how to use your prescriptions safely and effectively. Prescription filling and refills are handled with care, and we offer drug interaction reviews and consultations to prevent complications, especially for patients managing multiple medications.

Conditions
We support patients managing a wide range of needs including chronic conditions, complex specialty therapies, infectious disease and routine primary care maintenance
Chronic Disease Management Support
We support chronic disease management through collaborative care with providers, offering services like diabetic education, insulin management, and guidance for hypertension medications.
340B Pharmacy Program
For eligible patients, our 340B pharmacy program offers reduced-cost medications to ease financial burdens. Whether you’re navigating complex medication regimens, addressing polypharmacy concerns, or improving adherence, our pharmacy team is here to help you every step of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I request a prescription refill?
You can request a refill by calling the pharmacy directly or using our online refill portal. Please have your prescription number ready.
Can you transfer my prescriptions from another pharmacy?
Yes, we can easily transfer your existing prescriptions. Simply provide us with the name and phone number of your current pharmacy, along with the names of the medications you would like moved.
Do you offer medication counseling?
Absolutely. Our pharmacists are available to answer any questions you have regarding your medications, including proper usage, potential side effects, and drug interactions.
What if I can’t afford my medication?
Please speak with our pharmacy staff. We can discuss potential assistance programs, generic alternatives, or manufacturer coupons that may help reduce your out-of-pocket costs.
What if my medication requires prior authorization?
If your insurance requires prior authorization, our team will work directly with your healthcare provider to help facilitate the process and minimize delays in your therapy.

FAQs
How do I know if my medications are working well together?
Our pharmacists review your medications for interactions and provide consultations to ensure your treatment is safe and effective.
Do you accept insurance?
We accept many major insurance plans and are adding more all the time. To find out if we accept your specific plan, please speak with our pharmacist.
How do I refill my prescription?
Refilling your prescriptions is easy. Simply give us a call or stop by the pharmacy – our friendly pharmacists are always happy to assist you.
Can I transfer my prescription to your pharmacy?
Absolutely! We can transfer most prescriptions. Just provide us with the name and phone number of your previous pharmacy, and we’ll take care of the rest.
Do you have free delivery services?
Absolutely! As of 2026, we now offer free medication delivery from our pharmacies to your home!
How long will it take for my medications to arrive?
Unless there is an unusual delay, we typically deliver your prescriptions the same day or by the next business day. Cutoff time for same-day delivery is 11 a.m. Delivery time depends on your medication type.

Program Objectives
At least 80% of interns complete program/degree requirements within 13.5 months (150% of planned program length).
Of graduates who seek employment, at least 100% are employed in nutrition and dietetics or related fields within 12 months of graduation.
At least 80% of program graduates take the CDR credentialing exam for dietitian nutritionists within 12 months of program completion.
The program’s one-year pass rate (graduates who pass the registration exam within one year of first attempt) on the CDR credentialing exam for dietitian nutritionists is at least 80%.
80% of employers, who return surveys, rate graduates as “satisfactory” or “excellent” for ability to work effectively with multicultural populations from diverse backgrounds.
90% of program graduates work as entry-level Registered Dietitian Nutritionists in community nutrition.
90% of graduates work in Central Valley community programs for at least two years after graduation.
Do I Qualify for WIC?
WIC is a program that helps families with food and nutrition. Call us at (661) 862-5422 or send a message, and we’ll help you see if you qualify.
Here are some ways you might qualify:
You are pregnant
You are breastfeeding a baby who is less than one year old
You had a baby in the last six months
You have a child who is under the age of five
You experienced a loss of pregnancy or had a termination
You also need to think about your family’s income. Here are the monthly income limits for family sizes:
1 Person: $2,413
2 Persons: $3,261
3 Persons: $4,109
4 Persons: $4,957
5 Persons: $5,805
More than 5 Persons: Add $848 for each additional member
This income limit is based on 185% of the Federal Poverty Level, and these amounts will be effective from April 1, 2025, to June 30, 2026.
Also, if you’re pregnant, the baby counts as part of your family size when figuring out if you qualify.
