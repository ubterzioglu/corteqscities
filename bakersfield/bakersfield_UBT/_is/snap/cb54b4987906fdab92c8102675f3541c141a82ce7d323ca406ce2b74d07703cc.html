Orientation

Thank you for deciding to continue your education at our school. We want you to succeed at setting and reaching your goals, developing skills, being effective communicators, and being able to use resources in the community.

 

Bakersfield Adult School is now YOUR SCHOOL! We hope you will take pride in maintaining a clean learning environment. Please do all you can to help us keep your campus clean.

 

Here are some important facts and rules to remember that are part of the contract you must sign to enroll in BAS classes, which include the following:

 

  • When you register, you permit us to contact you by phone or email.
  • Cell phones should be turned off and put away while you are in class.
  • You may only park in designated parking spaces. If you do not display a special permit, you may not park in the accessible parking spaces. Drive slowly and safely.
  • There is no smoking anywhere on our campus, including in your car.
  • We have a snack bar, but it is only available for YOUR PROGRAM during your break times.
  • You may not bring drugs or alcoholic beverages on campus. If you come to school under the influence of alcohol or drugs, you will be suspended or expelled.
  • You are not allowed to carry any weapon at school. Anything that is made to look like a weapon is considered to be a weapon.
  • You may not copy answers or cheat on assignments or tests. This will result in disciplinary action.
  • Children are not allowed in Bakersfield Adult School classrooms. Visitors must report to the office for approval and wear a visitor badge on campus.
  • Please follow our dress code—no disruptive clothing. Shirts and shoes must be worn at all times. Students are not to wear clothing that has writing referring to alcohol, tobacco, drugs, gangs, or sex. If your dress causes a distraction, you will be asked to change.
  • Students must follow the attendance policy of their program.
  • Please keep a positive attitude. Your teachers determine the rules for your classroom. If you show a lack of respect for your teacher, school property (including books), or other students, you may be asked to leave Bakersfield Adult School.
  • We do not allow sexual harassment at Bakersfield Adult School. Please treat each other professionally.
  • Do not comment about someone’s appearance, clothing, or sexual behavior. If someone does not wish to give you personal information or to speak privately with you, do not ask them to do so. You may be dropped from Bakersfield Adult School if you violate this rule.
  • Remember!!! Bakersfield Adult School is YOUR school and your key to success!

 

The Bakersfield Adult School adheres to a philosophy that our students will be enthusiastic and motivated community members who will be prepared for the workplace. Education is our primary concern; their achievements measure our teachers' success.

Footer Statement

The Kern High School District (KHSD) is committed to ensuring equal, fair, and meaningful access to employment and education services. KHSD prohibits discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), intimidation, or bullying in any employment practice, education program, or educational activity on the basis and/or association with a person or group with one or more of these actual or perceived characteristics of age, ancestry, color, ethnic group identification, gender, gender identity or expression, genetic information, immigration status, marital status, medical information, national origin, physical or mental disability, parental status, political affiliation, pregnancy and related conditions, race, religion, retaliation, sex, sexual orientation, military or veterans status, homelessness, foster status, affiliation with the Boy Scouts of America and other designated youth groups, or any other basis prohibited by California state and federal nondiscrimination laws consistent with Education Code 200 and 220, Government Code 11135, and Title IX.
 
If you believe you have been subjected to discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), intimidation, or bullying you should contact your school site principal and/or the District's Equity Compliance/Title IX Officer, Melissa Jimenez, 5801 Sundale Avenue, (661) 827-3173, [email protected], Title II/ADA Officer, SELPA Director Special Education, Greg Hicks 5801 Sundale Avenue, (661) 827-3105, [email protected], and Section 504 Coordinator, Elaine Gregory, 5801 Sundale Avenue, (661) 827-4529, [email protected].  A copy of KHSD's Uniform Complaint policy and Nondiscrimination policy are available at www.kernhigh.org and upon request.
