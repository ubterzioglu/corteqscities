The Historic Bakersfield Fox Theater opened its doors in 1930 and since then has hosted a variety of community events, such as ballets, concerts, film festivals, school graduations, business conferences, church events, dance recitals, comedy shows, pageants, and more!
Ticket window located at: 1700 20th Street Bakersfield, CA 93301
Or reach our box office via phone at (661) 324-1369
Hours of operation: 10:00am – 1:00pm.
For general questions or help with an order, please email us at info@thebakersfieldfox.com.
