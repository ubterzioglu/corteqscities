Support Local Artists at First Friday’s ArtWalk

Hosted by the Arts Council of Kern, the ArtWalk showcasea a diverse array of talents, featuring artists, artisans, and vendors, creating a dynamic atmosphere for attendees. See what amazing hand-crafted goods local Kern County vendors have to offer!

ArtWalk takes place in downtown Bakersfield on the first Friday of every month, from October through June.

The cost to participate as an artist-vendor is $35 per month. Download the ArtWalk application and ACK’s release form below and pay online using Square. Questions? Don’t hesitate to get in touch with us at artwalk@kernarts.org.

ArtWalk on First Fridays FAQs

What is ArtWalk?
ArtWalk offers artists and artisans an opportunity to display and sell their works, and/or

demonstrate their crafts (handmade or designed by the artists.)

When is ArtWalk Held?
ArtWalk takes place on the First Friday of the month in downtown Bakersfield.

Does The Location Change Every Month?
For now, we’re located on the corners of 18th and N Street.

What is The Size of a Space?
Spaces are 10’x10’. There must be room for ArtWalk attendees to walk past your space
safely. No obstructions, please.

Does it Cost to Participate? How Much?
A permit is required at $35 per month. You may purchase more than one month at a time.
You may purchase a maximum of 2 10’x10′ spaces ($35 each) at each ArtWalk.

Can I Reserve the Same Spot Every Month?
If you purchase 6 months or more, you may reserve your spot.

How Are Spaces Allocated?
Other than spaces designated for 6 months or more, spaces are assigned upon registration.

Is Anything Provided?
The space and permit are provided. Participants bring their own tables and table covers,
displays, and chairs.

Is there Electric and Water Hookup?
There is limited electric hookup, but no water. You must tape down your electric cord and
keep it out of the walkways.

What is Set-up and Teardown Time?
Set-up begins at 3:30 PM and teardown no earlier than 9 PM.

Is There a Deadline for Registration?
Deadline for registration is the Wednesday before ArtWalk. Forms and waivers must be
signed and accepted, and payment must be made. Please download forms at
kernarts.org/ArtWalk. Forms are also available at ACK office {address below).

To pay online, please use the pay button above, or contact us at artwalk@kernarts.org.
Payment may be made by mail or in person, if preferred, at 1020 18th Street. Bakersfield 93301 (Monday-Friday)

Every first Friday of the month,

the Arts Council of Kern produces ArtWalk in historic downtown Bakersfield.

Along with artists and artisans, live music set the scene for this FREE, family-friendly event.

Downtown shops and restaurants are also open for business.
Feast your eyes or add to your art collection. Artworks of all forms, clothing, handmade jewelry, and more are onsite for demonstration or purchase.

Bakersfield locals,

often comment about enjoying all that artists and vendors offer. They are grateful for the opportunity to talk with the artists about their art and to watch demonstrations by some.

Local artists and artisans interested in participating, please get in touch with the council for details. Email: info@kernarts.org or phone us at: 661-324-9000.
