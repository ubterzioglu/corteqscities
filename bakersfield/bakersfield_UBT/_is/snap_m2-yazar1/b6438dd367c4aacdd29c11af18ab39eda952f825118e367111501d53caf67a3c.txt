Location
The Department of Music & Theatre Office is located in the Music Building, Room 105.
Office: (661) 654-3093
Fax: (661) 654-6901
The Box Office is located at the front of the Doré Theatre and is open one hour prior to theatre performances and accepts cash, checks, and credit cards. Reservations can be made for Theatre Program performances starting two weeks before the first performance. Call the Box Office at (661) 654-3150.

Directions:
From Stockdale Highway, turn onto Don Hart Drive West. The theatre parking lot is on your right. (Note: There are two entrances into the CSUB campus from Stockdale Highway, Don Hart Drive East and Don Hart Drive West. Don Hart Drive East has a traffic light and a large street sign; this entrance is not close to the theatre. Don Hart Drive West has no stop sign, just a center turn lane; this is the entrance next to the theatre and the one you want to take.)
