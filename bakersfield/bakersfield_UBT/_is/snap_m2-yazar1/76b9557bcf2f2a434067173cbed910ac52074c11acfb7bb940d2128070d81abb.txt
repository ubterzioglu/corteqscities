NOW HIRING:
Executive Director
The Fox Theater Foundation is seeking a dynamic and visionary leader to guide two of Bakersfield’s most
beloved historic landmarks - the iconic Historic Bakersfield Fox Theater and the Historic Nile Theater!
This is a rare opportunity to provide strategic leadership, operational oversight, and community impact for:
• The beautifully restored 1,500-seat Fox Theater (including the intimate Eva’s lounge)
• The Historic Nile Theater, originally opened in 1906 and now being restored as a vibrant 700-capacity
live music and performance venue
Position Highlights:
• Salary range: $100,000 – $160,000 per year
• Full-time, exempt with competitive benefits and discretionary bonus
• Drive programming, fundraising, operations, and historic preservation
If you’re a passionate arts leader with strong nonprofit or venue management experience, excellent fundraising
skills, and a deep commitment to historic preservation and community enrichment, we want to hear from you!
Application Deadline: May 27, 2026 at 11:59 p.m.
Send your resume and/or curriculum vitae, cover letter, and salary expectations to:
personnel@thebakersfieldfox.com
