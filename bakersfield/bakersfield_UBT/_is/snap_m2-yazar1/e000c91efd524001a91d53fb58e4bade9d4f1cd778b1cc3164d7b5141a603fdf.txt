what bags can be brought into the theater?
Beginning September 1, 2026, to help expedite entry and maintain a safe environment, all guests are required to carry belongings in a clear bag.
The following are allowed:
Clear bags
Small clutches/wallets up to 4.5" x 6.5"
Medical and diaper bags (subject to inspection)
All bags and personal items brought into the venue are subject to inspection by security personnel. Guests who arrive with prohibited bags or other restricted items may be required to return them to their vehicle, dispose of them, or may be denied entry at the discretion of venue management. Items will not be held for later pick-up. We reserve the right to expand or modify the items prohibited at any time.

Can anyone regardless of age attend a show at The Nile Theater?
The Nile Theater is typically an all-ages theater, but some shows do specify an age requirement. Please check the event listing in the Calendar or email info@thebakersfieldfox.com. Please note that some shows may be more suitable than others for children and are up to parental discretion.

What is a presale? How do I participate?
Presales are early access ticket sales. These are by invitation only via email and are usually offered by fan clubs or radio stations. The Nile Theater occasionally offers pre-sales to our mailing list members. Please subscribe to our mailing list to receive these pre-sale notices by entering your email on the home page.

When can I pick up my Will Call tickets?
If you placed your tickets under Will Call, tickets can be picked up on weekdays at the Fox Box Office between 10 AM and 1 PM. On the day of the show, tickets can be picked up at The Nile Theater 2 hours prior to the show. Be sure to bring a photo ID and/or your ticket confirmation, as these may be required for ticket pickup.
