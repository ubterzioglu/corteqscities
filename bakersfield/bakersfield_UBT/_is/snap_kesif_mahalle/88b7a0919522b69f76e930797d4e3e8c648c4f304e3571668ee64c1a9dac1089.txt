Caroline P. Harris Elementary
At Harris Elementary, we believe every student can be successful in and outside of the classroom. Harris staff is deeply committed to helping support students academically and socially-emotionally to ensure they are successful in all of their life endeavors. Harris Elementary has been providing educational services since 1960. It is named after Caroline Payne Harris, a dedicated educator, who served as teacher and principal in the Bakersfield City School District for many years. Harris school strives to maintain high-level, educational practices, and to ensure each student receives the attention and instruction they need to succeed in academics and life.
