Transportation
Bakersfield College's main campus is located at 1801 Panorama Drive, Bakersfield, California. The campus is on 153 acres between Panorama Drive and University Avenue and Haley Street and Mt. Vernon Avenue.
From California Highway 178 (CA-178), take the Mt. Vernon or Haley St Exits north to reach the campus.

Transportation Options
Carpool and Rideshare
Bakersfield College encourages you to carpool and rideshare to get to campus. We have several designated drop off locations along the entrance on Haley Street, in front of the Outdoor Theater on the northeast side, and near the entrance of Memorial Stadium.
Bus Transportation
Riding the bus frees you from the struggle with traffic or find parking, and you can focus on your studies as you commute to school. By using public transit, you help limit emissions and improve air quality in the Central Valley.
GET Bus
Golden Empire Transit (GET) provides FREE bus rides to Bakersfield College students from September 2025 - June 2026!
The free passes good for GET Bus fixed route buses and on-demand paratransit only are possible through a grant. Students can pick up a free GET Bus pass at the Office of Student Life during the Fall or Spring semesters. To ride the bus, show your student ID and pass to the driver. Then, swipe your pass through the fare box as you would with a regular bus pass.
LAXcar
Students can consider and utilize a 10% partnership promo code offered by LAXcar if they are planning to come to Bakersfield from LAX. LAXcar can provide vehicle transportation directly to the BC Panorama Campus.
Enter promo code: BC5 when booking. LAXcar also has answers to frequently asked questions such as booking and paying fees, number of luggage per vehicle type, locating your chauffeur, and other possible scenarios.
Please visit the Office of Student life for more information, or email Nicky Damania: nicky.damania@bakersfieldcollege.edu
Gold Runner (Amtrak)
With connections to nearly 30 colleges and universities across the state, the Gold Runner, operated by Amtrak, is an affordable option for students. While traveling, students can make the most of their trip by connecting to complimentary onboard Wi-Fi to complete their studies or unwind with free box office movies; grab a bite of food from the Cafe Car; or catch up on some much-needed rest. Students may receive up to 15% off on everyday travel. Explore the options for your next trip on the Gold Runner website.
Kern Transit
Bakersfield College students can ride all Kern Transit routes for FREE through June 30, 2026. This includes routes to and from the school, as well as other destinations throughout Kern Count. For more information about routes and schedules, visit the Kern Transit Routes & Schedules. To take advantage of this promotion, present your student ID to the bus driver when boarding.
