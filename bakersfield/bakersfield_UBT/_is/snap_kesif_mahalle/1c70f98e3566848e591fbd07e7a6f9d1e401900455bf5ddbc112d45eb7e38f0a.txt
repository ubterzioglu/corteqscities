ABOUT GET
Golden Empire Transit District (GET) was formed in July 1973. It is the primary public transportation provider for the Bakersfield Metropolitan Area. GET is the largest public transit system within a 110-mile radius. The District’s legal boundary includes Bakersfield city limits as well as adjacent unincorporated areas. GET has a fleet of 84 buses equipped with wheelchair lifts and bike racks. GET's fleet is comprised of 70 CNG (compressed natural gas) buses, and 14 hydrogen fuel cell buses. GET also operates 59 compressed natural gas GET-A-LIFT buses. GET services 16 routes, which operate 7 days a week and provides 3.6 million fixed-route trips each year.
GET has an economic impact; every $1.00 GET spends and invests creates $5.80 in return.
