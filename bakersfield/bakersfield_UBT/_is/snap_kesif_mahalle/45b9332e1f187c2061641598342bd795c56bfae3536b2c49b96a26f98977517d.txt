James Curran Middle School
Curran Middle School’s vision is to create a consistent, safe, and inclusive learning community where every student is challenged through grade-level rigorous instruction, supported socially and emotionally, and equipped with the knowledge, skills, and character to thrive in school, career, and life. Our mission is to foster a safe, inclusive, and academically engaging school environment where everyone feels heard, supported, and inspired to succeed. By prioritizing student learning, supporting student and staff well-being, strengthening the campus climate and culture, and elevating instructional practices, we commit to ensuring that everyone will thrive social, emotionally, and academically.Curran Middle School opened in 1960. The school is named after James Curran, a long-time early Bakersfield resident, and businessman. Curran Middle School strives to be a model of educational excellence and a source of pride for students, parents, staff, and the community.
