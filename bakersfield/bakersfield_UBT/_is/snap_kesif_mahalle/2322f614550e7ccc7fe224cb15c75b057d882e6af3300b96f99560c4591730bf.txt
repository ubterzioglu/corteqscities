Wayside Elementary
At Wayside, we are committed to creating a supportive learning environment for our students. We are actively engaged in perfecting our tiered system approach, ensuring that every student’s academic and social-emotional needs are met comprehensively. Our goal is to empower students to take ownership of their learning and explore their interests. Wayside Elementary first welcomed students onto its campus in 1949. Since then, the staff and administration have aimed to provide an outstanding, comfortable academic environment for each and every student that comes through the doors.
