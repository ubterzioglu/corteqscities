How Covered California and Medi-Cal Work Together
When you apply for insurance, we'll check to see if you’re eligible for Medi-Cal or for financial help to lower the cost of a health plan through Covered California. Medi-Cal provides free or low-cost health insurance for people with lower incomes. What you qualify for depends mainly on your income and household size. Learn more about Medi-Cal.
If you don’t want to apply for financial help, you can still buy a Covered California health plan at full price.
