Federal Budget Update

On September 2, 2026, the United States Congress passed a Continuing Resolution that would keep the federal government open through December 11, 2026. WIC offices are open. WIC continues to serve and enroll families. Families should continue to use their WIC benefits and attend their WIC appointments.

Visit WIC’s webpage on the federal budget for updates and information.
