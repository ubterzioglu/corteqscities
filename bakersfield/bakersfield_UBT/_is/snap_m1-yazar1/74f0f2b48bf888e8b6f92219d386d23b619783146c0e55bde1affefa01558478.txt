About Us
Founded in 1996
Dagny’s Coffee Company is a locally owned coffee house, located in downtown Bakersfield on the corner of 20th and Eye Streets. Bakersfield offers something for everyone, and Dagny’s is definitely the place where it all happens. Walk in the door to the smell of fresh coffee brewing, cookies, cakes and scones baking in the kitchen. Dagny’s was founded in the mid 1990’s and has been serving the Bakersfield community with pride ever since.
Dagny’s Coffee Shop, nestled in the heart of Bakersfield, has long been a cherished cornerstone of the community. Known for its warm ambiance and the rich aroma of freshly brewed coffee, Dagny’s is more than just a place to grab a cup of joe—it’s a gathering spot where neighbors connect, stories are shared, and friendships flourish.
Over the years, it has become a symbol of local culture, hosting art shows, poetry readings, and live music that reflect the vibrant spirit of our town. Regulars and newcomers alike find solace in the welcoming environment, where the baristas know your name and your favorite order.
