Overview
Flight Adventure Park is an indoor athletic entertainment facility with 40,000 square feet of adventure, excitement and challenges. Rush features 17,000 square feet of wall to wall trampolines, including 3 dodgeball courts, a battle beam, a foam pit and three slam dunk basketball hoops. Rush also features a climbing area that allows you to challenge yourself or a friend in reaching new heights.
We accommodate our non-participating guests with free wifi, a lounge, vending, and lots of big screen televisions, so you can enjoy the game while your little one’s enjoy the facility.
Rush will continue to add new and exciting elements to ensure that the park offers users a fresh experience.
We are locally owned, operated and dedicated to this community and to our guests. We are enthusiastic about health and fitness. We are devoted to your safety.
Whether you join us for a birthday party or an open jump hour, Flight Adventure Park will be an experience you won’t forget.
