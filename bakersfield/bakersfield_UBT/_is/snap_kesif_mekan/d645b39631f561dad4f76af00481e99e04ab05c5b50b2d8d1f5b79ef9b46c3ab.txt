Caribbean Restaurant Featuring Authentic Caribbean Food in a Family Atmosphere - Bakersfield, California
Takeout Available
Come to Mama Roomba for authentic Caribbean food in a relaxing family-style restaurant. Our family-owned-and-operated Caribbean restaurant is located in Bakersfield, California, and offers all of our dishes "tapas" style, which means small family-style dishes, and is perfect for sharing. Each dish is cooked with different spices grown on the Islands. Among the most commonly used are cinnamon, nutmeg, mace, allspice, and cloves. Other distinctive flavorings include ginger, garlic, coconut, and rum. These flavorings make our dishes more flavorful, enjoyable and fun!
Stop by our Caribbean restaurant's excellent location on Wall Street Alley and Eye Street.
