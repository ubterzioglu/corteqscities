Step Into History
At the Corner of Past & Possibility
The Woolworth’s Building stands in the heart of downtown Bakersfield, a landmark woven into the fabric of the community.
More than a historic site, it’s a gathering place. Home to The Basement, the Luncheonette, Reverb, and Moneywise Wealth Management, the building brings together food, music, and shared experiences all within a space that continues to evolve.
A symbol of Bakersfield then and now, Woolworth’s remains a place where stories are shared, traditions carry on, and new memories take shape.
Come experience it for yourself!

Frequently Asked Questions
Where is The Woolworth’s Building located?
We are located in the heart of downtown Bakersfield, California, at the corner of 19th and K Street. Moneywise is located at 1907 K Street, Bakersfield, CA 93301.
What are the building’s hours of operation?
The Woolworth’s Building is open Monday through Saturday from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. We’re closed on Sundays.
The building is open to the public Monday – Saturday from 10:30 AM to 10:00 PM.
Will parking be available?
Yes, both street parking and nearby public lots are available.
What can I expect to see at The Woolworth’s Building?
Visitors can enjoy:
A meal at the last surviving Woolworth’s Luncheonette, serving classic fare with a modern touch.
Live music, performances, and private events at The Basement, our immersive subterranean venue. The preserved Late Moderne architecture, including terrazzo floors and the original Luncheonette.
Can I take a guided tour of the building?
Yes! Guided tours available upon request. Email inquiries to info@historicwoolworths.com
Is the building accessible for visitors with disabilities?
Absolutely. The Woolworth’s Building is fully accessible, including elevators for reaching all levels and ADA-compliant restrooms.
How can I book The Basement for private events?
Booking is available now! Email inquiries to events@basementatwoolworths.com
Is the Luncheonette open daily?
The Luncheonette is open Monday through Thursday from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday from 11:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., and closed on Sundays, along with the rest of the building.
The Luncheonette is open Monday – Saturday from 10:30 AM to 10:00 PM.
Are there any special events or performances?
Upcoming special events and performances can be found on The Basement venue website. You can also follow @thebasementatwoolworths on Instagram to see what we’re up to!
Can I rent space in the building for my business?
Not currently.
Is photography allowed?
Personal photography is welcome! For professional shoots, please contact us to obtain the necessary permits.
How do I stay updated on building news and events?
Follow us on Instagram and Facebook, or subscribe to our newsletter for the latest updates.
Lost and Found
For any inquiries about lost items please email info@historicwoolworths.com
Where can I buy Woolworths Merchandise?
Merchandise can be purchased on-site in The Luncheonette.
Where can I learn more about the history of the building?
We’re glad you asked! Visit the Explore pages our website for a building history timeline, an inside look at renovation, and a gallery page showcasing all the behind the scenes details.
Still have questions?
For general inquiries, contact us.
