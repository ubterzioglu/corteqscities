Dignity Health Amphitheatre
Share
Overview
Located in the scenic Park at Riverwalk, the Dignity Heath Amphitheatre is Bakersfield’s premier outdoor performance venue. With seating for 4,000 people, the amphitheatre is an exceptional place to experience a concert or hold your next event. With generous production access and great sightlines, it has proven to be an excellent venue for renowned artists such as The Doobie Brothers, Peter Frampton, Earth Wind & Fire, The English Beat, and numerous others. In addition, 370 paved parking spaces are adjacent to the venue. Guest parking is free and adequate handicap spaces are available. A gated lot is located at the entrance to the venue to provide parking for special events. Auxiliary parking is accessible within the park. Food and drink vendors are available.
