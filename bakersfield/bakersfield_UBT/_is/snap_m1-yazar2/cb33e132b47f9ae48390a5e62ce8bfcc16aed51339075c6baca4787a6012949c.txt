The Moo Story
Moo Scratch Kitchen began in 2008 as Moo Creamery, founded on the belief that good food should be made with care. Real ingredients. Real effort. No shortcuts. It started as a small neighborhood restaurant in Bakersfield with bold ideas and homemade bacon-maple ice cream at the center of it all. The recipes were tested in a tiny apartment kitchen. The goal was simple: make food worth remembering.
Over time, Moo grew into a place where people returned again and again. Families gathered here. Friends marked milestones here. The menu expanded with curiosity, always grounded in the same scratch-made approach. From hand-ground burgers and house-made ice cream to chilaquiles and Manchego salads, every dish reflected the team’s dedication to quality.
Even during challenges, Moo stayed consistent. When road closures affected access to the original location, the team adjusted and kept serving. When food trends came and went, Moo kept showing up with real food and genuine hospitality.
In 2015, Moo was featured on Food Network’s Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives. The episode introduced viewers to the peanut butter and bacon burger and bacon ice cream, two long-standing favorites. But long before the cameras showed up, Moo had already earned its place in Bakersfield.
This brand was not created to follow a wave or chase attention. It was built with intention. Built for the people who live here. Built on the belief that doing things the right way still matters.
Moo Scratch Kitchen is a continuation of that belief. Same commitment. Same care. A fresh space to keep doing what we’ve always done.
Loyally Local
We believe real food should be made by real people, using real ingredients. That’s why we do things the hard way—handmaking sauces, grinding burgers, baking bread, and churning ice cream. You can taste the difference.
When we can, we partner with local farmers and producers. It keeps us connected to our roots and gives every dish a sense of place.
