Visitor Guidelines
Help keep CALM Zoo safe, welcoming, and enjoyable for everyone.
CALM Zoo is home to native California animals, plants, and habitats. These guidelines help protect
guests, staff, volunteers, and the animals in our care.
Respect the Animals
Please do not feed, touch, tease, tap on glass, throw objects, or disturb the animals. Many animals at CALM Zoo are non-releasable wildlife and require a calm, safe environment.
Stay on Paths
For your safety and the protection of habitats, please remain on designated guest paths and follow all posted signs.
Supervise Children
Children must be supervised at all times. Please help younger guests enjoy the zoo respectfully and safely.
Outside Food
Guests are welcome to bring a picnic and enjoy the zoo grounds. Alcohol is prohibited.
Water & Weather
CALM Zoo is primarily outdoors. Comfortable shoes, sun protection, weather-appropriate clothing, and refillable water bottles are recommended.
Pets & Service Animals
Pets are not permitted inside CALM Zoo. Service animals are allowed in accordance with applicable guidelines.
Smoking & Vaping
Smoking and vaping are not permitted inside zoo grounds.
Photography
Guest photography is welcome. Please be respectful of other visitors and do not cross barriers or disturb animals for photos.
Accessibility
CALM Zoo works to provide a welcoming experience for all guests. Strollers and wheelchairs may be available to rent on a first-come, first-served basis.
Questions before your visit?
Contact CALM Zoo at 661-546-8940 for current visitor information,
accessibility questions, or guest services support.
