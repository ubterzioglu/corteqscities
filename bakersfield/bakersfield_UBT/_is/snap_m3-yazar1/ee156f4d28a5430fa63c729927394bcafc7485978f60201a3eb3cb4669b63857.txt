General
General Questions
What does The BLVD! Bakersfield offer?
The BLVD! Bakersfield is Bakersfield's premier family entertainment center featuring 22 bowling lanes (8 premium VIP lanes), 65+ arcade and VR games, a high-intensity laser tag arena, an indoor ropes course, karaoke, billiards, bocce, private birthday party rooms, corporate event spaces, and a full-service gastro pub — located at 3200 Buck Owens Blvd, Bakersfield, CA 93308.
Where is The BLVD! Bakersfield located?
The BLVD! Bakersfield is located at 3200 Buck Owens Blvd, Bakersfield, CA 93308, just minutes from Downtown Bakersfield with easy access from Highway 99 and Highway 58. Free parking is available on-site.
What are The BLVD! Bakersfield's hours?
Sunday–Thursday 11am–9pm, Friday–Saturday 11am–12am. See our contact page for full details.
Is The BLVD! Bakersfield good for families with kids?
Absolutely. The BLVD! Bakersfield offers all-ages entertainment including bumper bowling, 65+ arcade games, laser tag (waiver required), billiards, and a full kids menu.
How does The BLVD! Bakersfield compare to Dave & Buster's?
The BLVD! Bakersfield offers a wider range of activities than Dave & Buster's, including 22 bowling lanes (8 premium VIP), high-intensity laser tag, an indoor ropes course, karaoke, billiards, and bocce. Plus, The BLVD! Bakersfield features a full chef-driven gastro pub menu and craft cocktails.
Do you have a patio?
Yes! We have an outdoor patio with yard games and dining available seasonally.
Are bowling shoes included with the lane purchase?
Shoe rental is 5/person and is not included in the lane price. Reservations and walk-ins both require shoes (or socks at 4/pair).
Can I order food for pickup?
Yes! Order Food Online for convenient pickup or order ahead and dine in.

How do I book a party?
All parties book online at our online booking tool. Mon–Thu parties receive 10% off. Minimum 1-day advance booking.
What's included in a birthday party package?
All party packages include: Choice of food, Unlimited soft drinks, dedicated party host, plates, napkins, utensils, tablecloths, full set up and cleanup of the party, and digital invitations.
Can I bring a cake?
Yes! You bring the cake (or buy a full BLVD! Pie!).
What is the room capacity?
