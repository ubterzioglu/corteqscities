Fruitvale Norris Park
6221 Norris Road
Bakersfield, CA 93308
Fruitvale Norris Park is located in North Bakersfield.
The park is 17.6 acres and includes the following amenities:
Four baseball diamonds (two lighted)
Multi-purpose field
Two basketball courts
Two tennis courts
Two horseshoe pits
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Two group picnic shelters
Children’s play area
Open play area
Recreation building
Mature shade
Directions to the park:
From HWY 99, take the Olive Drive exit and head west on Olive drive. Take a right at Fruitvale Avenue. Fruitvale/Norris Park is located on the southeast corner of Fruitvale Avenue and Norris Road.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures

Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
