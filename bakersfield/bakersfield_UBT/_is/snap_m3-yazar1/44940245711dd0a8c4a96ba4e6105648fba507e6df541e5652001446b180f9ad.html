Frequently asked questions

Does admission include a pumpkin?
Yes. One pumpkin is included with each paid Pumpkin Patch admission. Children ages 1 and under enter free, but their admission does not include a pumpkin.
Are all activities included?
General Admission covers the regular included farm attractions. The Paintball Gallery and Apple Blaster require an additional purchase. Super Admission includes 100 paintball shots.
Can we visit in the morning?
Regular Pumpkin Patch admission begins at 2 PM Monday–Thursday and 1 PM Friday–Sunday. Wednesday–Friday morning hours are reserved for scheduled school tours.
Is there food at the Pumpkin Patch?
Yes. Seasonal snacks and refreshments are offered at the farm. The Cook Shack is open Friday–Sunday. Specific menu items can vary.
Are there senior or veteran discounts?
Guests aged 65 and older and veterans with identification are eligible for a 10% at-gate discount. Discount offers cannot be combined.
Where can I purchase tickets?
Visit our online Square store to explore available ticket options, or purchase admission at the farm gate.

Sunflower Maze

A Selfie Lover’s Dream

Stroll through a maze intricately designed with hundreds of sunflowers and more than 18 varieties of multicolored sunflowers like you’ve never seen! Perfect for social media enthusiasts, our frames offer endless selfie and photo opportunities. But it's not all about the pictures; it's a learning experience, introducing the youngin’s to the diverse world of flowers.

Corn Maize & The Alien Invasion

Guests will be able to learn about farming and find their way through our maze with the help of various alien-themed posters and a companion cellphone quiz game. The corn maze will still be appropriate for all ages, just slightly more spooky!

Musical Garden

Brought to you by

Murray Family Farms Musical Playground: Where Creativity Comes to Life

Step into a world where rhythm and melody blend seamlessly with the natural beauty of the farm at the Murray Family Farms Musical Playground. This hands-on attraction is a celebration of sound, offering guests the chance to play with outdoor instruments and create their own symphonies in a unique, farm-inspired setting. Whether you’re tapping out a beat on the xylophone, striking a note on chimes, or exploring other instruments, the Musical Playground is designed to unleash creativity and bring families together through the joy of music.

Best of all? The Musical Playground is included with your admission to the Bakersfield Pumpkin Patch, making it a must-visit attraction that adds a melodic twist to your day on the farm. Whether you're a budding musician or just looking for a unique, family-friendly activity, this is one experience you won't want to miss!

For the Thrill-Seekers

Apple Blaster: Aim for an Unforgettable Experience

Get ready for a high-speed thrill that combines precision and pure fun—the Apple Blaster at Murray Family Farms’ Bakersfield Pumpkin Patch! Step up, load your apple cannon, and fire away at distant targets. Whether you're aiming for a bullseye or just having fun with family and friends, the Apple Blaster is an experience that will get your adrenaline pumping. Feel the rush as you watch apples soar through the air and smash into your target!

This one-of-a-kind activity is the perfect blend of excitement and challenge. Ready to prove your marksmanship? Take a shot with the Apple Blaster and enjoy a farm experience like no other! (Just don’t forget—it’s not included with general admission, so plan ahead for this extra fun!)

Paintball Gallery: Fire Up the Fun

The Paintball Gallery at Murray Family Farms is where friendly competition and colorful fun collide. Channel your inner sharpshooter as you load up and splatter paint across targets with precision and flair. Whether you’re a seasoned paintball pro or trying it for the first time, this activity offers an exhilarating way to test your skills.

The Paintball Gallery is all about creating unforgettable moments filled with laughter, competition, and plenty of paint splatters. Perfect for kids and adults alike, this attraction is sure to be a highlight of your day at the Bakersfield Pumpkin Patch. (Heads up: like the Farm Cannon, this activity requires a small additional fee, so make sure to bring your game face and a little extra cash!)
