RiverLakes Ranch Community Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 8 acres and includes the following amenities:
RIverLakes Ranch is home to the District's Administrative operations
Community center
Meeting rooms
Double indoor gymnasium
One outside half court basketball court
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Group picnic shelters
Children’s play area
Open play area
Directions to the park:
Take Coffee Road north from Rosedale Highway. Turn west on Riverlakes Drive. The park site is located on the left side of Riverlakes Drive.
