Almondale Park
5501 Verdugo Lane
Bakersfield, CA 93312
Almondale Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 12.1 acres and includes the following amenities:
Group picnic shelter
Gazebo
Two softball diamonds (backstops only)
Family picnic pads
Children’s play area
Basketball court
Two volleyball courts
Multi-purpose field
Open play area
Restrooms
Horseshoe Pits
Directions to the park:
Take Noriega Road west from Calloway Drive to Verdugo Lane. Turn north on Verdugo Lane past Chippewa Avenue and the Almondale Elementary School campus. The park is immediately north of the school on the west side of Verdugo Lane.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures

Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
