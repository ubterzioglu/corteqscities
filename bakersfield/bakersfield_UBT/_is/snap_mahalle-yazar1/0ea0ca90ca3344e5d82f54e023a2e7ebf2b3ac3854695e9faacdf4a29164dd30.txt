North Highland Park
4201 Orrick Court
Bakersfield, CA 93308
North Highland Park is located in the Oildale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 10.5 acres and includes the following amenities:
One softball diamond
Multi-purpose field
Two basketball courts
Two volleyball courts
Restrooms
Two group picnic areas
Children’s play area
Open play area
Mature shade
Directions to the park:
North Highland Park is located at 4010 Rio Del Norte Street. Rio Del Norte Street is located off 7th Standard Road east of Airport Drive and west of McCray Street (park is located just north of the water tanks).
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures
Community Center & Business Office Hours:
Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
Reservations
Certain facilities supervised and operated by NOR are available on a reservation basis to groups and invididuals for social, cultural, or recreational activities.
