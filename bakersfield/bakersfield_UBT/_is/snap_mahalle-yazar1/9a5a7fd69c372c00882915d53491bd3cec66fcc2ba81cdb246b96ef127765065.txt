Greenacres Community Center & Park
2014 Calloway Drive
Bakersfield, CA 93312
661.392.2010
Greenacres Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 13.5 acres and includes the following amenities:
Community center with several activity rooms
Preschool
Gymnasium
Two lighted softball diamonds
Eight pickleball courts
Multi-purpose field
Horseshoe pit
Swimming pool
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Group picnic shelter
Children’s play area
Open play area
Mature shade
Directions to the park:
From Rosedale Highway turn south on Calloway Drive past Holland Street. Turn right on the Calloway Frontage Road to cross over Calloway Drive to the east side. The park is immediately south of the Fruitvale Junior High School campus.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures
Community Center & Business Office Hours:
Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
Reservations
Certain facilities supervised and operated by NOR are available on a reservation basis to groups and invididuals for social, cultural, or recreational activities.
