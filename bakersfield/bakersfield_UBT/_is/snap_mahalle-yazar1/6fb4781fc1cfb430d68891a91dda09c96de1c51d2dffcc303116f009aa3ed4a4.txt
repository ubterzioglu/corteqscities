Standard Park
301 East Minner Avenue
Bakersfield, CA 93308
Standard Park is located in the Oildale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 16.5 acres and includes the following amenities:
Two lighted softball diamonds
Multi-purpose field
Two basketball courts
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Group picnic shelter
Children’s play area
Open play area
Mature shade
Directions to the park:
Standard Park is located on East Minner Avenue. East Minner Avenue intersects with North Chester Avenue just south of Norris Road. The park is east of North Chester, just east of the Standard School campus.
