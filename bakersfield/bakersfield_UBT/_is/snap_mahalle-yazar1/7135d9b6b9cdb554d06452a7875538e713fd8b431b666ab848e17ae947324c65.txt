Mondavi Park
601 Mondavi Way
Bakersfield, CA 93312
Mondavi Park is located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 9.3 acres and includes the following amenities:
Open Play Area
Multi-purpose field with two backstops (no sports lighting)
One basketball court
Two turf volleyball courts
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
Group picnic shelter
Children’s play area
Mature shade
Directions to the park:
Take Brimhall Road, west from Coffee Road or east from Calloway Drive. Turn south on Mondavi. The park site is located at the south end of Mondavi Way immediately south of the Columbia Elementary School campus.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures

Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
