The result was a co-mingling of light and shade, wide open and tight areas woven into the fabric of the course.Also meandering through the 18-hole, 6800-yard track is the Friant-Kern canal which provides water to much of the Los Angeles area. While not a natural river, the canal combined with the many lakes at RiverLakes Ranch provide a refreshing sensation for the game of your life.Another great way to enjoy RiverLakes is to take advantage of the cooling misters that constantly keep the air moist and cool around the club house. When you're making the turn, spend some time in the cool mist and grab plenty of beverages for the inward nine.Yet another way to enjoy The Links is our night-lit driving range. Simply wait until the sun goes down and enjoy the cool desert air while working on your game!After your round, you're invited to enjoy a great atmosphere and stunning views of the 18th fairway and its signature, island green. Join us for cocktails, good company and great food and drink in the ever-changing shades and shadows that only the journey of the desert sun can create!The Links at RiverLakes is part of the master community, RiverLakes and has quickly become a favorite both inside the community and throughout Bakersfield as a whole. The knowledgeable and courteous staff in the full-service clubhouse will professionally handle everything from lessons to tournament planning to helping you choose what ball is right for you.With over 12 blue lakes, three lighted fountains and the distinctly beautiful desert landscape, RiverLakes Ranch is a surprising unique experience. The man-made contouring of the course as it meanders through almond trees and Canary Island Pines as well as stately redwoods manage to create the illusion that they've been there all along.Riverlakes Ranch is a golf experience definitely worth the price of admittance.
