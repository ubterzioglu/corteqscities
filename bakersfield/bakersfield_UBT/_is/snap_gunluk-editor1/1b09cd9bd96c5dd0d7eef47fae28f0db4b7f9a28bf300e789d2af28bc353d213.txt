Kern Literacy Council
About Us
Who We Are
​Kern Literacy Council was established in 1966 and is a 501(C)3 Nonprofit Organization. Our mission is to empower individuals to improve the quality of their lives through literacy education.
The organization has an average of 30 volunteers each month, teaching a variety of subjects and programs. The Kern Literacy Council serves adults and children throughout Kern County at the 22 Kern County Library branches, Community Resource Centers, Partner Sites and Online.
What We Do for Kern
​Kern residents are able to receive free tutoring from one of our trained tutors, at each of the 22 Kern County Library branches, community resource centers, various community partner sites and online.
KLC can help you learn to read, write, do math, pass your GED exam, prepare you for your citizenship test, and teach you reading strategies to use with your children!
