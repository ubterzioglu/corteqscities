Transfer Requests
If you would like your child to attend a different BCSD school than the one assigned to your home address, you must submit a transfer request. Transfers are approved based on available space at the requested school. If a transfer is not approved, your child will attend their neighborhood school. Parents are responsible for providing transportation to and from the approved school. Approved transfers remain valid through the highest grade offered at that school. If your child moves on to middle or junior high school, a new transfer application is required.
Please keep in mind, transfer approvals depend on available space. Transportation is the responsibility of the parent or guardian. New applications are required when a student advances to a school that is not part of their approved transfer (such as moving from elementary to junior high).
Which transfer do you need?
My child is already a BCSD student, but I want them to attend another BCSD school.
This is known as an Intradistrict Transfer. You child would be moving from one BCSD school to another.
My child is enrolled in another school district, but I want them to attend a BCSD school.
This is known as an Interdistrict Transfer. Your child would be entering the district for the first time.
Intradistrict Transfer Information
Application windows are based on the next or upcoming school year that your child would attend a BCSD school.
Grades 1-8: The application window is November 30 through December 11, 2026. Notifications will be mailed in March.
All grades, including TK & Kindergarten: The application window is March 15 through 26, 2027. Notifications will be mailed in June.
Late requests for all grades: The application window is August 2 through the first week of school. Notifications by September 14.

What type of transfer does my child need?
There are two types of transfers:
Intradistrict Transfer Requests transfer a student from one BCSD school to another BCSD School
Interdistrict Transfer Requests transfer a student into BCSD from another school district.
What reasons are accepted for transfers?
Child Care
What you'll need: Proof you work at least 30 hours per week (pay stub or employer letter), or proof you're a full-time college student (12+ units). Name, address, and phone number of your child care provider.
How it's approved: We will verify your employment and confirm that the listed provider cares for your child.
Unsolvable Issue
What you'll need: A signed letter explaining the situation and why a transfer is needed.
How it's approved: Each request is reviewed individually based on the circumstances you describe.
Graduate with Classmates
What you'll need: Proof your child is currently enrolled in the requested district.
How it's approved: Your child must already be in the highest grade level offered at their current school so they can stay with their classmates.
Finish the School Year
What you'll need: Proof your child is currently enrolled in the requested district.
How it's approved: Your child must have attended the school for at least half of the current school year.
Moving into the District
What you'll need: Proof that you're moving, such as a lease agreement, rental contract, or escrow papers.
How it's approved: We will verify that your family is relocating into the requested school district.
Special Program (GATE, Dual Immersion, etc.)
What you'll need: An acceptance letter showing your child has been admitted to the program.
How it's approved: We will verify the acceptance letter.
Residency Based on Employment
What you'll need: Proof that a parent or guardian works at least 10 hours per week within the requested school district.
How it's approved: We will verify your employment.
What documents will I need?
Depending on your reason for requesting a transfer, you may need:
Proof of employment
Daycare provider information
Proof of current enrollment
Rental agreement or escrow papers
Acceptance letter for GATE or Dual Immersion
A letter explaining special circumstances
The required documents vary based on the type of transfer requested.
My child has been accepted into a GATE program
Families requesting a transfer to a GATE school may apply beginning February 15, 2027. Families whose students are selected will receive notification no later than June 30.
My child moved, but I want them to finish the school year at their current school
You may request a transfer to allow your child to remain at their current school for the rest of the school year. Applications are accepted March 15 through the last day of school. Decisions are typically made within one week, along with notifications.
I want my child to attend Downtown Elementary
Apply February 1 through 12, 2027. Families whose students are selected through the lottery system will receive notification from Downtown Elementary in April.
