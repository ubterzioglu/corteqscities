Welcome to Family & Community Engagement
The Family and Community Engagement (FACE) team is dedicated to building strong, positive connections between parents, schools, and the District. Each school is supported by a FACE Liaison, your on-campus partner in strengthening the home-school relationship and fostering a sense of community among staff and families.
Our FACE Liaisons play an important role in supporting student success by offering parents and guardians a variety of workshops and programs, including Parent University, Parents as Leaders, Loving Solutions, and Parent Project. They also help families access essential resources and services, whether it’s addressing health concerns, finding community support, or securing basic necessities.
The work we do together supports our District’s Portrait of a Graduate vision, ensuring every student is equipped with the skills, confidence, and character they need to be successful in school, career, and life.
We’re here to walk alongside families every step of the way, empowering parents and creating opportunities for everyone to thrive. Connect with your school’s FACE Liaison today!
