Frequently Asked Questions
Below is a list of our frequently asked questions. If you have a question that is not listed below, please email our staff at [email protected] or call our office during normal business hours at +1 (661) 391-1800.
How long before my flight should I arrive at the airport to check in?
You should arrive at the airport 2 hours prior to your scheduled departure time if you need to get a boarding pass or check baggage. Airline staffing considerations may require that you reach the ticket counter to check your bags at least 45 minutes prior to the stated departure time.
Without checked bags, feel free to arrive 1 hour and 15 minutes (75 minutes) prior to your scheduled departure time and use the kiosk to check-in for your flight and get your boarding pass.
If you checked in at home or on your mobile phone and are also able to carry on your personal items, feel free to arrive 1 hour (60 minutes) prior to your scheduled departure time and proceed directly to the TSA security checkpoint.
Boarding generally starts 30 minutes prior to your flight time and you need to be present at the gate at least 15 minutes prior to the stated departure time.
Enjoy your flight and thank you for flying Meadows Field Airport!

Does Meadows Field offer currency exchange?
Unfortunately, we do not offer this service. However, currency exchange is offered in the airports of our nonstop destinations (Dallas/Fort Worth, Phoenix, Denver, and San Francisco).
Are pets permitted to fly at Meadows Field?
Yes, but each airline has different policies on flying with pets.
For more information about United Airlines’ pet policy, visit the Traveling with Pets section of the United Airlines website.
For more information about American Airlines’ pet policy, visit the Pets section of the American Airlines website.
What time does the airport open and close?
Meadows Field Airport is open daily from 3:00 AM to 1:30 AM.
What are the directions to the airport?
The Meadows Field Airport William M. Thomas Terminal is located at 3701 Wings Way off of Merle Haggard Dr.. If you are using computer or GPS generated directions, be sure to enter the physical address of 3701 Wings Way in Bakersfield to receive the correct directions.
If you are traveling to the airport from the North, proceed southbound on Hwy 99 and Exit 7th Standard Rd.; continue for 0.3 miles to the stop light at 7th Standard Rd. and turn left onto 7th Standard Rd.; 7th Standard becomes Merle Haggard Dr. east of Hwy 99. Continue east on Merle Haggard Dr. through the tunnel to the stop light at Wings Way. Turn right into the terminal entrance.
If you are traveling to the airport from the South, proceed northbound on Hwy 99. Exit to Porterville Hwy exit, #30. to the first stop light at Merle Haggard Dr. and turn right onto Merle Haggard Dr.. Proceed through the tunnel to the stop light at Wings Way, turn right into the terminal entrance.

REAL ID Information
Real ID Information
Beginning May 7, 2025, every air traveler 18 years of age and older will need a REAL ID-compliant driver’s license, state-issued enhanced driver’s license, or another acceptable form of ID to fly within the United States.
Check for the Star
REAL ID-compliant cards are marked with a star at the top of the card. If you’re not sure, contact your state driver’s license agency on how to obtain a REAL ID compliant card.
For info by state, including where to obtain a REAL ID, visit the DHS REAL ID website and click your state on the map.
