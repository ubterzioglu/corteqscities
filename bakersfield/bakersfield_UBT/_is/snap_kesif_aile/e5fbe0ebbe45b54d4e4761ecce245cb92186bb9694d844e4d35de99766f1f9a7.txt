High School Diploma Registration
HOW DO I START MY REGISTRATION?
Step one: If the last school you attended is outside the Kern High School District, you must request your official transcript and provide it when you meet with one of our counselors. Please contact the last school you attended to request your official transcripts, as each school has a different process. If you receive an official transcript in a sealed envelope, please do not open it. An open envelope cannot be accepted.
**PLEASE NOTE** If the last school you attended is in the Kern High School District, you do not need to bring a transcript. We have access to it.
Step two: Come in to test on one of the dates below. A score of 239 is required to qualify for high school diploma classes.
