The Big Red Barn

Farm activities are available on select weekends only.

The Big Red Barn is our flagship location and home to all seasonal events and pick-your-own fruit. Located east of Bakersfield along Highway 58 at the General Beale exit, it's an easy on/off the highway stop. Our beautiful Farm Market is sure to impress with its unique selection of fresh fruit, pies, fudge, and much more!

  • Store & Kitchen Hours :
    Monday - Thursday Open 8:00am - 9:00pm
    Friday - Sunday Open 8:00am - 10:00pm

  • Chevron Gas Pump Hours : Open for credit purchase 24 Hours

  • Farm Activities & Pick Your Own Fruit opportunities available on select dates. Pleasesee Event Calendar for details on upcoming events

*Please visit Facebook or Instagram for holiday or emergency closures.
