Facilities

Memorial Stadium 

   

Opened in 1955, Memorial Stadium is the crown jewel of the Bakersfield College Athletics Department; it was the first structure completed on the Panorama Site where the Main Campus resides. After opening, it served as cafeteria, health center, student commons, and anything else the 2,500-strong student body needed. 

Now it only serves as the home of Renegade Football and Renegade Track & Field.

The 20,000 seat stadium celebrated it's 70th Anniversary in 2025, and is the largest outdoor multi-use stadium between San Jose and Los Angeles.  

The field and track were replaced in the summer of 2019. The field has been a grass surface since 1955 and was replaced with field turf. The track was replaced with a brand new mondo track.

The stadium hosted the 2019 CCCAA State Football Championships and the 2019 CCCAA Southern California Finals Track Meet.

Renegade Athletic Center

Opened in 2025, Renegade Athletic Complex (RAC) is the new multipurpose Gym Facacility to Bakersfield College. The Gym features a two story building with a new wrestling room, an equipment center, 9 locker rooms, and a state of the art Sports Medicine Facality. The RAC is also home to the new Carl Bowser Performance Enhancement and Training Facility. 

 

 

 

 

 

Gil Bishop Sports Center

 

The Gil Bishop Sports Center is home to the main Athletics Offices, the Sports Medicine facility, the Athletics Academic Advisor, the Perfromance Enhancement Center, Fitness Center, Adaptive Physical Education Room and several indoor practice and competition facilities

Renegade Basketball, Volleyball, and wrestling all share the main gymnasium in the center of the building. 

Aquatic Center 

 

BC Swimming trains and competes in the Aquatic Center adjacent to the Gil Bishop Sports Center. The recently renovated training pool pictured above forms the a disconnected t-shape with the olympic-sized competition pool above it. 

The Dean & Adah Gay Sports Complex

The Dean & Adah Gay Sports Complex is home to Bakersfield College's Diamond sports. Gerry Collis Field and the George Culver Fieldhouse are located near the corner of University and Bernard Streets, while the Renegade Softball Field-home to 9 consecutive CCCAA Softball State Championships-sits right next door. 

The Bakersfield College Tennis Complex

The Bakersfield College Tennis Complex features eight hardcourts quite close to the geographical center of campus, and directly across from the Aquatic Center. BC Tennis has enjoyed a recent resurgence, and spectators enjoy sitting in the shade of mature pine trees that border the northwest side of the courts. 

Bakersfield College Soccer Field

The Bakersfield College Soccer Field is located directly west of the BC Library and is home to the Renegade Men's and Women's Soccer programs. 

Performance Enhancement Center

The state-of-the-art Performance Enhancement Center serves all of our Student Athletes and is staffed full-time by the Strength and Conditioning Director.
