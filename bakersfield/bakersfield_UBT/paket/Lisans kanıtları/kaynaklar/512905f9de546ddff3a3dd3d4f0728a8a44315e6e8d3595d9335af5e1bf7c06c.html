Is My Address in Unincorporated Kern?

In order to qualify for a position on the Measure K Citizens Oversight Committee, you must be a resident in an unincorporated area of Kern County. Use the address search to determine if your address is in unincorporated Kern.

Address Search

Please make sure to include the street number and that the street type is abbreviated.

Example of Correct Street Address: 10200 Brimhall Rd

Unable to find your address? Contact the County Administrative Office for information.

Additional Information

What does Unincorporated mean?
  • Every community in Kern County is either an incorporated City or an unincorporated Community.
  • An Incorporated city will have a government body with a mayor, a city council, a police department, etc.
  • In some cases, there are pockets of unincorporated addresses within city limits.
