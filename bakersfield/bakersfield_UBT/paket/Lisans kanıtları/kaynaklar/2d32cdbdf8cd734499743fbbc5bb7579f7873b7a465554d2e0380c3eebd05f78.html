Admission Requirements

International admission requirements vary significantly between undergraduate and graduate programs. Please review all document requirements carefully. Graduate programs may have additional criteria beyond the general university admission requirements.

For more information, refer to the application procedures or contact our office with any questions.

Undergraduate Application

Before beginning your application, make sure CSUB has the undergraduate program for which you are applying. View a list of all CSUB undergraduate programs.

How to Apply: Undergraduate/Bachelor's Degree

To begin your online application to CSU Bakersfield, please visit: www.calstate.edu/apply 
Then, follow the instructions below:

  1. Select the semester you wish to apply for.
  2. Click "Create an Account" to get started.
  3. Choose CSU Bakersfield as your campus and select your intended major.
  4. Complete all four sections of the application.
  5. Pay the $70 application fee online and click "Submit."
    See the sections below for specific information for First-Time Freshmen and Undergraduate Transfer applicants.

After completing the steps above, please prepare the following documents:

1. AFFIDAVIT OF FINANCIAL SUPPORT

All international students must complete the CSUB Affidavit of Financial Support Form.
  • If you are receiving a government scholarship, you must still complete the form and list your government as the sponsor. A signature from the government is not required.
  • If you are receiving an athletic scholarship, list CSUB Athletics as your sponsor. While a signature from from Athletics is not required, you must include your official scholarship award letter as supporting documentation. 

NOTE: Please visit Student Financial Services for full fee disclosure statement.The amounts listed are estimates and are subject to change without notice upon approval by the Board of Trustees, the Chancellor, or the Presidents, as appropriate. Actual expenses may vary depending on individual circumstances. The Form I-20 is intended as a general certification of financial eligibility and does not represent a final or exact bill.

2. INTERNATIONAL STUDENT INSURANCE AGREEMENT FORM

All students are required to complete the International Student Health Insurance Agreement Form, regardless of whether their insurance is covered by a government or CSUB athletic scholarship. If you have dependents accompanying you, they are also required to have valid health insurance coverage.

3. BANK STATEMENT AND/OR PROOF OF FINANCIAL SUPPORT

  • Bank statements must be in English and show a minimum of $38,956 in liquid funds available in the student’s or sponsor’s account. Statements must be dated within the past six months. Investment or non-liquid accounts will not be accepted.
  • Government-sponsored students must submit an official "Financial Guarantee for Admissions Purposes" letter.
  • Athletics-sponsored students must provide the scholarship award letter from CSUB Athletics. 


NOTE: Please visit Student Financial Services for full fee disclosure statement.The amounts listed are estimates and are subject to change without notice upon approval by the Board of Trustees, the Chancellor, or the Presidents, as appropriate. Actual expenses may vary depending on individual circumstances. The Form I-20 is intended as a general certification of financial eligibility and does not represent a final or exact bill.

4. OFFICIAL TRANSCRIPTS

  • First-time freshmen:
  • Request official high school transcripts to be sent to CSU Bakersfield. Official transcripts must be in English and indicate proof of graduation.
  • A minimum overall GPA of 2.5 is required for admission consideration. 
  • Undergraduate Transfers:
  • Request official transcripts from all previous colleges/universities attended. If you're transferring with fewer than 60 college units, you must also request official high school transcripts to be sent to CSU Bakersfield. Official transcripts must be in English and indicate proof of graduation.
  • A minimum overall GPA of 2.4 is required. 
  • Transcript Evaluation:
  • Transcripts from colleges or universities outside of the U.S. must be evaluated through a course-by-course analysis. This evaluation must be completed by a NACES approved organization, such as The Evaluation Company (formerly SpanTran) or WES. 
  • Sending Transcripts to CSUB
  • When requesting your transcripts and other official documents for your application, please use one of the options listed below. Transcripts received by California State University, Bakersfield Office of Admissions become property of the university and cannot be returned.

CSU Bakersfield will accept official transcripts via the following:

  1. Sent electronically via Parchment, Certree, National Student Clearing House, Military Services, or e-Transcripts California
  2. Emailed if your institution provides certified electronic transcripts through another service, transcripts can be sent directly from the institution to the Office of Admissions at incomingtranscripts@csub.edu.
  3. By mail to:
    CSU Bakersfield
    Office of Admissions, 47SA
    9001 Stockdale Highway
    Bakersfield, CA 93311-1022

Please send ONE official copy per institution and keep your order receipt as documentation. Transfer credit visible on a transcript from another institution will not be considered official. It can take up to three weeks to receive and process your transcript. Items will be marked as "Received" on your To Do List once CSUB Office of Admissions receives them.

Please note: Transcripts opened or sent from the student's personal email address will be considered unofficial.

5. ENGLISH PROFICIENCY REQUIREMENTS

All students must submit one of the following:
  • TOEFL: Score of 3.5 or 61 iBT required. Request scores to be sent to school code 4110.
  • IELTS: Score of 5.5 required (with a minimum of 5.5 in each section).
  • Duolingo English Test: Minimum score of 95.
  • SAT with a score of 550 or higher in the Evidence-Based Reading and Writing Section.
  • Pearson Test Examination (PTE): Minimum score of 43.
  • College level English course with a grade of C or higher:
    • CSUB will not accept online English courses to prove English Proficiency.
  • CSU Bakersfield may waive language proficiency requirements for students from these countries: Anguilla, Antigua & Barbuda, Ascension, Australia, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bermuda, Botswana, British Virgin Islands, Canada (except Quebec), Cayman Islands, Dominica, England, Eritrea, Fiji, Gambia, Ghana, Gibraltar, Grenada, Guyana, Ireland, Jamaica, Kenya, Kiribati, Lesotho, Liberia, Malawi, Mauritius, Namibia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Papua New Guinea, Saint Helena, Saint Kitts & Nevis, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent & The Grenadines, Scotland, Sierra Leone, Singapore, Solomon Islands, Swaziland, Tanzania, Tonga, Trinidad & Tobago, Tuvalu, Uganda, Wales, Zambia, and Zimbabwe.

A clear copy of the picture page of your unexpired passport.

Before you begin your application, it is important to make sure that CSUB has the graduate program you are applying for. Please click here for a list of all our graduate programs. For detailed information about the application process, visit our Graduate Admissions webpage.
