Group Camps

  • Greenhorn Mountain Park
  • Tehachapi Mountain Park

Group Picnic Areas

  • Buena Vista Aquatic Recreational Area
  • Kern River County Park
  • Lake Woollomes
  • Neighborhood Parks
  • Stramler Park

Reservation Form

Cancellation Process: To cancel reservations for Group Camps, Group Picnic Areas and County Buildings, call the Parks and Recreation Division at (661) 868-7000.

All events are subject to Fire Department and Law Enforcement review. If either agency requires a permit for the event, there may be a permit fee due, payable to that agency.
