FINDING THE CEMETERY

The English Cemetery in Malaga is at number 1 Avenida de Pries, across the road from the new hotel Miramar at the end of Paseo de Reding.

It is a 5 minute minute walk from the Pompidou museum, or 15 minutes from the city’s main street Calle Larios, so it is within easy reach for anyone spending a morning visiting the sights.

OPEN MAP

If you are coming by car, we recommend parking in the municipal ‘Aparcamiento Cervantes’ car park which also serves the bullring.

OPENING HOURS

The cemetery is open to the public:

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 09.00 to 14.00
Closed Monday (except public holidays)

Ticket sales cease 30 minutes before closing time.
