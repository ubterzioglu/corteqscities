RESIDENCY TALK & ACTIVATION

Friday 2 October from 19:00. The residents will discuss their experiences of the residency period at De Fabriek. 

Kexin Hao, Hussein Shikha and Tyson Goudsmits will take you through their inspiration, research and wider artistic practice. From cultural memory to digital archiving and organisational structures, the artists will provide insight into their projects.  

During the programme, Kexin Hao will perform a live activation of her work.  

Doors open at 18:30. The programme starts at 19:00.
