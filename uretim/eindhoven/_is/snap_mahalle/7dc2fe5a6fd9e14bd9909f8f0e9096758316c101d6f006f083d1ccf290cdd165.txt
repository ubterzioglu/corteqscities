Woensel-West
This former working-class area of Eindhoven has grown into a hip neighborhood where cultures, creativity, and eclectic architecture merge. Cultures living side by side, starting artists, young families, creative people, and children in the street. You will find it all here. Don't forget to stop by the Woensel WestSide Stores on Edisonstraat. A motley crew of entrepreneurs make this a vibrant neighborhood with a diverse range of shops.

KEVN: Kelderman & Van Noort
Woensel-West is bursting with creativity. Kelderman & Van Noort are one of the cornerstones of the creative community there, and they have been for over twelve years. They transformed the classrooms of an old school building into studios, and turned the gym into an exhibition space.
