Wilhelminaplein
The authentic pubs at the historical Wilhelminaplein all have a lively atmosphere. Here you'll find an original, historical area of Eindhoven.
15 min. walk
Spend the afternoon at the terrace or go to a pub where you can enjoy live performances during the weekends. Events often take place at the square itself.
