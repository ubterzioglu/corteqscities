18 Septemberplein
27 October 2026, 09:00 - 16:00
Or add to calendar
Every Tuesday, the 18 Septemberplein comes alive with a wide variety of market stalls. From fresh vegetables and fruits, to Turkish delicacies and local treats. Don't miss out on the stands with fabrics, flowers and cosmetics!
Done shopping? Treat yourself to some kibbeling (fried fish bites) or a worstenbroodje (sausage in a bun): Dutch snacks you just have to try!
