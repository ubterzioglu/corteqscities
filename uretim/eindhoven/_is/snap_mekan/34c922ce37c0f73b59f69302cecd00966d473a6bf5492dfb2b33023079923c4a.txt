Temporary Art Centre (TAC)
Temporary Art Center (TAC) is a breeding ground for cultural renewal. They believe it is important that promising culture makers from all disciplines are allowed to develop and show themselves. TAC can do this in all sorts of ways: in addition to eighty studios and workshops, they have 2000 m² of large and small halls that are suitable for exhibitions, concerts, performances, presentations, parties, etc. Besides all the space for culture makers to develop themselves, TAC also offers a broad stage for cultural experiments.
