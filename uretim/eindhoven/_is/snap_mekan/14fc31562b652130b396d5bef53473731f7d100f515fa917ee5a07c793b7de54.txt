Natlab
Since 2013, Natlab has been a film theater with a wide range of cultural activities. Visit Natlab for promising arthouse films, theater shows, dance, exhibitions, education, and more. In addition, Natlab has a café and outdoor seating area where you can enjoy refreshing drinks. Also, the rooms of Natlab are used for (business) events, from small meetings or brainstorming sessions to symposia and presentations for large groups. Have a look at Natlab's website for up-to-date information, such as the café's opening hours.
