De Oude Telefooncentrale
This former Philips building at Strijp-S used to be called SEY. The Eindhoven telephone exchange was located here until 1978 with the switchboard operators on the 1st floor. At The Old Telephone Exchange you can take some time for each other. And also the staff is at least happy to chat with you.
