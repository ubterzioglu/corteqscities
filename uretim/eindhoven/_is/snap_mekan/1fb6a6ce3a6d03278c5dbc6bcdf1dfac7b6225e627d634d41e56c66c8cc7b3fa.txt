Glow Golf Eindhoven
Glow Golf is a super fun activity for the whole family! The theme is "adventure". You play mini golf in an decor which is totally fluoresce, what makes it even more adventurous. From science fiction to spaceships to the Jurassic zone you wil encounter it all along the way. With the special 3D glasses, the experience is even greater. The floors, walls and scenery seem to have depth and the game also becomes more challenging.
