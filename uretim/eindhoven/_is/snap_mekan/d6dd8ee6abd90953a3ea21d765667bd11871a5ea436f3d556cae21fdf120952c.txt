Filmhuis De Zwarte Doos
The films are all carefully selected by the film commission, consisting mainly of students. Filmhuis De Zwarte Doos is a collaboration between Studium Generale and Natlab.
The restaurant is open every day from 08:30 to 22:00 for lunch, dinner, or tasty street food!
