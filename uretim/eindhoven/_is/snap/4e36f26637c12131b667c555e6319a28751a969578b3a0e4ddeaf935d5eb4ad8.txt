GLOW ROUTE
Map of GLOW 2026
The GLOW route takes visitors on a loop of around 5 km through the city centre, passing light artworks created by local and international artist, residents and your talent.
Soon, you will be able to access an interactive map of GLOW Eindhoven. On your phone, you can see exactly where you are, how to follow the route, and find all the information about the light artworks, food & drink, and other facilities. For now you can find the concept map in the image below.
The printed map is available during GLOW for €2 at an information container along the route (at Central Station and Stadhuisplein).
At Tourist Information (VVV) in De Heuvel, the map is also available in advance!
For the route of GLOW Helmond, Oirschot, Best, Veldhoven and Lieshout. Please look at www.glowinderegio.nl
