Kazerne is located on the corner of Grote Berg and Paradijslaan in the centre of Eindhoven, within walking distance of Eindhoven Central Station and only 15 minutes from Eindhoven Airport. Several bus stops and parking facilities are available nearby.

Entrance & Tickets

Exhibitions
Entrance to our exhibitions is usually free. Only for special events, such as during Dutch Design Week, we may ask for a small ticket fee. Details can always be found in the agenda.

Activities
Many activities are also free of charge, though sometimes we ask you to reserve a seat so we can plan for your visit. Details can be found in the agenda as well.

Park your Bike

Park your bike across Kazerne at the Grote Berg or the secure bicycle parking at the Heuvel, 400m distance, 5 minute walking distance.

Park your Bus

Bus and Coach ParkingGuests can be dropped off at the loading zone between Paradijslaan 2 and 8.

‍Vans can be parked at Frederik van Eedenplein. Touring coaches and trucks are welcome to park at the TU grounds (southern side road of Onze Lieve Vrouwestraat, between John F. Kennedylaan and Dommel) or in the Irisbuurt on Gabriël Metsulaan (between Lucas Gasselstraat and Kanaaldijk-Zuid).

Do you have a sleeper coach? Just ask us about the friendly arrangements we have in place.
