Genneper Parken
One of the attractions is the cozy Pavilion Genneper Parken, where you can play miniature golf in a beautifully landscaped garden or 'pick up' a terrace. There is a wide range of specialty beers, delicious wines or trendy drinks. The cuisine is also very well known in the area. Many regional dishes, honest products and everything fresh on the table every day. For children there is also an extensive choice including fries, pancakes or tasty pizzas from the oven.
