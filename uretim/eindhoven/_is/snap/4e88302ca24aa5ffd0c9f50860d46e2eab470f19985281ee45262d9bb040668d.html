Taxes

All about municipal taxes.

  • Paying municipal taxes by direct debit

    You can pay your tax demand by direct debit, by bank transfer or you can come to the Inwonersplein to pay it. If you are unable to pay, find out if you qualify for tax remission.

    Belastingaanslag betalen per automatische incasso

  • Waste collection charge

    All households pay a waste disposal levy. This covers the cost of the collection and processing of household waste.

    Afvalstoffenheffing

  • Dog tax

    If you live in the Netherlands and you have one or more dogs, you must pay dog tax to the municipality. You can register and deregister dogs online.

    Hondenbelasting

  • Property tax

    If, on 1 January, you are the owner and/or user of a property or a piece of land, you will have to pay property tax (OZB) for that year, for which you will receive a municipal tax bill.

    Onroerende zaak belasting (OZB)

  • Objection to property tax

    If you do not agree with the municipal tax demand or the WOZ value that has been set, you may lodge an objection with the Municipality.

    Bezwaar tegen OZB
