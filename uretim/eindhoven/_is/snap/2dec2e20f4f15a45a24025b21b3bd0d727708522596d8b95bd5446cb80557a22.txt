Single Permit: GVVA
Last update: 4 August 2026
If you want to work in paid employment in the Netherlands, you need a single permit (in Dutch: gecombineerde vergunning verblijf en arbeid or GVVA). The single permit combines the residence permit and the work permit. Find out what the requirements are and how to apply for a single permit.
