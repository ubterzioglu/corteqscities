Brouwerij Het Veem
Local breweries are popping up like mushrooms. People are living more consciously and are looking for honest, artisanal products, preferably local ones—products that clearly receive more attention. And that’s exactly what Jos van Stiphout offers in his brewery and tasting room.
From the twenty-four taps at Proeflokaal Het Veem flow the most unique beers with the most distinctive names: Oersoep Sergeant Pepper, Pampus Wisselshop Mosaic, Gajes Bruut. Finding it hard to choose? The beer experts at Het Veem are happy to join you for a tasting – and a drink. Don’t want to choose? Just order a tasting flight: three or five different beers at a great price. Cheers!
