Café Barolo
Café Barolo on Keizersgracht is a cozy wine bar offering more than 30 wines by the glass. Pair your wine with artisanal cheeses, paper-thin cured meats, or refined bites by chef Adrian Zarzo. Perfect for drinks.

Café Barolo
The café also features a cellar with 300 carefully selected gems. You can choose from a variety of tastings, ranging from four half-glasses with small bites to six half-glasses paired with a full meal.
The cold dishes on the menu at Café Barolo are not to be missed. Will you choose oysters, pistachio mortadella, fennel salami, or caviar? If you still have room afterward, options include artichoke flowers, oxtail croquettes, and Abuela's meatballs in tomato sauce. A fan of cheese platters? Then you'll feel right at home here.
