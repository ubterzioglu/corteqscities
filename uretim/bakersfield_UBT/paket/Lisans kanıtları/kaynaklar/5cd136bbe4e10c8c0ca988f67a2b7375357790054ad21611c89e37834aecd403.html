Bulky Waste & Electronic Waste Recycling Collections

Bulky Waste Collection Events have been hosted by Kern County Public Works for more than 20 years in the Metro Bakersfield area. As of 2020, Kern County Public Works has decided to expand its Bulky Waste/Large Item Collection Events to include unincorporated communities to reach residents living in the outlying County areas. During the events, residents can bring unwanted bulky household items such as appliances and furniture to a convenient designated location for recycling and disposal. California law states that Electronic Waste (E-Waste) must be recycled. Residents may also bring in their E-Waste to these collection events and any of the County-operated facilities. See information about E-Waste Collection options.

If you have residential hazardous waste, these locations and dates might be the opportunity you’ve been looking for to get it off your hands and safely out of your home.

Tire Amnesty Events

Kern County Public Works has partnered with CalRecycle to host tire drop-off events throughout the County. During these events, residents can drop off their tires free of charge at participating County disposal sites until capacity is reached.

Event Guidelines:

Remove rims from all tires before drop-off.

Per California State law, transport up to 9 tires per trip.

Only residential tires will be accepted free of charge.

No tires from waste tire generating businesses will be accepted.

All loads must be tarped or secured.
