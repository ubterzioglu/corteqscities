CSUB Venues

California State University, Bakersfield offers a wide range of spaces to fit any event, from intimate meeting rooms and classrooms to expansive outdoor lawns, athletic facilities and more. Browse the venues below to find the right fit for your next gathering, ceremony, or celebration.

Booking a Venue

Campus departments and organizations

Campus departments and organizations can submit event requests through 25Live using your CSUB credentials.
25Live

Community and external groups

Community and external groups can fill out our External Event Request Form and an assigned scheduler will follow up with you directly.

Hillman Aquatic Center

Home to an eight-lane, 50-meter competition pool with a separate six-lane training pool, one- and three-meter diving boards, and an electronic timing and scoreboard system. Bleachers and canopy seating make it a great fit for swim meets and aquatic events.

Square footage: n/a
Max capacity: 500
Scheduler: Eddie Brewer

Icardo Center

CSUB's largest indoor arena, seating up to 3,800 across 44,000 square feet. Bleachers can be raised for use as three full courts or lowered for larger seated events, making it ideal for commencements, concerts, and major athletic competitions.

Square footage: 44,000
Max capacity: 3,800
Scheduler Eddie Brewer

Icardo Lawn

An open lawn just outside the Icardo Center, well suited to outdoor gatherings, tailgates, or receptions before and after Icardo Center events.

Square footage: n/a
Max capacity: 250
Scheduler: Eddie Brewer

Harvey L. Hall Family Plaza

A welcoming plaza in front of the Icardo Center, featuring a two-tone brick walkway and an eight-foot bronze Roadrunner monument, with benches and shade trees—ideal for receptions and outdoor gatherings near the south lot.

Square footage: n/a
Max capacity: n/a
Scheduler: Eddie Brewer

Icardo Room

Square footage: 2,000
Max capacity: 100
Scheduler: Eddie Brewer

Soccer Complex

Dore Main Theatre

The Doré's proscenium mainstage seats 495 (including wheelchair-accessible spots) and features a 48-foot proscenium, a 24-line fly system, an orchestra pit, and computerized lighting and sound. It's the primary venue for Theatre and Music Department productions and is available for rental on select dates; the adjoining Doré Arena Theatre is a flexible black-box space used for classes and intimate performances, seating up to 85, and is generally not available for rental. 

Square footage: n/a
Max capacity: 495
Scheduler: Travis Von Schriltz (internal), Kristina Saldana (external)

The Rock

Open to all members during hours, with all climbing gear (shoes, harnesses, belay devices) provided free of charge. Available for group rentals: on-campus departments can submit a 25Live booking request; off-campus groups can email bdunn1@csub.edu.
