North Rosedale Park

Overview

Located in the Rosedale Area of Bakersfield. The park is 14.4 acres and includes four lighted softball diamonds, a multi-purpose field, restrooms, family picnic pads, group picnic shelter, play area, mature shade and basketball, tennis and volleyball courts.
