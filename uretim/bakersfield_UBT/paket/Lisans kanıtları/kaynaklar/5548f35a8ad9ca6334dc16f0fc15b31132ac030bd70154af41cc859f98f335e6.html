    A WELCOMING HAVEN FOR RELAXATION AND SOCIALIZING

    Tuesday thru Sunday 12pm - 1:30am • Dinner 4pm - 9pm (8pm Sundays)

    Happy Hour 3pm - 6pm Tuesday thru Friday

    Sandrini's was established in 2005 with the primary objective of offering a welcoming and comfortable atmosphere for people to gather and unwind. We recognize that in the midst of their busy lives, individuals require a place to disconnect, and between home and work, they require a sanctuary to relax. Our intention was to become that place. Additionally, we are committed to providing our patrons with exceptional drinks and food that have been thoughtfully prepared in a warm and friendly atmosphere. At Sandrini's, we firmly believe that our guests deserve nothing but the best.

    GATHER

    Sandrini's Public House is a cozy and inviting destination that's ideal for solo visits or group hangouts with friends. Our warm and inviting atmosphere provides a refuge from the stresses of daily life, while our friendly team, combined with a welcoming community of regulars, create an atmosphere that's perfect for catching up with old friends or making new ones.

    EAT

    At Sandrini's Public House, we believe that our guests deserve exceptional food. That's why we make every dish to order, and we never serve food from a microwave. We take great pride in our kitchen and make our own sauces, dressings, and roast meat to ensure the highest quality and freshness.

    DRINK

    Sandrini's Public House takes great care in crafting our drinks using the highest-quality ingredients, including fresh-squeezed juices and house-made syrups. In addition to our signature hand-crafted cocktails, we also offer barrel-aged cocktails, as well as superb selection of whiskeys and craft beers to cater to any taste preference. Whether you're looking for a classic drink or something more adventurous, we have something that will surely satisfy your every desire.
