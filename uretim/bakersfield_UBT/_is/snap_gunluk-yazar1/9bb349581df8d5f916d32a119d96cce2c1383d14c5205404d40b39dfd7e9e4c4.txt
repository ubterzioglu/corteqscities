Should I take an online class?
Your answers to the following questions will help you to decide:
Am I a self-starter and able to motivate myself to work?
Can I budget my time and set schedules to complete work on time?
Do I have basic computer skills: word processing, internet browsing, and e-mail (including sending and receiving attachments)?
Can I communicate adequately in writing? Can I understand and follow the instructions given in writing?
Am I comfortable participating in a class where I am not physically present and may not know the instructor or the other students?
If you answered "yes" to most of these questions, chances are you are a good candidate for success in an online course. A few "no" answers to these questions may identify areas that need strengthening or addressing, but you may still decide that online learning is a good choice for you.
If you answered "no" to many of these questions, this may indicate that your chances for success may be greater in a traditional course setting at this time.
How do online courses work?
Online courses are structured so that all course content traditionally delivered in the classroom is delivered online. Course materials may be accessed using a Learning Management System (LMS) such as Canvas, the LMS used by Bakersfield Adult School. Some classes may include online teacher-led interactive sessions. The online course syllabus will show the schedule and requirements of the course.
I don’t have a computer or WiFi access. Can I enroll?
All students will need reliable internet access along with their devices. Bakersfield Adult School will not provide each student with phones, mobile devices, tablets, Chromebooks, or other hardware.
AJCC Supportive Services may be able to provide you with a computer and other resources if you qualify. Please see this AJCC flyer for more information.
How do I access my classes?
You can access Canvas at https://kernhigh.instructure.com/login/ldap or through the Canvas Student phone app.
For tutorials and help using Canvas, please visit https://sites.google.com/kernhigh.org/khsddigitalresources/canvas/canvas-for-parents-and-students?authuser=0

How can I get my diploma?
Diplomas are available for pickup at the Administration Office two weeks after the end of the current quarter. Students must present a valid photo ID at the time of pickup. Please note that pickup availability may vary during holidays and school closures.

GED Records Requests
Bakersfield Adult School no longer collects nor stores GED results for exams taken at any of our facilities.
To obtain GED results/transcripts/certificates for exams taken at Bakersfield Adult School or Lerdo Correctional Facility, please request them on the website www.ged.com. If you do not have access to a computer, call 1-877-392-6433 and ask them to mail an order form to you.
If you took your exams on a computer, you can receive your GED records through the GED account you would have created at the time you took the exams.
There is a charge for receiving your document. If you request an electronic copy and provide an email address, you can download, save, and print five copies of each document ordered.
If you encounter problems and need assistance obtaining GED records, call GED at 1-877-392-6433 and explain the issues you are experiencing. For further assistance, contact BAS at 661-835-1855 and ask to speak with the Registrar.
