Ways to pay my PG&E bill
Pay your bill now
PG&E bill payment options
Show all
Hide all
Pay online
Pay online as a registered user or use guest bill pay access with no registration required.
Pay as a registered user
Registered users have the following payment options:
Pay from a bank account. Free of transaction fees.
Pay with a credit or debit card. A transaction fee applies.*
Sign in as a registered user.
Pay with no registration required
Don't have an online account set up? Don't want to sign in to pay your bill? No problem. Use guest bill pay. No username or password required.
A transaction fee applies whether you use a bank account or a credit or debit card.*
Learn more about guest bill pay.
Pay as guest
Pay by phone
To pay your energy statement by phone, call 1-877-704-8470. Have your 11-digit account number ready.
A transaction fee applies when you use a bank account or a credit or debit card.*
Pay by mail or in person
Pay by mail
Make your check payable to PG&E
Send it, along with your energy statement remittance stub, to the following address:
PG&E
P.O. Box 997300
Sacramento, CA 95899-7300
Pay in person
Pay your energy statement in person at one of our many authorized neighborhood payment centers.
Find a neighborhood payment center near you.
Manage account access
Give family members or trusted users access to your account.
Learn how to give others access
Pay with Google Pay or Apple Pay
Digital wallet options (Google Pay, Apple Pay) are now available for immediate payments (i.e., no future dated payments).
Apple Pay will only be supported on devices running iOS 17 or later.
Set up autopay
Never worry about missing a payment again. Set up autopay with a valid credit card, debit card or bank account. Cancel anytime. Autopayments let you:
Choose when you want your bill to be paid
Set a maximum payment amount
Payment options:
Visa, MasterCard, Discover or American Express credit or debit card. Card payments require a transaction fee.*
Bank account. Payments from a checking or savings account do not require any transaction fees.
IMPORTANT! If your payment can't be processed, your recurring payments will be cancelled. First time cancellations may re-enroll after 30 days.
To understand why your payment couldn't be processed, please contact your financial institution.
To find another way to pay, refer to this page or call:
Residential customers: 1-877-660-6789
Business customers: 1-800-468-4743
Agricultural customers: 1-877-311-3276
Learn more about autopay
Set up a payment plan or due-date extension
A payment plan, also known as a payment arrangement, allows you to break up your existing balance into smaller monthly payments.
A due-date extension, also called a payment extension, moves the due date up to 30 days into the future. This allows extra time to pay the balance in full.
Learn more payment plans and due-date extensions.
Effective June 9, 2025: Bill payment transaction fees have changed.
Avoid fees by signing up for autopay with a checking/savings bank account or by logging in and using the one-time payment option with a checking/savings bank account.
If you pay by phone or use any payment method with guest bill pay, the revised transaction fees below will apply:
Why do I have to pay a transaction fee?
PG&E has partnered with an independent payment provider.
This provider allows PG&E customers to pay with a credit or debit card on a guest payment or recurring basis.
The fees for these payments are set by credit card networks, payment processors and banks—not by PG&E.
California Assembly Bill 746 restricts PG&E from passing these costs to all customers.
Only those using this payment service are charged these fees.
The last fee change occurred in 2017.
Don't want to pay a transaction fee?
You have these fee-free options
Sign into your account and make a one-time payment with your checking or savings account.
Sign up for autopay using your checking or savings account.
Pay by mail. Make your check payable to PG&E. Send it, along with your energy statement remittance stub, to the following address:
PG&E
P.O. Box 997300
Sacramento, CA 95899-730

Gift payment
Help others with their bill
Want to help pay the energy bill of a friend, neighbor, college student or local business? Make a payment of any amount.
It's secure and confidential
Your payment will:
Be applied to their account.
Reduce any balance reflected on their next month's bill
Note: For security reasons, we cannot share any of your recipient's account or balance information.
Send your gift payment in three easy steps:
Download and print the Energy Giving form (PDF).
Return your completed form and payment.
Once processed, the recipient will receive an email or letter confirming their gift.
Pay gift by mail
Mail the Energy Giving form and payment to:
PG&E
Attention: Energy Giving Payment
P.O. Box 997300
Sacramento, CA 95899-7300
Do not mail cash. Make check payable to PG&E and indicate "Energy Giving Payment" in the memo line.
