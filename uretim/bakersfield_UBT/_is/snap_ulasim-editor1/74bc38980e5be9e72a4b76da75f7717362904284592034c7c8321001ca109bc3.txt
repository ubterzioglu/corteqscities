Gold Runner Service Adjustments
Effective October 25, 2026
Due to planned infrastructure improvements, Amtrak Gold Runner Service will operate with the following modified service:
Southbound Service
Trains 702 and 704, which normally operate between Sacramento (SAC) and Bakersfield (BFD), will terminate in Fresno (FNO). Buses 4302 and 4304 will provide alternate transportation between Fresno (FNO) and Bakersfield (BFD) with an intermediate stop at Hanford (HNF).
Trains 710, 712, 714 and 716, which normally operate between Oakland (OKJ) and Bakersfield (BFD), will terminate in Fresno (FNO). Buses 4310, 4312, 4314 and 4316 will provide alternate transportation between Fresno (FNO) and Bakersfield (BFD) with an intermediate stop at Hanford (HNF).
Northbound Service
Train 703, which normally operates between Bakersfield (BFD) and Sacramento (SAC), will originate in Fresno (FNO). Bus 4303 will provide alternate transportation between Bakersfield (BFD) and Fresno (FNO) with an intermediate stop at Hanford (HNF).
Trains 713, 715, 717 and 719, which normally operate between Bakersfield (BFD) and Oakland (OKJ), will originate in Fresno (FNO). Buses 4313, 4315, 4317 and 4319 will provide alternate transportation between Bakersfield (BFD) and Fresno (FNO) with an intermediate stop at Hanford (HNF).
No alternate service will be provided at Corcoran (COC) or Wasco (WAC).
