801 N Vignes Street
Los Angeles, CA 90012
United States

Frequently asked questions

How long does the bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles take with Greyhound?
The fastest Greyhound bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles takes around 2 hours and covers approximately 114 miles. Actual travel time may vary depending on traffic and the route.
How much does a Greyhound bus ticket from Bakersfield to Los Angeles cost?
Greyhound bus tickets from Bakersfield to Los Angeles start from $24.48. For the best fares, book early or choose midweek departures.
How do I track my Greyhound bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles ?
To track your Greyhound bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles, use our bus tracker. It provides real-time updates on your bus’s location and expected arrival times.
How much baggage can I bring on the bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
On the Greyhound bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles, you can bring one carry-on bag (16x12x7 in, up to 25 lbs) and one checked bag at no extra cost. With a Flexible fare, a second checked bag is also free. Need more? Add extra luggage when you book. For full details, check our baggage policy.
Is traveling with Greyhound from Bakersfield to Los Angeles environmentally-friendly?
Choosing Greyhound for your journey from Bakersfield to Los Angeles is an eco-conscious option, helping to reduce emissions and traffic compared to traveling by car.
What onboard services are available on Greyhound buses from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
Greyhound buses from Bakersfield to Los Angeles offer free Wi-Fi, power outlets at each seat, reclining seats with extra legroom, overhead storage, and onboard restrooms.
What time is the first bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
The first bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles departs at 04:10 am.
What time is the last bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
The last bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles departs at 08:50 pm.
Can I book bus tickets both for Greyhound and FlixBus to travel from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
Yes, you can book tickets for both Greyhound and FlixBus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles on our website. After entering your travel details, you can compare schedules and fares for both options.
How many daily Greyhound bus trips are there from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
Greyhound operates 6 daily departures from Bakersfield to Los Angeles. Check available times for your dates on the website or app.
What facilities does Greyhound provide for passengers with reduced mobility and service animals?
Greyhound buses are equipped with wheelchair lifts and spaces for passengers using mobility devices. We recommend booking your trip from Bakersfield to Los Angeles in advance. Service animals are welcome on all Greyhound buses. For more information, please visit our accessibility page.
Where are the Greyhound bus stops in Bakersfield and in Los Angeles?
You can find the bus stops for Greyhound in both Bakersfield and Los Angeles by viewing the map on this page, which includes addresses and locations of all stops.
Do I need to print my Greyhound bus ticket from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
No need to print your ticket. Just show the QR code from your confirmation email or the Greyhound app to the driver when you board your bus from Bakersfield to Los Angeles.
How can I pay for a Greyhound bus ticket from Bakersfield to Los Angeles?
You can pay for your Greyhound bus ticket from Bakersfield to Los Angeles by card, digital wallet, and other methods. All available payment options are shown at checkout. For more details, check our payments and tickets page.
