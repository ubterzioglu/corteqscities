Box Office & Will Call
The Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center Box Office is located at 1001 Truxtun Avenue, at the northeast corner of Dignity Health Arena between the Arena and Theater, directly across from the Bakersfield Condors main office.
IMPORTANT NOTICE!
AXS.com is the only official online site for tickets to events at Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center. Tickets for our events listed on other websites may be counterfeit and/or sold at higher than face value.
CURRENT BOX OFFICE HOURS:
Monday – Friday
Event Days: 3 hours prior to start of show
Non-Event Days: 12 PM – 4 PM
Saturday and Sunday
Event Days: 3 hours prior to start of show
Non-Event Days: Closed
Box Office hours may vary on holidays.
BOX OFFICE PURCHASES
Tickets may be purchased directly at the Box Office. Dignity Health Arena accepts the following methods of payment: Cash, American Express, Mastercard, Visa and Discover. There is a $4 walk-up box-office fee per ticket, additional fees may apply. When purchasing tickets with a credit card, you must show a valid photo identification to complete the transaction.
ONLINE PURCHASES
Tickets may also be purchased online via AXS.com.
WILL CALL PICK UP
Purchaser Pick Up - Tickets may be picked up at the Will Call window of the Box Office two hours prior to the event start time. You must have the credit card used to make the purchase and a valid photo identification to pick up tickets at Will Call.
ACCESSIBLE/DISABLED TICKETS
Accessible/Disabled seating is available on both levels of the Arena, as well as in the Theater. Accessible seating is available through AXS.com and through the Dignity Health Arena Box Office. When ordering accessible tickets, it is important to indicate that you require accessible seating so we can properly seat you.
PURCHASE ACCESSIBLE TICKETS
Visit AXS.com, find your event, click the "TICKET" link, then click the "Accessible Tickets."
You may also visit the Dignity Health Arena Box Office and request accessible tickets.
CHILDREN
In general, most events allow children under 2 to enter without a ticket, provided they do not need an actual seat. However, some events require all attendees, regardless of age, to present a ticket for entry. Please check the event ticket policies at the time of purchase.
MOBILE TICKETING
Dignity Health Arena only accepts and scans tickets off a mobile device if they are within the AXS mobile application. For more information on the AXS app, click here.
PARKING
Timed (15 minute) parking for the box office is located along Truxtun Avenue in front of the Arena. Additional parking may be found in the lots directly behind the Arena.
