IMPORTANT INFORMATION
GUESTS UNDER 21
Unaccompanied guests under the age of 21 should contact the hotel prior to their arrival to make arrangements for their stay.
VALET
Valet Parking is available for an additional fee of $18 per night. Valet parking is validated for 2 hours when dining at Padre Hotel.
SMOKING
Padre Hotel is a non-smoking facility.
CHECK-IN/OUT
Check-in time is guaranteed at 4:00pm. Check-out time is 11:00am.
CREDIT CARD GUARANTEE
An incidental hold will be placed on your credit card at check-in. If unused, the hold will be released upon check-out. We recommend you use a credit card for payment as it may take up to 7 days for your bank to release the hold on a debit card.
PETS
The Padre Hotel is a pet friendly hotel. We charge a $50 non-refundable pet cleaning fee per pet. Occasionally the Hotel will need to contact pet owners if the pet is left unattended and causing a disturbance. Please leave your cell phone contact information with the front desk for this purpose. If the pet causes any damage to the room or furniture, you will incur further fees.
