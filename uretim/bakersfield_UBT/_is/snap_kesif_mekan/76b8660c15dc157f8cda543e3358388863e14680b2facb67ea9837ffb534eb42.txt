Overview
Completed in 1989 the 44,000 sq. ft. venue is home to women's volleyball and basketball as well as men"s wrestling. The center seats 3,800 and has multiple locker rooms and shower facilities. Additional amenities include a concession area in the lobby, a state-of-the-art sound system, center court scoreboard and digitized message boards situated on diagonally opposite ends of center court
