Kern County Soccer Park
Share
Overview
Nestled in the foothills of northeast Bakersfield, the complex is the largest facility with lighted fields in the San Joaquin Valley and one of the largest soccer facilities in California. The venue has 25 fields, including 21 regulation size fields (four lighted), four modified fields, and parking for over 1,500 vehicles. Additionally the venue has four restroom/concession facilities conveniently placed throughout the complex. Separate tournament headquarter and referee buildings are located near the center of the complex. The venue has been home to numerous successful large tournaments, as well as local club league matches. Telephone, internet, and overnight parking services are available on-site.
