Californian newspaper moves to Cal Twin Towers in SW Bakersfield
Robert Price
Tue, March 11, 2025 at 12:26 AM UTC
1 min read
Add Yahoo as a preferred source to see more of our stories on Google.
BAKERSFIELD, Calif. (KGET) — The Bakersfield Californian newspaper has returned to Bakersfield.
After occupying offices near the Meadows Field airport north of Oildale for six years, the newspaper has moved back into the heart of Bakersfield.
The new location, on the first floor of the Cal Twin Towers, is at 4900 California Avenue, Suite 100-A.

The newspaper, which traces its origin to 1866, has been published as The Californian since 1907. It moved out of its iconic downtown home at 17th and Eye streets in 2018, and the Harrell-Fritts family sold the business the following year.
No date has been set, but a ribbon cutting is planned for the paper's new headquarters, a couple of blocks east of the Stockdale Tower.
