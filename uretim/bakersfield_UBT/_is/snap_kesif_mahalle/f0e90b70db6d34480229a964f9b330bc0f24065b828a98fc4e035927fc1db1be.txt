Casa Loma Elementary
At Casa Loma Elementary our goal is to build strong readers and students who can think creatively, critically, and communicate effectively to prepare them for their futures and beyond. Casa Loma first opened in 1949. The school has gone through several changes since then, but the goal has always been to provide the best educational opportunities for each student that passes through those doors. Casa Loma is focused on academic achievement, social-emotional learning, and family and community engagement.
