Riverview Community Center & Park
401 Willow Drive
Bakersfield, CA 93308
661.392.2020
Riverview Park is located in the Oildale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 20.9 acres and includes the following amenities:
Community center with activity rooms
Gymnasium
Lighted baseball diamond
Multi-purpose field
Disc Golf Course
One basketball court
One volleyball court
Horseshoe pits
Restrooms
Family picnic pads
2 Group picnic shelters
Children’s play area
Open play area
Mature shade
Water Play Area
Neighborhood Place Community Learning Center
Directions to the park:
From North Chester, turn west on Roberts Lane to Oildale Drive. Turn south on Oildale Drive until the street dead ends at Willow Drive. Turn east on Willow Drive which dead ends at Riverview Park.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures
Community Center & Business Office Hours:
Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
Reservations
Certain facilities supervised and operated by NOR are available on a reservation basis to groups and invididuals for social, cultural, or recreational activities.
