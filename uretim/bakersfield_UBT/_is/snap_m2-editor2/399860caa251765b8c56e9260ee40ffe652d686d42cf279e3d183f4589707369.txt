Studio 17: BarrelHouse Brewing Taps Into Bakersfield
Kait Hill and Presented by: BarrelHouse Brewing Co.
9 months ago
Presented by: BarrelHouse Brewing Co.
Bakersfield has a new spot to gather, unwind, and try something fresh. BarrelHouse Brewing has officially opened its doors in town, bringing its Valley-rooted craft beer, laid-back atmosphere, and strong sense of community to the local scene. Known for bold flavors and creative brews, BarrelHouse is all about creating a space where people can slow down, connect, and enjoy something made with intention.
The team says Bakersfield felt like a natural fit, sharing the same Central Valley pride that’s always been at the heart of the brand. To celebrate their arrival, BarrelHouse is hosting a special event on Saturday, January 24th, from 12pm to midnight. They’re inviting the community to stop by, check out the space, and experience what they’re all about. Whether you’re a craft beer fan or just looking for a new local hangout, BarrelHouse Brewing is ready to become part of Bakersfield’s social scene.
