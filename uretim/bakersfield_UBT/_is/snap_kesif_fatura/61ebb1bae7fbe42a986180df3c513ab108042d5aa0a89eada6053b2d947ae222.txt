Within the unincorporated areas of the County, from Lost Hills to Boron, local waste haulers are contracted under a franchise system to provide curbside trash collection services to residents. Incorporated cities either provide their own service or negotiate hauling contracts for trash collection services to their residents.
There are remote areas of the County whereby collection service is provided through bin sites and transfer stations. Residents living in rural, unincorporated county areas may contact Kern County Public Works to find out more information regarding available disposal options.

Bulky Waste Collection
Residents with trash collection service may also have curbside collection of large household items available to them. This “Call-to-schedule” service provides an easy opportunity for residents to get rid of bulky household items such as mattresses, couches, appliances, and other similar items. These items are scheduled for pick up and are recycled or disposed of. For information on Curbside Bulky Waste Item Collection, call your local trash collection service provider. If you’re not sure who your provider is, see the list below or call us at 661-862-8900 for assistance.
Common large items collected include:
Furniture: sofas, love seats, chairs, tables, desks, shelving units, mattresses, bed frames, box springs and dressers
Appliances: washers, dryers, stoves, refrigerators, water heaters, microwave ovens and range tops
Outdoor Toys/Patio: lawn chairs, patio tables, patio chairs, large toys, grills and kiddie pools
No Household Hazardous Waste: paint, varnish, motor oil, automotive fluids, car batteries, insecticide, pesticide, pool/spa chemicals, bleach, ammonia, household or shop cleaners
No Tires
No construction, demolition or remodeling materials
For the Bulky Waste Collection Events Schedule, see the Waste & Recycling Events page.
