The Empty Space Gallery proudly presents
The Art of Nicole Lowrie and Kaylee Mayo
Nicole Lowrie is a Bakersfield artist specializing in fiber art. She found love in the art of crafting at the age of 9, when her mother placed her in a sewing class. Since then, Nicole has gone on to become the first in her family to go to a four-year university. She gained her Bachelors degree in Art at California State University, Bakersfield, is an instructor at Art with Heart, and assists future artists of tomorrow at Highland High School.
Kaylee Mayo is the supporting artist in our gallery. She is an up and coming artist in Bakersfield, specializing in different types of traditional art. Kaylee also has a Bachelor’s degree in Art from California State University, Bakersfield, graduating in 2023. She participates annually in Via Arte at The Marketplace and teaches at Highland High School.
The Art of Nicole Lowrie & Kaylee Mayo features acrylic works of art, fiber art, and more!
The Art of Nicole Lowrie & Kaylee Mayo will be on display in The Empty Space Gallery from October 23 - November 7, 2026.
Join us for a closing reception celebrating the artists and their works on November 7, from 4:30pm to 6:30pm. During this time, you can enjoy complementary refreshments, meet the artist, and purchase any of the pieces in the collection.
