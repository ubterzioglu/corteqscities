Parking
Parking for Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center is located off of N Street between Truxtun Avenue and California Avenue. Parking rates vary by event - advanced parking can generally be purchased via AXS.com.
During non-event hours, limited-term box office parking is available on Truxtun Avenue in front of the Arena.
From Southern California
Take Highway 99 North to Bakersfield
Exit California Avenue and turn right to head east on California
In approximately 1.5 miles, turn left on to N Street
From Northern California
Take Highway 99 South to Bakersfield
Exit California Avenue and turn left to head east on California
In approximately 1.5 miles, turn left on to N Street
From Tehachapi, Lancaster/Palmdale, Las Vegas
Take Highway 58 West to Bakersfield
Exit Chester Avenue and turn right to head north on Chester
In approximately 1.5 miles, turn right on to California
In approximately 1000 feet, turn left on to N Street
From Lake Isabella and Ridgecrest
Take Highway 178 West to Bakersfield
Turn left on L Street
In approximately 0.5 miles, turn left on Truxtun Avenue
From the Central Coast
Take Highway 46 to Highway 99 South
Exit California Avenue and turn left to head east on California
In approximately 1.5 miles, turn left on to N Street
