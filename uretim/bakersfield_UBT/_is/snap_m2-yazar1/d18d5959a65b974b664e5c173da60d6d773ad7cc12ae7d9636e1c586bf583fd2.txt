MISSION STATEMENT
Our Mission as the Founders and Advisory Board of ‘The Bakersfield Music Hall of Fame’ is to create a self sustaining and on going organization with a performing Arts Theatre and two Professional Recording Studios. It is our goal to provide a fun, exciting and comfortable environment for our members that will enrich and encourage appreciation for live performance of music and theatre. We hope to inspire, promote and help grow most genres of musical expression within our facility.
We will strive to be an entry point into the music industry for the new generation of artists within our community. We will seek to acknowledge, honor and promote the past, present and future professional achievements of our rich local music community in all genres of music, while employing courtesy, dignity and respect in every endeavor.
