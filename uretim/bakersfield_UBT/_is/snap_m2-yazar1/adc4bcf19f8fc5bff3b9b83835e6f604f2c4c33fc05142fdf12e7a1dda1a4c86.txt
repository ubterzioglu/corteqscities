Age Restrictions
Unless otherwise noted on the event page, all events at Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center are all ages.
Generally, children under the age of 2 do not need a ticket as long as they can sit on a caregiver's lap. This policy may change from show to show -- consult the show page for details.
Animal Policy
Service animals are welcome at Dignity Health Arena, Theater & Convention Center. All other animals are strictly prohibited.
Bag Policy
Unless otherwise specified, Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center allows small bags no larger than 12"x12"x6" inside the building. Bags do not need to be clear.
Camera Policy
As a general rule instamatic type cameras are allowed at most events. Professional cameras or cameras with long telephoto lenses and video cameras are strictly prohibited. If there is a prohibition on cameras at a particular event the policy will be posted at all entrances.

Code Of Conduct
To ensure a safe, respectful, and enjoyable experience for everyone, all guests, staff, and participants are expected to adhere to the following Code of Conduct:
1. Respectful Behavior
Treat all individuals with courtesy and respect regardless of race, gender, religion, ability, sexual orientation, or background.
Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language or behavior will not be tolerated.
2. Prohibited Conduct
No fighting, physical aggression, or threatening behavior.
No throwing of objects or projectiles.
No vandalism, defacing, or damaging Building property.
No unauthorized access to restricted areas (e.g., locker rooms, playing surfaces, backstage).
No possession or use of illegal substances or weapons.
3. Alcohol and Substance Use
Alcohol must be consumed responsibly.
Intoxicated guests may be refused entry or ejected without refund.
Underage drinking and use of illegal substances are strictly prohibited.
4. Smoking and Vaping
Smoking and vaping are only permitted in designated areas
5. Ticketing and Entry
Only valid tickets will be accepted for entry.
Re-entry policies must be followed as posted.
Unauthorized resale or duplication of tickets is prohibited.
6. Fan Conduct
No offensive signs, chants, or clothing.
Follow instructions from Building staff and security at all times.
Stay seated during play or performance unless otherwise permitted.
Respect others' enjoyment of the event—no excessive noise, obstruction, or unruly behavior.
7. Safety and Security
All guests are subject to search upon entry.
Report suspicious behavior to Building staff immediately.
Emergency procedures (e.g., evacuation) must be followed promptly.
8. Photography and Recording
Flash photography, professional equipment, or video recording may be restricted depending on the event.
Enforcement
Violations of this Code may result in ejection, revocation of tickets or passes, banning from future events, and/or legal action.
Building management reserves the right to amend this Code at any time.

Metal Detectors
Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center utilizes metal detectors at all venue entrances. Upon entry, all guests will be required to have their bags checked and pass through a metal detector. In order to increase efficiency of these machines, guests are asked to keep the following guidelines in mind when attending an event:
Arrive early in preparation for additional security measures
Limit the number of personal items brought into the venue in order to expedite the screening process
Once in the screening area:
Make sure you have your ticket in hand
Prepare for a bag check by event security
Place your cell phone, camera and purse on the screening table and prepare to walk-through the metal detector
Guests will be subject to a secondary screening process if the alarm sounds when passing through the walk-through metal detector.
An alternative security screening is available to guests who are: expectant mothers, wheelchair bound or have a medical condition.

Prohibited Items
Guests, including law enforcement personnel not present in an official capacity, are prohibited from bringing weapons into the arena. Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center does not provide weapon lockers and will not check in weapons at Guest Services. Weapons include, but are not limited to, the following: firearms, explosives, stun guns, handcuffs, brass knuckles, sticks, clubs, batons, martial arts instruments, pepper spray, tear gas, knives, etc. Guests found in possession of the above-mentioned items will be asked to remove the item from the arena or dispose of it. Guests who refuse to comply will be ejected from the arena and may be subject to arrest
The following is a list of items prohibited inside the venue:
Cameras with interchangeable/detachable lens or external flash (No Professional Photography)

Backpacks or oversized bags

Flammable Liquids, Aerosol Cans, or Permanent Markers

Smoking Policy
Dignity Health Arena, Theater & Convention Center are smoke free buildings. The smoking area for Dignity Health Arena is located outside the blue doors behind section 114/115. The smoking area for the Theater & Convention Center will vary from event to event. Please see an usher and they will direct you to the smoking area.
Sign Language Interpreters
We are pleased to offer signed performance for our hearing impaired guests. Interpretation services are not guaranteed for every event; however, we will make every effort to accommodate your request. Requests for this service must be received at least four weeks prior to the event by contacting the Human Resources Manager at 661-852-7314.
