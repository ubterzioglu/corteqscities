Frequently asked questions
Does admission include a pumpkin?
Yes. One pumpkin is included with each paid Pumpkin Patch admission. Children ages 1 and under enter free, but their admission does not include a pumpkin.
Are all activities included?
General Admission covers the regular included farm attractions. The Paintball Gallery and Apple Blaster require an additional purchase. Super Admission includes 100 paintball shots.
Can we visit in the morning?
Regular Pumpkin Patch admission begins at 2 PM Monday–Thursday and 1 PM Friday–Sunday. Wednesday–Friday morning hours are reserved for scheduled school tours.
Is there food at the Pumpkin Patch?
Yes. Seasonal snacks and refreshments are offered at the farm. The Cook Shack is open Friday–Sunday. Specific menu items can vary.
Are there senior or veteran discounts?
Guests aged 65 and older and veterans with identification are eligible for a 10% at-gate discount. Discount offers cannot be combined.
Where can I purchase tickets?
Visit our online Square store to explore available ticket options, or purchase admission at the farm gate.

Murray Family Farms Musical Playground: Where Creativity Comes to Life
Step into a world where rhythm and melody blend seamlessly with the natural beauty of the farm at the Murray Family Farms Musical Playground. This hands-on attraction is a celebration of sound, offering guests the chance to play with outdoor instruments and create their own symphonies in a unique, farm-inspired setting. Whether you’re tapping out a beat on the xylophone, striking a note on chimes, or exploring other instruments, the Musical Playground is designed to unleash creativity and bring families together through the joy of music.
Best of all? The Musical Playground is included with your admission to the Bakersfield Pumpkin Patch, making it a must-visit attraction that adds a melodic twist to your day on the farm. Whether you're a budding musician or just looking for a unique, family-friendly activity, this is one experience you won't want to miss!

Apple Blaster: Aim for an Unforgettable Experience
Get ready for a high-speed thrill that combines precision and pure fun—the Apple Blaster at Murray Family Farms’ Bakersfield Pumpkin Patch! Step up, load your apple cannon, and fire away at distant targets. Whether you're aiming for a bullseye or just having fun with family and friends, the Apple Blaster is an experience that will get your adrenaline pumping. This one-of-a-kind activity is the perfect blend of excitement and challenge. Ready to prove your marksmanship? Take a shot with the Apple Blaster and enjoy a farm experience like no other! (Just don’t forget—it’s not included with general admission, so plan ahead for this extra fun!)
