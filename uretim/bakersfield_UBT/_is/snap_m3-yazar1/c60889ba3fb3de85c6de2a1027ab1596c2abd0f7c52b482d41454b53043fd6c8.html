Group Tours

A group tour is an excellent opportunity for travel groups, service organizations, and large parties to receive a special group rate when visiting the Kern County Museum. Group tours must make a reservation in advance, use one form of payment for the entire group, and consist of 20 or more people to receive a special group rate. Please telephone 661-437-3330 ext 210 for information and/or assistance with your reservation.

How to Make a Reservation

Tour fees may be paid in advance to secure the date and time of your choice. Methods of payment accepted are cash, check, or credit card (VISA, MasterCard, and Discover).
