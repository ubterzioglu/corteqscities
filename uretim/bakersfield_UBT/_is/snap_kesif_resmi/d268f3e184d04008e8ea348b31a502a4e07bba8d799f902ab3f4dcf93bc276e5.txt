Use the options below to learn how to obtain certified or informational birth and death certificates from Kern County Public Health.
Birth Certificates
Order a Birth Certificate
Eligibility
The birth must have occurred in Kern County.
The birth must have occurred after 1964.
For births before 1965, contact the Kern County Recorder’s Office
Fees
$31.00 per copy for both certified or informational certificates
How to Request a Birth Certificate
1. By Mail
English (PDF)
Spanish (PDF)
Application must be notarized.
Mail your:
Completed Application for Birth Certificate
Check or money order (no cash)
Self-addressed stamped envelope
To:
Kern County Public Health
Attn: Office of Vital Records
1800 Mt Vernon Ave, 1st Floor
Bakersfield, CA 93306-3302
2. In Person
Monday – Friday, 8:00 A.M. – 5:00 P.M. (excluding holidays)
Visit:
Kern County Public Health
1800 Mt. Vernon Ave, 1st Floor
Bakersfield, CA 93306-3302
(661) 321-3000
3. Online
Order securely through VitalChek.

Death Certificates
Order a Birth Certificate
Eligibility
The death must have occurred in Kern County.
The death must have occurred after 1992.
For deaths before 1993, contact the Kern County Recorder’s Office.
Fees
$26.00 per copy (certified or informational death certificate)
$23.00 per copy (certified or informational fetal death certificate)
How to Request a Death Certificate
1. By Mail - General Public
Complete the Death Certificate Request
English (PDF)
Spanish (PDF)
Application must be notarized.
Mail your:
Completed Application for Birth Certificate
Check or money order (no cash)
Self-addressed stamped envelope
To:
Kern County Public Health
Attn: Office of Vital Records
1800 Mt Vernon Ave, 1st Floor
Bakersfield, CA 93306-3302
2. By Mail - Funeral Establishments
Credit Card Authorization Form (PDF)
Funeral Establishment Death Certificate Request (PDF)
Mail both forms along with payment and self-addressed stamped envelope to the address above.

Monday – Friday, 8:00 A.M. – 5:00 P.M. (excluding holidays)
