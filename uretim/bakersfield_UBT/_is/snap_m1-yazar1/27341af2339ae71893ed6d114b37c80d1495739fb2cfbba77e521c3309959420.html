    • Welcome to Luigi's

    Luigi’s Restaurant & Delicatessen has proudly stood at its present location since 1910, making it one of California’s oldest family-operated businesses and one of its most treasured landmarks. Learn More

    Menus

    Luigi's Restaurant offers a menu steeped in tradition, featuring authentic recipes passed down for over 115 years. Alongside these time-honored recipes, you'll also find classic favorites that bring comfort and familiarity to the table. It's old-world flavor with a timeless touch.

    The Deli

    Our Luigi's Delicatessen & Wine Shop is located directly next door - connected to the restaurant. You can fill your shopping cart with branded Luigi's merchandise, Italian hosting essentials and pottery, curated specialty products, and freshly sliced meats and cheeses. Find all of Luigi’s house made products from our Extra Virgin Olive Oil to our fresh lasagnas.
