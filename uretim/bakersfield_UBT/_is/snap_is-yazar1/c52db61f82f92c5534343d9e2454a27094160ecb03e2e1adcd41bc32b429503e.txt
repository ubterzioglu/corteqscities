Court hours
Court is in session:
8:30 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. morning session
1:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. afternoon session
Injured worker workshop:
Workshops are held via Zoom at 3 p.m. on the first Friday of every month in English and the third Friday of every month in Spanish.
To request a continuance or to contact the office: E-mail us
The court lobby is open from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
There is no calendar on Mondays.
Walk through of case-opening documents are expected to comply with 8 CCR section 10417(d), which requires that the document be filed by noon on the day prior to the walk through conference.
Walk-through hours are 8:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. and 1:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. Monday through Friday.
See the presiding judge regarding any emergency situations.
