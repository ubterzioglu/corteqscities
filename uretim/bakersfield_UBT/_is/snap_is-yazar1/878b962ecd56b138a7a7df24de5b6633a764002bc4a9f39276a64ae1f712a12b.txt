Learn More/FAQs
We realize you have choices when it comes to choosing advisors for your business. The experts at your local SBDC and across the Central California SBDC Network provide no-cost business advising and are 100% focused on making companies start, grow and thrive. Here are answers to many of the commonly asked questions about working with your local SBDC. Don’t see answer you’re looking for? Call or email us.
Do I have to be in business to use the services of the SBDC?
No. You only have to be considering the idea of opening a business, researching the feasibility of a proposed profit-making venture, or be an existing small business to take advantage of our resources.
How does the Central California SBDC work?
It starts with registering for SBDC assistance. Tell us about your company and needs and we tell you how we can help. Then, we establish goals to work on (business loan, equity capital, etc.) and we work together to reach that goal. We will meet with you in-person, on the phone or at a mutually convenient location. It’s like having another confidential member on your team – someone who is specifically focused on the business goal you identified as most important.
Is there a cost or fee for me to get help from the SBDC or meet with the Advisors?
The SBDC’s professional advising services are offered at no cost. Most workshops and classes are low or no cost.
Who are Central California SBDC Business Advisors?
SBDC Business advisors are individuals who have significant experience in the private sector, and many have extensive management and financial consulting backgrounds. Our SBDC advisors can offer individual guidance concerning the formation, management, financing and operation of your business. Sign up today!
How do I work with the advisors?
After initial consultation, our expert SBDC staff will pair you up with the advisor(s) best suited to help you with your business needs. The advisor(s) will contact you to arrange a meeting at your earliest convenience, and start the process of assisting you. Amount of time spent with a advisor will vary, depending on the issues you are facing and the complexity of your business needs.
Are there any grants, loans or financing available from the SBDC for my business?
The SBDC does not provide grants or loans. Grants generally are designed to support non-profit organizations and state or local governments.
While the SBDC does not provide loans or financing, we work closely with financial partners who offer a wide variety of loan programs, as well as other regional and community funding sources. The SBDC financing professionals can also assist you in going through the process of applying for funds.
Are my employees allowed to attend the workshops and classes?
Owners, managers and key employees are welcome to sign up for the workshops and classes.
How do I get a business loan?
In most situations getting a business loan is challenging and usually requires extensive preparation. While the SBDC does not provide loans, we have financing professionals who understand what lenders want to see in a borrower’s proposal. SBDC consultants will also give advice on which source of funding is right for you.
I’m thinking of starting a business. What should I do first?
Starting a business requires a number of important steps, from planning to funding. Whether you’re just in the idea stage, or have started up, the SBDC offers a variety of resources to assist you, as well as workshops and classes to help you understand the ins and outs of the process. SBDC consultants can then help you with the details of turning your plan and business concept into a successful reality.
My business has been established for some time. How can the SBDC help me?
The SBDC can assist you in every phase and most areas of your business. Our expert staff and local professional business consultants will work with you. The SBDC offers a comprehensive spectrum of services, including advising and management training, as well as an exceptional variety of classes.
I’d like to learn the essential things I need to know about starting and running a Business. Do you offer a class on this?
Great question, and yes. We offer a virtual Business Bootcamp program. Email us to find out when the next program will start.
