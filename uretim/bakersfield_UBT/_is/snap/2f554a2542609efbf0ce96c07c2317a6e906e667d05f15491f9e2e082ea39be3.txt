Rebates and Conservation Programs
Cal Water offers both indoor and outdoor rebates and programs to help our customers save water every day. For more information on conservation programs, Qualified Product Lists and rebates currently available in your district, please visit our new Conservation Portal and click “Sign Up Now” to create a new user account and get started. For assistance with rebates, programs or the Conservation Portal, click “Contact Us” at the top of this page and select Rebates and Conservation Programs in the dropdown menu for the subject.

Residential Programs and Rebates
Smart Landscape Tune-Ups
Cal Water is offering a Smart Landscape Tune-Up Program to eligible residential customers in select service areas to help save water outdoors. This program is available at no charge. Customers must have an active, functional irrigation system to be eligible for this program.
How Does It Work?
There are four easy steps to participate:
Visit the Conservation Portal to confirm that the program is available in your area.
Sign up to receive an irrigation system efficiency evaluation from one of our contractors.
A Cal Water conservation team member will verify your eligibility and initiate the scheduling process with the contractors.
Upon receiving a call from our contractor, schedule a site visit with one of our contractors to receive a site evaluation.
To participate in our Smart Landscape Tune-Up program, please visit our new Conservation Portal and create a user account.
If you would like to speak to a Cal Water representative about this program, please click “Contact Us” at the top of this page and select Rebates and Conservation Programs in the dropdown menu for the subject.
Conservation Kits
We are pleased to offer our single-family residential customers conservation kits featuring a range of water-saving plumbing retrofit fixtures. These kits are available at no charge to help make conserving water that much easier.
Each kit includes:
Two high-efficiency showerheads (use 1.5 gallons per minute [gpm])
One hose nozzle
Two bathroom faucet aerators (use 1.0 gpm)
Toilet leak tablets
One kitchen faucet aerator (uses 1.5 gpm)
One Practical Plumbing Handbook
These fixtures are available to current Cal Water single-family residential customers while supplies last and will be mailed to the service address on record. There is a limit of one kit per service address per three-year period. To request your free Conservation Kit, please visit our new Conservation Portal and create a user account.
Lawn-to-garden (lawn replacement) rebate
The Lawn-to-garden program provides a per-square-foot rebate for our customers that transform grassy areas into water-wise gardens and landscapes using our recommended lists of beautiful California Native plants. 3 approved plants per 100 square foot converted is required. Artificial turf does not qualify for this rebate and pre-approval is required prior to beginning your project. For more information on program availability, eligible plants and qualifications, please visit our new Conservation Portal and create a user account.
Residential high-efficiency toilet rebate (MaP Premium Models)
Cal Water offers rebates for replacing old residential toilets with high-efficiency MaP Premium toilets. In addition to meeting the rigorous performance standards of WaterSense labeled toilets, MaP premium toilets must use 1.1 gallons per flush or less and meet a minimum MaP threshold of 600 grams of waste removed from the toilet fixture in a single flush. Toilets must have been purchased within 6 months of the date applying. To apply for a residential toilet rebate and search eligible products please visit our new Conservation Portal and create a user account.
High-efficiency clothes washer rebate
Cal Water offers rebates for upgrading to high-efficiency clothes washers that use 35-50% less water and approximately 50% less energy than traditional washers. To apply for a residential high-efficiency clothes washer rebate and search eligible products please visit our new Conservation Portal and create a user account.
Smart irrigation controller rebate
Cal Water offers rebates for upgrading to smart irrigation controllers automatically adjust their watering schedule in response to changing weather conditions. Please visit the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
High-efficiency sprinkler nozzle rebate
Cal Water offers rebates for upgrading to high-efficiency sprinkler nozzles that save water by applying water more slowly and uniformly, minimizing run-off. Please visit the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
Commercial Rebates
High-efficiency toilet rebate
Cal Water offers commercial rebates for High-Efficiency Toilets (HETs) which are defined as fixtures that flush at 20% below the 1.6-gallons-per-flush (gpf) U.S. maximum or less, equating to a maximum of 1.28-gpf. (The HET category includes dual-flush toilets). New toilet must be replacing an existing toilet that flushes at 3.5 gpf or greater. Please visit the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
High-efficiency urinal rebate
Cal Water offers commercial rebates for High-Efficiency Toilets which use less water than traditional urinals and include non-water urinals. New urinal must flush at .125 gpf or less and be replacing an existing urinal that flushes at 1 gpf or greater. Please visit the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
Smart irrigation controller rebate
Cal Water offers commercial rebates for upgrading to smart irrigation controllers automatically adjust their watering schedule in response to changing weather conditions. Please visit the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
Large rotary sprinkler nozzle rebate
Cal Water offers commercial rebates for large rotary nozzles which improve distribution uniformity while controlling head-to-head pressure variations and misting. Sign up for the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
Pressure regulated sprinkler spray body rebate
Cal Water offers commercial rebates for spray bodies with integrated pressure regulation and check valves that save water by providing optimal pressure and minimizing loss of water through low-head drainage. Sign up for the Conservation Portal for more information and a searchable list of qualified products.
