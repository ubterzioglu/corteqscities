🚨 SERVICE ALERT | Sunday, October 25
Due to scheduled track work, select Gold Runner trains will have modified service between Fresno and Bakersfield on Sunday, October 25.
Affected Trains:
Southbound: Trains 702, 704, 710, 712, 714, and 716 will terminate in Fresno.
Northbound: Trains 703, 713, 715, 717, and 719 will originate in Fresno.
Alternate Bus Transportation:
Connecting bus service will be provided between Fresno and Bakersfield, with an intermediate stop in Hanford.
Fresno to Bakersfield: Buses 4302, 4304, 4310, 4312, 4314, and 4316.
Bakersfield to Fresno: Buses 4303, 4313, 4315, 4317, and 4319.
Please Note: No alternate transportation will be provided at Corcoran or Wasco.
Passengers traveling on affected trains are encouraged to plan ahead and allow additional travel time.
