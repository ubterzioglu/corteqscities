Free Bus Rides for Students
UPDATE September 2026: Students, you can continue to use your student passes for the Fall 2026 school year! Students MUST show school ID to board.
Golden Empire Transit District received a grant to provide FREE bus rides to all students Kindergarten through College including vocational and private schools in the Bakersfield Metro area. Students can use GET's service to get to and from school, work, the mall, a friend's house, anywhere a bus can take you!
HOW DOES IT WORK?
Students 7th grade and older must show their student ID on the bus to the driver when presenting the pass. The pass swipes in the fare box like a regular bus pass. The pass does not need to be used only for school trips, and can be used for any activity. Passes are valid until May 31, 2024.
WHO CAN USE IT?
Any student that attends a school in the Bakersfield area. Your school must have agreed to participate in the program to receive passes.
HOW LONG WILL IT LAST?
Passes are valid through Summer!
WHERE DO WE GET A PASS?
For Summer 2026, you can pick up a pass at the Customer Service Center next to the Downtown Transit Center. 2111 Chester Ave.
WHAT IF I LOSE MY PASS? CAN I GET ANOTHER ONE?
If your pass is damaged and can't be used in the fare box, you can turn it in for a replacement pass at the Customer Service Center, 2111 Chester Ave. Treat your card like cash and keep it in a safe place when not in use.
I DON'T HAVE A STUDENT ID.
All students 7th grade and older must present a current student ID to the driver in order to use the student pass. If you do not have a student photo ID, you can get one taken at our offices at 1830 Golden State Ave., or at our Customer Service Center on Chester Ave. You will need to bring proof of enrollment, like your school schedule.
