What are the individualized services available with enrollment?
Plan B Individualized Career Services
Includes all Plan A – Basic Self-Services
Career Planning, Exploration & Counseling provided by trained staff
WorkKeys Assessments
Three 55 minute tests to show employers your skill levels – Workplace Documents, Graphic Literacy and Applied Mathematics
4-Day Job Search Assistance Class
Monday-Thursday 8AM-4PM to cover resume building, interview preparation, job searching and career counseling by staff instructor
Farmworker Opportunity Programs
Job services for migrant & seasonal farmworkers and their dependents – Workshops, Bilingual Job Search Class, paid work experience, training opportunities, rent & utilities assistance available
Young Adult Programs / EPIC Center
Job Services for young adults aged 18 – 24 who have barriers to graduating high school, finding a job or launching a career
WIOA Requirements
Must be 18 years or older, resident of Kern, Inyo or Mono counties, High School Diploma or GED, Legal right to work status and documents, meets Selective Services requirements, actively seeking full time employment
