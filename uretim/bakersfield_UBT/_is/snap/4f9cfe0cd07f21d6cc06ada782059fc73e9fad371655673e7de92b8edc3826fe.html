What are the services available without enrolling?

Resource Room Access
Monday – Friday from 8AM – 4:30PM

  • Computers with internet access for job search
  • Printers, copiers, scanners, fax machines and telephones available

Self-Referrals to Job Openings
Register online at CalJOBS.ca.gov

Typing Tests (No Cost)
Tuesday & Thursdays at 12:00PM by appointment at SE Bakersfield AJCC Office – see list for other locations available in Kern County
