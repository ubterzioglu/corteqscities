Overview
Take a self-guided tour of arts attractions. Stop in for a coffee at the locally owned Dagny's, catch a concert at Jerry's Pizza, where the hometown, Grammy-winning band Korn first played or check out the Metro Galleries to see the best in locally produced artwork. Don't forget to pick up an Arts & Culture District map at the visitors center.
