Dine With us
From rooftop ambiance to hipster hangouts, dining and bar options await at The Padre. Grab a table at Brimstone as you feast on an elevated menu that excellently pairs with its unmatched selection of local brews, wine, and top shelf whiskey at this bar's saloon-like digs. Or tune into weekly happy hour and live music perched upon Prairie Fire's vibey, outdoor lounge before waking up to a vibrant selection of breakfast eats and local coffee at Farmacy Café.
