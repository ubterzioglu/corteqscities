Privacy Policy
Personal information entered into the EnviroFlash system (e.g., name, email address, ZIP Code) are used (by EPA) solely for the purpose of sending subscriber-requested air quality notifications. Email addresses and other information are not shared, sold, or otherwise disclosed to any other organizations or individuals. All information submitted by visitors is voluntary. EnviroFlash is a Confirmed Opt-In (COI) system in which subscribers ask to be included in the list and email address confirmation is required before notifications are sent. EnviroFlash subscribers retain complete control of their subscription at all times, and may modify or delete their subscription using the EnviroFlash website (www.enviroflash.info) or by contacting info@airnowtech.org Additional privacy policy information can be found at https://www.enviroflash.info/privacy.cfm.
