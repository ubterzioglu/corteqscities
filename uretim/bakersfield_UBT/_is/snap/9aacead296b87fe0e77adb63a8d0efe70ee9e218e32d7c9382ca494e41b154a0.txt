Kern River Valley Dog Park Now Open
The new dog park, located next to the Linda Kissack Ballfield in Lake Isabella, is now open. Address information and a map link can be found in the parks list below.
General Information
The Kern County Parks Division has neighborhood parks with picnic areas in almost every community in Kern County. Most of these parks offer you cooling shade trees, playgrounds and picnic tables. They are an ideal spot for a family gathering, for example, as an inexpensive and fun alternative for your children's birthday parties. Availability is by reservation or first come/first serve, if the park shelter is not reserved by another party. You can even bring a bounce house, with a confirmed reservation, if it is on our list of approved providers.
