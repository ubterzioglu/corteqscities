The Historic Bakersfield Fox Theater
Share
Overview
The Majestic Fox Theater is Bakersfield's premier source for concerts and live entertainment events. The historical theater provides extraordinary acoustics in an intimate setting to deliver an amazing entertainment experience that you won't find anywhere else. Enjoy top name artists, comedy events, movies and much more! Recent artists to perform at the Fox include The Pretenders, Carlos Mencia, and John Prine. Additionally, the Fox is home to FLICS, a local organization that hosts viewings of foreign films each Friday evening.
