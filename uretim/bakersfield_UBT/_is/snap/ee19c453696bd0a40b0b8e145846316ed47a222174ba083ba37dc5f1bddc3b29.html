Our Mission

Mission for Portrait of a Warrior Gallery Kern County

The Portrait of a Warrior Gallery Kern County is established to enhance our community by creating portraits of our Post 9/11 Fallen Service Members; working directly with their Gold Star Families to keep their memories alive and portray these heroes through art, education & services for our local Veterans, their families & our community

The Gallery

Memories kept vibrantly alive

The purpose of the Portrait of a Warrior Gallery is to keep our memories of Kern County's Fallen Heroes alive through portrait display, biographies, education and ever-evolving exhibits. The Gallery is in a continuous state of transformation to accommodate the healing of our veteran population and to educate the communities within Kern County.

We will never allow the memories of our Fallen Heroes to fade.

Inside the Gallery

Rooms that tell the story

Vietnam Veterans Memorial Room

176 photographs of Kern County's Vietnam fallen, with their branch, rank and date of casualty, alongside memorabilia entrusted to us by local veterans.

Explore the room

Hall of Honor

22 crosses represent the veteran and active duty suicides that occur every day, alongside drawings of post-9/11 veterans and America's cost of freedom.

Explore the room

Resource Room

Health and mental health assistance, veteran services, and veteran-based recreational programs across the state.

Explore the room

Additional Exhibits

The Gallery also features a Team Room which emulates what living conditions were like in Iraq and Afghanistan for our service members, and an Education Room, a unique space for veteran organizations to hold meetings and conduct Q&A sessions with veterans for school field trips learning the cost of freedom.
