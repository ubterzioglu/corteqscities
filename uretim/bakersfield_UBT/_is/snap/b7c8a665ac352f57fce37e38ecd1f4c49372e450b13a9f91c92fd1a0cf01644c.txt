Overview
Murray Family Farm offers an ‘on the farm’ experience for the whole family! Enjoy hand-picking in-season fruits and vegetables or shop the market for fresh local produce. Farm tours, educational demonstrations and live entertainment is available. Enjoy an opportunity to sample seasonal delights in the country store while the kids pet the farm animals or try to find the exit in the maze.
