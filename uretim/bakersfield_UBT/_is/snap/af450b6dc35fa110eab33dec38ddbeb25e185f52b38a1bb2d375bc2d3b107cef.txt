Bakersfield: A Journey Through Time Historical Trail
Share
19th Street looking west c. 1920s
“History is who we are and why we are the way we are.”
David McCullough, American Historian (1933-2022)
Get ready to step into the past with the Bakersfield: A Journey Through Time Historical Trail! Each step you take along this trail, will open your eyes to the rich and enduring history of our great city. You'll learn about it's early pioneers, and visit historical sites while having a fun time! With over 25 stops to explore, there’s sure to be some hidden gems or historical facts that will peak your interests.
As you discover the history of Bakersfield, you'll earn points at each site you visit for a chance to win some great prizes. Keeping track of your points is easy, simply visit one of the Places To Go on the pass, and complete the check-in quiz with your free digital passport. Make sure you listen carefully to get the right answer! Sign up today and let the journey begin!
While taking this tour, we strongly encourage participants to follow all traffic laws and safety guidelines at all times. Have fun and be safe!
HISTORICAL LOCATIONS
Here are just a few of the many locations that you will visit:
Bakersfield Fire Station No. 1
The Historic Fox Theater
The Haberfelde Building
The Standard Oil Building
The Kress Building
Padre Hotel
The Bakersfield Californian Building
and lastly,
The Historic Woolworth Building!
Be sure to use the map within the mobile trail pass for directions and addresses of each of the points of interests. Also, keep an eye on your pass, new places to explore may be added periodically.
