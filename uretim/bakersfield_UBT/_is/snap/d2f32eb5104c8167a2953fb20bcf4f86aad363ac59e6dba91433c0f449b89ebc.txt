Frequently Asked Questions
Can I pay cash?
If paying with cash, you must have exact change. The driver cannot make change. We recommend using the Transit App or buying passes by mail or at GET’s downtown transit center.
What time does the trolley run?
Only on Wednesday, Thursday Friday, Saturday
10:00AM – 10:00PM
Can I bring my service animal on board?
Yes. Service animals are allowed on trolley vehicles, if there is enough room. Pets must be in a carrier to board.
Can the trolley be rented for private events?
No. The trolley is a public service route connecting Downtown and Old Town Kern. The trolley is on a fixed schedule and route.
Can the trolley take me directly where I want to go?
The Trolley will stop at designated stops along the route. If your destination is away from the stop, you will need to make your own accommodations to go to a specific location. For ADA accessible rides, we encourage you to apply for GET-A-LIFT, which is a curb-to-curb service.
How many people fit on one Trolley Vehicle?
TBA
I’m a visitor and don’t want GET-A-LIFT. Is the Trolley ADA accessible?
There is one wheelchair ramp on board.
I have a lot of things with me. How much can I bring on board?
You may only bring as much as you can carry. The driver cannot assist you in bringing items on board. Strollers must be ready to be stowed at the time of boarding.
For More Information
Call 869-2GET(2438) for GET’s customer service team. Our representatives can guide you on how to ride, route information and stop locations.
