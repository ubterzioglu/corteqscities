Are You Eligible?
Head Start programs across the nation use the Federal Poverty Guidelines to determine eligibility. However, your child may also be eligible if they or your family identifies with one or more of these categories:
You receive SSI or Public Assistance (Public Assistance eligibility requires specific Aid Code);
You are a SNAP/CalFresh recipient;
Your child has an IFSP or IEP;
You are experiencing homelessness.
Did you know? Extra enrollment priority is granted to families experiencing homelessness, Foster Children, children with an IEP or IFSP, and more.
To apply for enrollment in our State Child Development program, parents or guardians must be working, seeking employment, enrolled in education or job training programs, or medically incapacitated.
