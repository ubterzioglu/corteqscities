Welcome to Multilingual Education Programs
The Multilingual Educational Programs Department is committed to improving services for the District’s English learners. We promote academic excellence, equity, inclusion, and access for all the District’s English learners
The District has adopted a variety of instructional programs to best meet the needs of these students. The programs are designed to help English learners fully and meaningfully access and participate in a 21st century education that results in their attaining high levels of English proficiency, mastery of grade level standards, and opportunities to develop proficiency in multiple languages.
El Departamento de Educación Multilingüe está comprometido a mejorar los servicios para los estudiantes aprendices de inglés del distrito. Nosotros promovemos la excelencia académica, la equidad, la inclusión, y el acceso para todos los estudiantes aprendices de inglés del Distrito.
El distrito ha adoptado una variedad de programas educativos para satisfacer mejor las necesidades de estos estudiantes. Los programas están diseñados para ayudar a los estudiantes aprendices de inglés a acceder y participar plena y significativamente en una educación del siglo 21 que les permita alcanzar altos niveles de dominio del inglés, dominio de los estándares de nivel de grado y oportunidades para desarrollar el dominio de varios idiomas.

English Learner Programs
Bakersfield City School District implements a Comprehensive English Language Development Program in ALL program options for English Learners.
Structured English Immersion (SEI) is a language acquisition program for English learners in grades TK-8th grade in which nearly all classroom instruction is provided in English, but with curriculum and a presentation designed for pupils who are learning English. (EC Section 306[c]).
This program provides language learning and academic instruction for native English speakers and native Spanish speakers in English and Spanish and includes structured and sequential daily English Language Development (ELD) (EC Section 306[c]).

Reclassification Resources for Families
Reclassification is the process that determines whether an English learner should be reclassified as Fluent English Proficient (R-FEP). The California Department of Education reclassification relies on evidence demonstrating that the student has achieved a level of English proficiency comparable to that of average native speakers of English in the district and is achieving and sustaining a level of academic achievement with students whose native language is English.

Immigrants / Newcomers
A Newcomer (Immigrant) Is a Child or Youth That Is Between the Ages of 3 and 21, were not born in any of the States in the United States, and Has Not Been Attending One or More Schools in Any One or More States for More than 3 Full Academic Years.
