988 is an easy-to-remember nationwide phone number to connect people experiencing mental health-related distress with trained crisis counselors in more than 200 crisis centers. These counselors offer skilled, compassionate help for mental health and substance use problems for anyone at any time.
988 Lifeline services are free and confidential.
When to contact 988
You can contact 988 for yourself or if you are worried about a loved one who may need crisis support. That could be:
Suicidal thoughts
Mental health challenges
Substance use crises
Emotional distress
How to connect to 988
The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week nationwide. To reach a 988 crisis center:
Call or text 988
Chat online at chat.988lifeline.org
Call 988 with a Videophone
Specialized counselors
Crisis centers also help connect people to specialized services including:
Veterans Crisis Line
Call 988 and press “1”
Chat at veteranscrisisline.net/get-help-now/chat/
Text “838255” to 988
988 counselors in other languages
Trained crisis counselors who speak Spanish, American Sign Language (ASL), and other languages are available.
Spanish-Speaking Counselors
Voice callers press “2”
Text “AYUDA” to 988
Chat at linea988.org
American Sign Language (ASL)
Videophone (VP) or text 988
or chat.988Lifeline.org
If you or a loved one speaks another language, call 988 and ask for an interpreter in English if you are able. Or you can say the name of the language you need. You will then be connected with an interpreter. This is available 24/7 through voice calling only.
