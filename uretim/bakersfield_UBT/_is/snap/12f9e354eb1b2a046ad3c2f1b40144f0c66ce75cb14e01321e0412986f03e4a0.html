FOX THEATER - VENUE POLICIES

Cashless

Starting September 1, 2026, our venues will be going cashless.

All purchases throughout the venues will be made using card or contactless payment only. All major credit cards accepted.

Purchasing your Tickets

Buy your official tickets online via our Calendar of Events.

Each person attending must purchase a ticket, regardless of age. Contact the Box Office for specific requirements for infants and toddlers.

Cameras, Audio and Video Recorders

Usually, non-professional point-and-shoot cameras without flash and cell phone cameras are permitted, however, audio or video recorders are not permitted in The Fox Theater. Professional cameras, audio, or video recorders are not permitted. This policy is adjusted to accommodate the preferences of the artist. To inquire if cameras, audio, or video recorders are permitted for a particular event, please contact 661.324.1369.

Security Screening

All patrons are subject to a courteous screening upon entering The Fox Theater. Security measures may include screening via metal detector. All personal belongings (purses, bags, backpacks, etc,) are subject to inspection and prohibited items discovered during security inspections must either be returned to the owner’s vehicle or discarded. Items will not be held for later pick-up.

Prohibited Items

Items prohibited in The Fox Theater include – but are not limited to – weapons, aerosol cans (hair spray, mace, pepper spray, etc.), outside food & beverage, pocket knives, utility tools, laser light pointers, bottles, cans, large bags and coolers. Prohibited items discovered during security inspections must either be returned to the owner’s vehicle or discarded. Items will not be held for later pick-up. The Fox Theater Foundation reserves the right to expand or modify the items prohibited at any time.

‍Starting September 1, 2026, all venues will move to a Clear Bag Policy.

Clear Bag Policy Guidelines

Approved Bags

  • Plastic Storage Bags: One-gallon clear plastic freezer bags (e.g., Ziploc or similar) are fully permitted.
  • Small Clutches / Wallets: Small non-clear clutch bags or wallets are allowed, provided they do not exceed 4.5" × 6.5" (about the size of a hand).
  • Medical Bags & Diaper Bags: Permitted but are subject to mandatory security screening and X-ray inspection prior to entry.

Prohibited Bags

  • Backpacks of any kind (including clear backpacks)
  • Briefcases, luggage, and computer bags
  • Opaque (non-clear) purses and large tote bags
  • Tinted or patterned plastic bags (contents must remain 100% visible)
  • Cinch bags and string bags

Key Guidelines

  • Limit: Each ticketed guest may bring one large clear bag and one small clutch.
  • Inspection: All approved bags are subject to inspection at the security checkpoint.
  • Personal Items: Blankets, jackets, and permitted medical devices may be carried separately and are subject to inspection upon request.
  • Guests arriving with prohibited bags must return them to their vehicle or dispose of them before entering. Items will not be held for later pick-up.

Disturbances

Patrons must maintain appropriate behavior at all times. Any person using offensive language, engaging in disorderly conduct, disturbing other patrons, or violating Venue Policies may be ejected from the theater. Ejected patrons will not receive a ticket refund.

Parking

Street parking is available around the Fox Theater and the surrounding areas.

Smoking

Smoking is prohibited inside The Fox Theater. Designated smoking areas are located outside the building, 20 feet away from any entrance or window.

Serving and Consumption of Alcohol

The Fox Theater has the right to deny alcoholic beverage service to anyone. Anyone under the age of 21 who is observed consuming alcohol or appears intoxicated will be removed from the theater.

Ticket Reselling and Scalping

Any ticket resold at a value higher than listed on the ticket or sold in or around the Fox Theater is illegal and will be reported to local law enforcement.
