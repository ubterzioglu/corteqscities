Welcome to EnviroFlash! Air quality affects how you live and breathe. Like the weather, it can change from day to day, or even hour to hour. Up-to-date information allows you to make decisions based on air quality forecasts. EnviroFlash comes to you through a partnership between the US EPA and your state or local air quality agency - notifying you about air quality so you don't have to go searching for it!
Note: Not all agencies issuing forecasts through AirNow participate in the EnviroFlash programs
EnviroFlash is sponsored by the EPA with support from state and local air quality agencies. EnviroFlash provides air quality information such as forecasts and action day notifications via email for your area of interest.
An on-line subscription page allows you to sign up, edit your profile, or cancel the service. After you choose the type and frequency of service you want, EnviroFlash will be sent to your email or cell phone as specified.
