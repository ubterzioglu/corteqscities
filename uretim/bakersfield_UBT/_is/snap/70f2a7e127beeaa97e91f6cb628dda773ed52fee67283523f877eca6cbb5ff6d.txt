Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center is Bakersfield’s home for live entertainment! Home of the AHL’s Bakersfield Condors, Dignity Health Arena, Theater and Convention Center hosts concerts, family shows, community activities and many other events throughout the year. The complex is owned by the City of Bakersfield.
