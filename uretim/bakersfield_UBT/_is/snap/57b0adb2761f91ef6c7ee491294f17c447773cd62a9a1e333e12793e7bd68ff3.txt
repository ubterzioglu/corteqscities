Donations
Although we receive grant funds for revegetation and education, no grant funding is discretionary. Donations are vital as they help maintain the Preserve; supplies, gasoline for our tractor and gator, equipment maintenance, PG&E & water bills, insurance, and more. Without donations, we would not be able to maintain the Preserve.
KRCE/Panorama Vista Preserve is a 501 (c) (3) public benefit nonprofit organization. All Donations are tax-deductible
