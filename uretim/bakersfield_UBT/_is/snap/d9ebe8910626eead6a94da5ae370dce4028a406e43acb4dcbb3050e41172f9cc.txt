About the Gallery
The Jones Art Gallery at Bakersfield College is dedicated to showcasing a diverse range of contemporary visual art while supporting student learning, creative exploration, and community engagement. The gallery hosts rotating exhibitions featuring Bakersfield College students, Kern County high school students, faculty, and guest artists.

Past Exhibitions
Panorama Invitational | 2026
Exhibition Dates: February 2 - March 3, 2026
The Panorama Invitational is an annual exhibition showcasing the artwork of Kern County high school students. Each year, works are selected by teachers for their craftsmanship and artistic interpretation. The exhibition features a diverse range of media, including painting, sculpture, photography, and mixed media, and provides a platform for emerging artists to share their perspectives with the broader community.
The opening reception took place on Thursday, February 12, 2026, bringing together students, families, educators, and community members to celebrate student achievement and creativity.

Gallery Director
Angel Lesnikowski
Director, Jones Art Gallery
Angel Lesnikowski is a contemporary artist, professor, and the Director of the Jones Art Gallery at Bakersfield College. She holds a Master of Arts in Art from California State University, Fresno, where she graduated with distinction.
Her artistic practice explores themes of mortality, human rights, and feminist advocacy through painting, mixed media, and installation. Lesnikowski has exhibited widely throughout California and serves on the Council of 100 committee for the Fresno Art Museum.
As Gallery Director, she is committed to expanding exhibition opportunities, strengthening community partnerships, and creating accessible, professional gallery experiences for students and visitors alike.

Accessibility Statement
The Jones Art Gallery is committed to providing an inclusive and accessible experience for all visitors. The gallery space is wheelchair accessible and located within an ADA-compliant campus facility.
If you require accommodations or have accessibility-related questions prior to your visit, please contact the Gallery Director in advance so we can best support your needs. angel.lesnikowski@bakersfieldcollege.edu.
Mission Statement
The Jones Gallery is committed to showing a diverse range of visual art produced by contemporary artists living and working outside of Kern County with annual shows devoted to the artworks of Bakersfield College art students, Kern County High School art students (The Panorama Invitational) and a biannual show of the work of the Bakersfield College Fine Arts Faculty.
