Samn Lynn Ball Park
Share
Overview
A recently improved, 72-year old stadium in northern Bakersfield. It has a seating capacity of 1,839, and includes both grandstand stadium seating, as well as open-air skyboxes, and bleacher seating. The ballpark includes two shaded areas: the right field picnic tent and the Cool Zone along the first base line. The stadium is one field, with field lights for night games/events. It includes a built-in concession stand, as well as numerous portable concessions stations and a merchandise store.
