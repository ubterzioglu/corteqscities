Bakersfield College’s Adult Education Program offers tuition-free career development courses to help adult students reach their educational and career goals.

Our program serves a variety of students, including disadvantaged individuals and those learning English as a second language. Bakersfield College Adult Education offers a variety of services to help students succeed, including advising, tutoring, and job readiness assistance.

Adult Education Services

Been on hold with your education? We get it. Bakersfield College Adult Education is here to help you overcome obstacles and reach your goals. We offer tuition-free career-focused courses and support you every step of the way.

Our support includes:

  • Helping you enroll at BC and registering for classes
  • Advising you on an education plan to meet your goals
  • Job readiness and professional development opportunities
  • Connecting you to resources
  • Assisting with foreign degree transfers
  • Technology support, DSPS accommodations, and  tutoring
  • Textbooks and materials (contingent on funding)
