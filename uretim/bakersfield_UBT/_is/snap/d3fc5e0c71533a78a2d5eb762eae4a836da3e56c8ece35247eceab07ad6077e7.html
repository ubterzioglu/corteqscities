Your Guide to Purchasing Tickets

Buying tickets at Ovation Theatre is simple and seamless.

Start Your Journey

Step 1

Choose Your Show

Browse current productions and select the performance date that works best for you.

Step 2

Select Your Seats

Use our interactive seating map to choose exactly where you’d like to sit.

Step 3

Complete Your Purchase

Secure checkout takes just a moment.

Step 4

Receive Your Tickets

Your tickets are delivered instantly via email with a QR code for entry.

Seating at Ovation Theatre

All Mainstage performances feature assigned seating, allowing you to select your exact seats during checkout.

  • Interactive seating map during purchase
  • Seats are reserved immediately upon checkout
  • Accessible seating clearly marked

Helpful Tip:
We recommend selecting seats early for the best availability.
