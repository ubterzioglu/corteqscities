THE STORY OF A THIRD PLACE
Not simply a coffee shop.
A room Bakersfield grew into.
Dagny's opened in 1996 in the heart of downtown Bakersfield. Over three decades, its retro storefront, warm interior, coffee, pastries, music and creative community have made it one of the city's enduring gathering places.
This anniversary archive follows Dagny's through photographs taken across the years — not as a museum piece, but as a living room that kept changing because the people inside it did.
1996
THE BEGINNING
A coffeehouse on the corner.
Dagny's Coffee Company is founded downtown at 20th and Eye Streets. The idea is simple and durable: good coffee, a welcoming room and enough character to make people want to linger.
Business records date the start of Dagny's to October 1996.

2003
THE ROOM BETWEEN
Home, work — and somewhere in between.
By the early 2000s, Dagny's had become the kind of place people used however they needed it: a reading room, a meeting spot, a place to think, a place to talk, a place to do nothing for a while.
“Stay awhile” became less of an invitation than a habit.
2004
A SMALL ROOM, A BIG STAGE
Music became part of the furniture.
Live sets, poetry, impromptu performances and local art gave Dagny's a cultural pulse of its own. The room could turn from coffeehouse to listening room without losing the feeling that everyone belonged there.
Live music at Dagny's · September 2004 · Photo: Felix Adamo
After dark · December 2006 · Photo: Felix Adamo
2006
DAGNY'S IN FULL BLOOM
The visual identity became part of downtown.
The curved corner storefront, the red sign, the illustrated woman above the cup, the handwritten boards and the coffee cards became unmistakable. Inside, the nights ran long and the room kept collecting stories.
2009—12
A DOWNTOWN LIVING ROOM
Conversation, work and celebration shared the same tables.
Some came for a laptop and a quiet corner. Some came to meet friends. Some came for an event and stayed because the next table was interesting. Dagny's identity lived as much in those overlapping uses as it did in the coffee.
Community night · February 2010 · Photo: Felix Adamo
Behind the counter · January 2016 · Photo: Felix Adamo
2016
THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PLACE
A coffeehouse is built one welcome at a time.
Regulars remember drinks, tables and events — but they also remember the people behind the bar. Across the years, Dagny's staff helped turn routine visits into relationships.
2018
A NEW STEWARD
Preserve the soul. Let the room keep evolving.
In April 2018, Annie Florendo stepped into ownership. Her approach centered on stewardship — protecting the community character already there while allowing Dagny's to grow with the people who use it.
The everyday ritual continues · December 2021 · Photo: Felix Adamo
Still on the corner · December 2021 · Photo: Felix Adamo
2021
STILL GATHERING
The same corner. A new generation of regulars.
Three decades of a local place is really three decades of changing faces. Dagny's continued to draw students, artists, readers, musicians, workers, neighbors and newcomers into the same shared room.
2025
THE ARCHIVE LOOKS BACK
The photographs become part of the story.
By the eve of its 30th year, Dagny's history was visible not only in the building, but in the images, flyers, keepsakes and memories accumulated around it — proof that a neighborhood institution is made from thousands of ordinary days.
Looking back · August 2025 · Photo: Felix Adamo
2026
THIRTY YEARS
Still brewing. Still listening. Still here.
Today, Dagny's remains a downtown gathering place for coffee, fresh baked goods, open mics, book gatherings, art and the spontaneous conversations that have always been its real specialty.
