Accessibility
BVM strives to provide an enjoyable environment that is inclusive and accessible to everyone.
​
If you have any questions about your visit please call (661) 324-6350
or email info@buenavistamuseum.org
​
Wheelchair Access
All public areas and exhibits at BVM are accessible to visitors with mobility equipment and wheelchairs.
Elevators
The elevator is located on the east side of the building and can access all three floors of the museum as well as the mezzanine.
Restrooms
Accessible, family restrooms with changing tables are available on the first floor. There is also a first aid station available on the first floor.
Seating
Seating is available throughout the museum on every floor.
Service Animals
BVM allows trained and certified service animals in all public areas of the museum.
Vision
The "Oh Zone" provides tactile opportunities to explore through hands-on activities. In addition, there are some exhibits throughout the museum that include touchable objects.
​
BVM also offers Docent guided group tours, such as our "Sensory Safari" for groups with visual disabilities. Please contact the museum at (661) 324-6350 for more information or to request a tour.
