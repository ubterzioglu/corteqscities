Major Life Changes
If you’ve had a major life change, you may be able to get health insurance through Covered California, even outside of open enrollment. Find out if a recent life change lets you sign up for coverage now.
What is a major life change?
A major life change — also called a qualifying life event — lets you enroll in a Covered California health insurance plan or change your current plan outside the regular open-enrollment period. This is known as a special-enrollment period.
Not sure if you qualify? You can still apply. Covered California will review your application and let you know what you are eligible for.
Common Major Life Changes
These are the most common life changes that qualify for special enrollment. Not sure if your situation counts? Get help from us. You can also apply regardless. Covered California will review your application for Covered California plans, Medi-Cal and other programs.

Lost health insurance through your job
Lost Medi-Cal coverage
Lost student health coverage
COBRA coverage ended

Had a baby, adopted a child or began foster care
Got divorced or legally separated

Moved to or Within California
Moved to California from another state
Moved within California and plan options changed

Lost insurance tied to military service
Special Situations
Experienced domestic abuse or spousal abandonment
Were released from jail or prison
Are a federally recognized American Indian or Alaska Native member
Changes to Existing Enrollment
Lost financial help after an income change
Lost financial help after a household change
Lost insurance after a household member died
