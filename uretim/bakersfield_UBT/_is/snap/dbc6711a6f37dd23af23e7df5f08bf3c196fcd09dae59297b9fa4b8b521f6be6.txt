FAQ
Attractions FAQ
How much does bowling cost at The BLVD! Bakersfield?
Bowling is priced per lane, per hour. Mon–Fri before 5pm: $30/hr/lane. Mon–Thu after 5pm: $35/hr/lane. Sat–Sun before 5pm: $50/hr/lane. Fri–Sun after 5pm: $55/hr/lane. Shoe rental is $5/person; socks $4/pair.
What's the difference between VIP and public lanes?
The BLVD! Bakersfield has 22 total lanes: 8 premium VIP lanes and 14 public lanes. VIP lanes feature ambient lighting, lane-side seating, and a more private feel — ideal for parties, groups, and special occasions. Public lanes are perfect for open-play and walk-ins.
How much does laser tag cost at The BLVD! Bakersfield?
Laser tag starts at $10 / 1 game (Mon–Thu and Fri before 5pm). Multi-game passes save more: 2 games $14, 3 games $17. Fri after 5pm and weekends: $12 / 1 game, $17 / 2 games, $22 / 3 games. Valid waiver required — sign online.
