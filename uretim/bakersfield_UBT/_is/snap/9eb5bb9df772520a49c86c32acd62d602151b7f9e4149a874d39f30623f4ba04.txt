Hodel’s Country Dining is a locally owned and operated restaurant celebrating 50 years of tradition as Kern County’s best buffet. Hodel’s has an excellent variety of homemade salads and hot entrees prepared fresh daily. Whether you’re in a hurry or in need of rest and relaxation, Hodel’s charming atmosphere is sure to remind you of days gone by. We invite you to dine with us or to use our banquet and catering services for your next event.
We are conveniently located right off the 99 freeway. Take the Olive exit west and turn right at Knudsen Dr.
