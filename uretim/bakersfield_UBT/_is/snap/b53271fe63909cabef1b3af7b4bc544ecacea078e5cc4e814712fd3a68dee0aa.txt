What is WIC?
Women, Infants & Children
The Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC) provides nutrition education, breastfeeding support, and nutritious foods to supplement diets. WIC is a federally funded USDA nutrition program that is administered by the California Department of Public Health. Our WIC team includes registered dietitians, degreed nutritionists, International Board Certified Lactation Consultants (IBCLC), certified lactation educators, and other trained professionals. WIC services are provided at no cost. There are currently 16 CAPK-WIC sites, 3 facilities are located in San Bernardino County.

Who is eligible for WIC?
• Pregnant women
• Breatsfeeding women up to one year postpartum
• Non-breastfeeding women up to six months postpartum
• Women who had a pregnancy loss in the past six months
• Infants & children under five years old
• All foster children under five years old
• Fathers, grandparents, and caretakers may receive WIC food benefits for eligible infants & children
• Migrant & military families

Your WIC Journey
First Trimester
Sign up for a WIC card! Go grocery shopping after your first visit. WIC food benefits are added to your card monthly.
Second Trimester
Learn about breastfeeding and eating well for a healthy pregnancy.
Third Trimester
Get ready for birth and caring for your new baby.
Congratulations!
Your baby is here! Receive expert breastfeeding support from the start.
Baby's First Week
Talk to a WIC counselor and gain confidence in feeding your baby.
4, 6, and 9 Months Post Birth
Let’s talk about your baby’s eating and development.
Happy First Birthday!
Your baby is now a year of age! Check in with a WIC counselor about your child’s eating and growth.
They Grow So Quick!
Get WIC until your child turns 5! Meet with a Win counselor 2 or more times every year to learn about healthy foods and what your child needs for their age.
