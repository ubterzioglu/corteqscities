January 23 - March 7
The Bako Bandits
A thrilling tale of the old west about two bandits who are terrorizing Kern County. It appears that a mysterious man and woman robber team have been holding up banks in the region, always one step ahead of the law. And speaking of law, crooked sheriff Dastardly Drygulch is no better than the bank robbers since he is involved in every crooked deal in the territory. It's a case of one crook chasing two crooks! Meanwhile preacher Hugh Boring is looking for the robbers and at lovely saloon owner Libby Libation while erstwhile saloon lady Trixie La Rue Jones has her bloodshot eyes on everybody else, while wondering if anybody can bring bank robbers Donny Dynamite and lovely Marie Marie to justice?
March 20 - May 2
The Lady From Lancaster
Spam Diamond is a slightly shopworn private eye with his own odd code of ethics who always manages to do the right thing thanks to his faithful secretary Angel Eiffel. The Troll of Trafalgar is a treasure so rich it could, and has, turned good men bad. Now Noel Alexandria, a nasty gangster, and Gidget O'Malley, a beautiful and treacherous woman whose loyalties shift at the drop of a dime, are darkening Spam's door hot on the trail of the fabulous wealth that is The Troll. They will do anything to have The Troll as their own. Will Spam be able to save the day and his lovely secretary Angel before it's too late? The Lady from Lancaster is a silly, family-friendly take on classic film noir detective tales and sure to be a show you don't want to miss!!!
May 15 - June 27
Cruisin' Chester
The 1950's in Bakersfield, California were a different time. Fast cars and fun times ruled the main drag, Chester Avenue, and rock and roll was pumping across the airwaves on KAFY, The BIG 55! And no one was enjoying themselves more than The Jets and The Sweet Ladies, Bakersfield's coolest of cool cats and kittens. But when evil businessman, Jack Fleming, walks into their favorite hangout, Andre's, and threatens to buy it up and put in a dry cleaners to anticipate the onslaught of new development coming with the construction of Highway 58, the coolest kids in town will have to find a way to save their spot and their streets for all future Cruisers to come. Dust off your old leather jackets and get on your poodle skirts, because this is one journey the whole family will want to see. This 50's Jukebox Melodrama musical was written and directed by our very own, Michael Prince.
July 10 - September 5
Rosedale The Untold Story
Like Sands Through The Hourglass, So Are The Days of Our Lives... in ROSEDALE! The question on everyone's mind is "Who shot R.J. Brewing?", the patriarch of the biggest almond farming family in Rosedale. Could it be R.J.'s wife, Katheryn? Could it be Chastity Von Good-in-time, the local harlett? Or maybe it was Sir Allen Quartermain, the patriarch of the biggest dairy farming family outside of Shafter? Complicating matters even worse are the upcoming nuptials between the only children of the Brewing's and the Quartermain's, Daisy and Allen, Jr. Not to mention the fact that aunt Roseanne is six months pregnant and we still don't know who the father is. Nothing is as it seems and no one is as they appear in this hilarious send up of all things soap opera. Written and directed by our very own, Michael Prince, this brand new melodrama musical comedy is one the whole family will enjoy!
September 18 - November 14
A Funny Fright In The Night
Spunky Heroine Nancy Nightingale is out to prove herself in the world. Her plan is to take her deceased gangster uncle's abandoned roadhouse and turn it into a successful and family friendly establishment. Handsome real estate agent Chick Smith is there to help Nancy as much as he can while winning her heart. Will Chick and his bumbling assistant Wilbur be able to save Nancy from the clutches of her uncle's evil henchman Spider Moran? And what about the hidden treasure? Not to mention everyone pretending to be ghosts or zombies! Will somebody get rich, or will somebody get a… A Funny Fright In The Night?
