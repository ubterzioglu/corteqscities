Budget Billing
Keep your energy bills consistent all year round
Apply now to manage your energy costs with Budget Billing.
Enroll now
Program overview
Get more control of your bills
Budget Billing is a free program that helps you easily manage your monthly energy costs. We calculate your monthly payment amount based on your average energy costs over the past year and adjust your payment amount each month, so that you don't have big spikes on your bill.
While it's not a savings program, Budget Billing helps you stay in control of your bill by avoiding seasonal bill spikes.
Budget Billing Program
The Budget Billing program averages your energy costs over the previous year to determine your monthly payment amount. Each month, we recalculate your payment using a rolling average of your energy costs from the past 12 to 13 months.
During your time on Budget Billing, you will have an account balance that tracks any difference between your actual bill and your monthly payment amount. This balance is updated monthly so that you do not accumulate a huge balance that becomes due if you unenroll from Budget Billing. If you leave the program, any remaining balance will be applied to your final bill. Don't worry, on Budget Billing you only pay for what you use—nothing more, nothing less.
✓ Pro tip: To realize the full benefits of Budget Billing, stay enrolled in the program for at least a year.
Budget Billing is available to:
Residential customers who are not on a NEM rate plan
Small business customers on A1, A6, B1, B6 and GNR1 rate plans
How to qualify
Have a history of on-time, complete bill payments.
Not be on a payment plan or have a history of partial or late payments.
To stay enrolled in the program:
Pay the exact monthly Budget Billing amount. Underpayments may result in unenrollment.
Pay on time. Past due payments may result in unenrollment.
Note: Overpayments will be applied to your account balance but will not reduce your monthly Budget Billing amount.
Still have questions? Visit the Budget Billing frequently asked questions.
CCA, DA and CTA Customers
Are you a Community Choice Aggregator (CCA), Direct Access (DA) or Core Transport Agent (CTA) customer? PG&E's Budget Billing program may not apply to the electric generation or procurement portion of your energy costs. Please consult with your local CCA, CTA, or DA provider for more information.
How to enroll
Here are three ways to enroll:
Enroll through your online account
Currently available to residential customers only. Business customers see step 2 or step 3 below.
Follow these steps:
Sign into your account.
Go to the My Account dashboard
Click "All billing tasks."
Select "Budget Billing".
Enroll with the online enrollment form
Have your PG&E account number ready.
Complete the online enrollment form.
Submit your form to PG&E.
Enroll by phone
Call 1-877-660-6789.
Unenroll at any time
You may leave the program at any time. You'll also be unenrolled from Budget Billing if you:
Stop your PG&E service
Have past due payments
Switch to an ineligible rate plan (e.g., enroll in Net Energy Metering)
Leaving the Budget Billing program? Your remaining balance will be calculated and reflected on your next bill.
For more details, visit the frequently asked questions.

Frequently asked questions
Show all
Hide all
How is the Budget Billing amount calculated?
The Budget Billing amount is calculated by averaging your energy costs from the previous year to determine your monthly payment amount. Each month, we recalculate your bill using a 12 to 13 month rolling average of your energy costs. In addition, we apply 1/12 of any account balance or credit to your monthly bill.
Please note, your monthly Budget Billing amount may sometimes be based on a 13-month average instead of a 12-month average depending on the timing of your bill cycle.
I've lived at my current location for less than a year. Can I still enroll in Budget Billing?
We recommend 12 months of usage at your current address before enrolling in Budget Billing.
Why? If you don't have one year of usage history at your address, Budget Billing averages in the previous account holder's usage.
If the location is a new construction or new build, it has no usage history. You won't be able to enroll in Budget Billing until the location has 12 months of data.
When does Budget Billing take effect?
Budget Billing takes effect in the billing cycle following enrollment.
Does the Budget Billing amount ever increase or decrease?
Yes, your Budget Billing amount changes every month. We adjust your payment amount each month to make sure you're paying an average amount based on your past year of usage.
What happens if I pay more than my monthly Budget Billing amount?
There is no benefit to overpaying your monthly bill while enrolled in the Budget Billing program. If you pay more than your monthly payment amount, you won't see the credit reflected on your account until you unenroll from the program.
What happens if I pay less than my monthly Budget Billing amount?
If you miss two payments or underpay for two billing cycles, you:
Will be removed from Budget Billing
Will be required to pay off your entire balance
Won't be allowed to re-enroll in Budget Billing for six months
What happens to my Budget Billing if I transfer my service to a new address served by PG&E?
When you transfer your service to a new address served by PG&E, your Budget Billing also transfers.
However, your Budget Billing amount will need to be recalculated at the new service location. To do this, we will:
Look at the new location's usage history.
Adjust your monthly payment amount based on this history.
Did you stop service at your old address before starting your service at your new address?
You may end up being unenrolled from the Budget Billing program.
If this happens, your account balance may be reflected on your next energy statement.
Call 1-877-660-6789 to re-enroll in Budget Billing and transfer your account balance to your new service.
Can I enroll in other billing options if I'm on Budget Billing?
Yes. You may also enroll in:
Paperless billing
Automatic payments
Energy alerts
CARE
FERA
Medical Baseline
May I enroll in payment plans if I'm on Budget Billing?
No. If you are enrolled in a payment plan or have past due payments, you cannot enroll in Budget Billing.
Will I receive the California Climate Credits on Budget Billing?
Yes. However, you will not see it applied directly to your monthly Budget Billing amount. The credits will be applied to your account balance.
What happens if I decide to leave the Budget Billing program?
The program remains in effect unless you decide to cancel or default on your payments. You can remove your account from the program at any time. Access your account online or call 1-877-660-6789.
Note: If you cancel the Budget Billing program while you have a payoff balance, the amount you owed or credited will be applied to your next month's bill.
Detailed breakdown of your bill
Descriptions of each section are listed below.
Account No: Your account number is a 10-digit number. We use this number to identify your account if you call us about your energy use. Your account number and due date are at the top of every page of your statement. You receive a separate monthly energy statement for each active account.
Service For: This is the address where your charges were incurred. Some customers receive PG&E service at multiple locations. This section indicates which property incurred the charges.
Budget Billing Summary: Your 'Budget Billing Amount This Period' is based on average energy charges over the past year. Although a more detailed breakdown of your usage and payments is available on your energy statement, you're required to pay this amount.
Questions About Your Bill?: Contact us with questions using the information found on the first page of the statement.
Total Amount Due: This shows the amount you need to pay for that billing period. This charge includes your calculated Budget Billing monthly payment amount and any generation or procurement charges for Community Choice Aggregator (CCA), Direct Access (DA) or Core Transport Agent (CTA) customers. The notes below reflect other information about your account and any special programs you participate in.
Savings Alert: We use this space for notes about your account, including any special programs that might affect your total charges.
How Budget Billing Affects Your Energy Payments: This chart helps you identify any trends in your monthly energy usages over the past year. Use the data to understand how Budget Billing helps equalize your payment across different seasons.
Your Account Details: The information in this section helps explain how your payments and energy usage are currently affecting your account balance.
Account Balance from Previous Statement: This shows your total account balance, the amount that you owed (positive value) or the amount you were credited (negative value) before you made a payment per your previous bill.
Payment(s) Received Since Last Statement: This shows the payment from your previous bill. Payments made to PG&E are shown as credits—hence, the negative value.
Account Balance Before Current Charges: This shows the total account balance after your payment for your previous bill. It's the sum of your a) Account Balance from Previous Statement; and b) Payment(s) Received Since Last Statement.
Current Electric Charges: This is the actual cost related to your electricity usage in the last billing period. It's the amount you'd be required to pay for your electricity usage if you were NOT enrolled in Budget Billing.
Current Gas Charges: This is the actual cost related to your gas usage in the last billing period.
Total Current Account Balance: This shows your current account balance at this billing period. This is the accumulated differences between your monthly payment amounts and actual usage costs during your enrollment in Budget Billing. The amount you credit/owe would be reflected on your bill if you voluntarily unenroll or are removed from the Budget Billing program.
Important Messages: We use this space to share timely information—from summer safety tips to regulatory updates.
Payment Stub: Return this form to the address indicated with your payment. The remittance stub indicates your account number, bill due date and total amount due. For your convenience, we have included a windowed return envelope. Please place the remittance stub with the PG&E address visible in the window. The back of the remittance stub includes an area for you to update your account information, as well as an overview of payment options.
