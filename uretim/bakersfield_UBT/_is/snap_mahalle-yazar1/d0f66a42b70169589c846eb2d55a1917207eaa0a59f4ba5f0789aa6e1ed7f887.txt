North Meadows Park
3300 McCray Street
Bakersfield, CA 93308
North Meadows Park is located in the Oildale Area of Bakersfield.
The park is 8.8 acres and includes the following amenities:
Open Play Area
1 Backstop
Children’s play area
Water play feature
Group picnic shelter
Family picnic pads
Basketball court
Restrooms
Multi-use playfields
Turf volleyball
Directions to the park:
North Meadows Park is at 3300 McCray Street. From North Chester Avenue, take West Day Avenue west. The park is on the northeast corner of West Day Avenue and McCray Street.
Hours of Operation & Facility Closures
Community Center & Business Office Hours:
Monday – Friday, 8am - 5pm Closed weekends and holidays.
Park Hours:
Open 7 days a week, 365 days a year from 6am - 10pm.
Reservations
Certain facilities supervised and operated by NOR are available on a reservation basis to groups and invididuals for social, cultural, or recreational activities.
