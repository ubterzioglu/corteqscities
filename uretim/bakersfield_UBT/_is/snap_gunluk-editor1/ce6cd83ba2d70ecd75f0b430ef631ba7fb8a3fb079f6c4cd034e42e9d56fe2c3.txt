Our Services to the Community
​Free One-to-One or Small Group Tutoring

Community Collaborations
​Adult & Child Basic Education
Help Kern County residents with reading, writing, and math skills necessary to function in society.
English as a Second Language
Learn to read, write, and speak English from beginning to advanced levels.
Citizenship Test Preparation
Provide assistance to individuals working to pass their United States Citizenship Test. Classes meet every Tuesday from 9-11 at KLC main office and Wednesday from 5-6 pm at Shafter Youth Center.
GED Preparation
Tutor people in a variety of subjects to help them pass their GED, or high school equivalency test.
Free Community Tutoring Services
Basic reading, writing, and math skills, preparing for the U.S. citizenship test, GED, etc. tutors are here to guide learners of all ages. Our community members can now receive free tutoring from trained tutors both in-person at community resource centers countywide and online.
