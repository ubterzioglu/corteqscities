Medi-Cal for Children
Many children in California qualify for free or low-cost health insurance through Medi-Cal. In certain counties, they could be eligible for CCHIP. This guide helps parents and caregivers understand children’s insurance options, including who qualifies, what's covered, what it costs and how to apply.
Insurance Programs for Children
Children may qualify for one of two programs: Medi-Cal for Children or County Children’s Health Initiative Program (CCHIP).
Does my child qualify?Compare Medi-Cal and CCHIPCovered Benefits for Children
When you apply through Covered California, your child may qualify for a different program than you do.
If you decline insurance for your child, they can’t get financial help for a plan through Covered California. This means they would need to be enrolled in a full-cost plan.
Which program your child gets depends on:
your child’s age
your household income
where you live
your child’s immigration or citizenship status
How to Apply
Applying through Covered California is free. One application checks what each family member qualifies for, including Medi-Cal and CCHIP for children.
1
Gather basic information.
Have your household size, household income and each family member's information ready before you start.
2
Check your eligibility.
You can use the Shop and Compare Tool to see what programs your family may qualify for, or start your application today!
3
Review your results.
The application will show the program each family member qualifies for, including any family members who qualify for Covered California, Medi-Cal or CCHIP.
4
Follow the next steps.
For Medi-Cal or CCHIP, their office will contact you with enrollment information and any next steps. For Covered California, you will be able to choose a plan online and pay your first bill.
Need help or have questions? You can get free help from a certified enroller.
