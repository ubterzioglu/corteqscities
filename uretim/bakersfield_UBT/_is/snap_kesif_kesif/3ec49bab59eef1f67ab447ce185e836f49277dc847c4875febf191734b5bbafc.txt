Lori Brock Discovery Center
Admission to the Lori Brock Discovery Center is included with Kern County Museum general admission.
The Discovery Center provides a special place for children to learn through play using open-ended tools. Imagination, Exploration, and discovery are encouraged thru our Steam Town. Learn about different types of energy in the Power Lab. Let your creativity flow with our daily craft in Art Express. Wonderful things in Lori Brock.

A Place for children to Learn Through Play
The Lori Brock Discovery Center is a place for children of all ages to learn, explore, and have fun!
Within the doors of Lori Brock you’ll find The Makers Annex and STEAM Town where kiddos can use their imagination and learn through creative play.
