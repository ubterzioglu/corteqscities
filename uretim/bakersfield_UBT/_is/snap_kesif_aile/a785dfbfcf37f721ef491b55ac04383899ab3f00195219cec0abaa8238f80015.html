Incoming Freshmen Information

WELCOME FRESHMEN CLASS OF 2030!
 
 
Welcome future Drillers!  We can't wait to meet you at orientation and registration in March 2027! Please take some time to preview our various elective programs by clicking on the links in the "Electives and More" table below. Our ultimate goal is to get you connected to the programs you enjoy most!  
 
Being a Driller means that you are now a part of a legacy that is enriched in pride, excellence, and integrity. You will have access to some of the greatest teachers, staff members, and opportunities that any school can offer to help you fulfill your goals. Remember, ONCE A DRILLER, ALWAYS A DRILLER! 
 
For registration questions, please contact the BHS main office at (661) 324-9841 during normal business hours, Monday-Friday, 7:30am-4:30pm. 
 
If you would like to contact one of our counselors, please click here to visit our Counseling page for email contact information.

INCOMING FRESHMEN REGISTRATION INFORMATION:

 

STEP 1: "DRILLERPALOOZA!" Attend the Incoming 9th Grade Orientation night on Monday, March 16, 2026. Registration packets will be handed out at this event.

 

STEP 2: Gather necessary registration documents. To enroll your student, you will be asked to provide ONE COPY of each of the following:

  •     PARENTS/GUARDIANS, YOU MUST PROVIDE A COPY OF YOUR PHOTO ID TO ENROLL YOUR STUDENT—NO EXCEPTIONS
  •     Current PG&E, So Cal Gas, or water bill (in Parent/Guardian(s) name) for proof of residence*
  •     Student’s Immunization records, including TDap (TB Test also REQUIRED when entering from outside state/country)
  •     Student’s Birth Certificate
  •     If applicable, legal custody/guardianship documents
 
*RESIDENCE REQUIREMENT: In order to be compliant with KHSD guidelines, proof of residence will be required in order to register for Bakersfield High School.  Without this verification, your student’s registration will be delayed until we receive the needed information. If you have any questions or concerns, please call 661-324-9841.*

 

 

STEP 3: Register your student. Please complete your registration packet and have copies of all required documentation listed above. Please bring your registration materials and copies with you when you register your student. We have two days of on-campus registration on March 23rd & 24th from 4:00-6:30pm. BHS personnel will be on hand to make copies of necessary registration documents if needed.

BHS ATHLETICS INFO
 
***PLEASE NOTE: Incoming freshmen must be enrolled before they can begin the process of getting "cleared" to participate in athletics. 
 
BHS is home to an incredible athletics program!  We encourage all incoming freshmen to choose a sport that interests them and get more involved! 
 
In order to participate in high school athletics, incoming freshmen must obtain a physical from a doctor dated after April 1st.  After you have enrolled at Bakersfield High School and completed your physical, you must then create an online Sportsnet account where you will upload your completed physical form. 
 
Click here to create your online Sportsnet account.
(Remember--your student must be fully enrolled before you can create an account.)
 
Once you are cleared by the BHS Athletics office, and both your Sportsnet account and completed physical are confirmed, you can being practice.  Sportsnet Sign-up instructions and the physical form are included below.  Contact the coach for specific information about practice schedules.
 
For questions regarding BHS Athletics, please contact BHS Athletic Director, Matt Ornelaz at: [email protected].

Footer Statement

The Kern High School District (KHSD) is committed to ensuring equal, fair, and meaningful access to employment and education services. KHSD prohibits discrimination, harassment (including sexual harassment), intimidation, or bullying in any employment practice, education program, or educational activity on the basis and/or association with a person or group with one or more of these actual or perceived characteristics of age, ancestry, color, ethnic group identification, gender, gender identity or expression, genetic information, immigration status, marital status, medical information, national origin, physical or mental disability, parental status, political affiliation, pregnancy and related conditions, race, religion, retaliation, sex, sexual orientation, military or veterans status, homelessness, foster status, affiliation with the Boy Scouts of America and other designated youth groups, or any other basis prohibited by California state and federal nondiscrimination laws consistent with Education Code 200 and 220, Government Code 11135, and Title IX.
