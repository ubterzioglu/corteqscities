Instalaciones Culturales
Level 1 Is the first step for every person who wants to learn Spanish. It is designed for those who have never studied the language or who have a very basic understanding of it. In this level, you will learn to use basic expressions and ask questions and a lot of helpful vocabulary. You will also learn how to conjugate verbs in present, present progressive and future tenses. At the end of this level, you will feel more confident about your communicative abilities in Spanish and more motivated to continue advancing in your learning.
Level 2 Will allow you to go beyond the basic level. You will continue to learn new vocabulary and acquire grammar skills that will help you communicate in different day-to-day situations.
You will also learn to communicate in simple sentences, using Spanish expressions in the present, past and future tenses.
Level 3 If you are already experienced in Spanish, this level will allow you to strengthen your ability to describe things and situations in the present, future and all past tenses, develop your writing skills, and improve your coherence.
You will gain more confidence and fluency in Spanish and will feel like you can communicate verbally with greater ease.
