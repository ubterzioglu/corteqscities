BIOPARC Fuengirola closing times
Important information:
On the first Friday in June of each year, the park closes at 17:00 p.m. and last access at 16:00 p.m.
On February 5th, closing time is 16:30 pm and last entry is at 15:30 pm.
On December 24th it closes at 15:00 p.m. and last access at 14:00 p.m.
On December 31th it closes at 15:00 p.m. and last access at 14:00 p.m.
TICKET OFFICE: every day of the year it closes one hour before the park. Both for daily entry and for annual pass B! Access will not be possible after hours.
