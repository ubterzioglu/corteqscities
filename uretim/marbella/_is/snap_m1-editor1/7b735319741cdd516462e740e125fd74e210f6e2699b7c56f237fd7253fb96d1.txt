Walk across our bridges
During your expedition, enjoy the spectacular suspension bridges similar to those that span the rivers and lakes of the legendary African territories and the wooden brigde over the elephant territory with views to the Lakes Reserve. Not to be missed!
Discover our bridges!
* Important information
The tour of the bridges is open during opening hours, unless weather, security or operational conditions do not allow it. For operational reasons, the viewpoints of the Lakes Reserve and the Central Village (upper area of the park) close one hour before the rest of the park, while the Portico de la Naturaleza can be visited until closing time. Selwo Aventura reserves the right to modify or suspend its opening hours and certain services or activities due to weather or operational reasons.
