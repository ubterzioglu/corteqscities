Maestranza Theatre

Maestranza Theatre. Seville © Guillermo Mendo

Seville

Sevilla
  1. Home
  2. Art and culture
Seville

One of Andalusia’s great temples of music and the performing arts. Its carefully curated programme brings together top-class productions in a venue renowned for the excellent quality of its acoustics, making it an essential cultural landmark in Seville.

Situated on the banks of the River Guadalquivir and next to the bullring, with which it shares its name, this avant-garde circular building hosts operas, symphonies and dance performances. It is also home to the Royal Symphony Orchestra of Seville (ROSS), and one of the regular venues for the famous Flamenco Biennial.Since its opening in 1991, its stage has hosted masterful performances of Carmen, The Barber of Seville, Aida and other classic operas, and great figures of bel canto such as Luciano Pavarotti, Montserrat Caballé, Plácido Domingo and Cecilia Bartoli.
