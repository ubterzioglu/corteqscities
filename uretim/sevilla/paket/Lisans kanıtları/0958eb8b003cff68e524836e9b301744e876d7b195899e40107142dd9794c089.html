On the occasion of the International Congress of Brotherhoods and Popular Piety:

  • From December 4 to 7, the cultural visit will only include the ascent to the Giralda and the visit to the Fons Pietatis Exhibition.
  • Online access to the visit will be through the Puerta del Perdón (Gate of Forgiveness), and the ticket office will be located at the Puerta del Lagarto (Alligator Gate).
  • The cultural visit is suspended on December 8.
