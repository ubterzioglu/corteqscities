Practical information

Everything you need to know before visiting GLOW

Light art in Eindhoven & region

GLOW Festival is a celebration for the senses and the imagination. The upcoming edition, CONNECT, will take place from 7 to 14 November 2026. This free festival will once again light up the heart of Eindhoven, as well as Helmond, Oirschot, Best, Veldhoven and Lieshout. Visitors can explore a walking route filled with inspiring light artworks, specially created for and by the region by international and local artists, emerging talents, children and residents.

Plan your visit to GLOW Festival and discover everything you need to know for the ultimate GLOW experience!

For more information about GLOW in the region, please visit glowinderegio.nl

Opening times
7 t/m 14 NOV 2026

The entire region will light up from Saturday, 7 November through Saturday, 14 November 2026.
Opening hours may vary by region.

  • Eindhoven: from 18:30 to 23:00

  • Lieshout: from 18:30 to 22:00
  • Helmond: from 18:30 to 22:00

Route

This year’s GLOW Festival route guides hundreds of thousands of visitors along an approximately 5 km walk through the city centre, featuring around 30 light artworks.

With the interactive GLOW map, available online before the festival, you can easily track your location on your phone (when location services are enabled), follow the route, and discover detailed information about the artworks, restaurants and other facilities along the way.

Prefer a printed version? A physical map of Eindhoven is available during GLOW for €2 at an information container along the route (at Central Station and Stadhuisplein). At Tourist Information (VVV) in De Heuvel, the map is also available in advance!

Travel Information

Plan ahead for the crowds around GLOW. The easiest and most environmentally friendly ways to get to GLOW Eindhoven are by public transport or bicycle. This will help you avoid traffic congestion and disruption as much as possible, while also being better for the environment.

Road closures

Every day, several roads in the city centre will be closed to through traffic between 6:00 pm and midnight. See the Car section below for details of the road closures.

Residents

If you live directly along the GLOW route, you will receive a letter from GLOW with information on the best way to access your home during the event. Read more information in the Q&A nelow. More questions? Email info@gloweindhoven.nl.

Find all information about accessibility and transport below. 

Train

Are you coming by train? Good choice! Eindhoven has two stations: Eindhoven Centraal and Strijp-S Station. This year the GLOW route is near both stations. Check ns.nl  for the latest travel information.

Tip: You no longer need to buy a separate ticket for public transport. With OVpay, you can easily check in and out using your debit card, credit card or mobile phone on trains, buses, trams and the metro. Alternatively, you can apply for the new OV-pas travel card via ovpay.nl.

FAQ
General

When does GLOW start and finish?

You can visit all the light artworks in the city center of Eindhoven from Saturday, November 7th, up to and including Saturday, November 14th, 2026, between 6:30 PM and 11:00 PM. The routes in the municipalities Helmond, Best, Oirschot, Veldhoven and Lieshout can be visited from Saturday, November 7th, up to and including Saturday, November 14th, 2026, between 6:30 PM and 10:00 PM.

Do I need a ticket for GLOW?

No. GLOW is a free light art festival in the public area. The city will be illuminated with large and small light art projects that are linked together along a walking route through the city. The artworks are created by national and international light artists and for everyone to visit. You do need a ticket for the unique drone experience at the Philips Stadium in Eindhoven. You can reserve yours via this link.

What time does GLOW start?

You can visit all the light artworks in the city center of Eindhoven between 6:30 PM and 11:00 PM. The projects in Helmond, Oirschot, Best, Veldhoven & Lieshout can be visited between 6:30 PM and 10:00 PM.

Where can I find the map/route for GLOW?

The interactive digital map of Eindhoven can be found here.

The physical map is available at the following information points during GLOW:

  • Info container near DOMUSDELA. Open from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
  • Info container at Stadhuisplein. Open from 6:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
  • This is Eindoven Store in the Heuvel. Check their website for opening hours.

 

Can I book a GLOW tour?

Yes, guided tours are also available. These can be done with a guide or as a self-guided culinary tour. For more information about the tours, check here.

How long is the GLOW route?

The light artworks are connected by a walking route through the city. The route is around five kilometres long and you can join anywhere on the route.

Are there public toilets available?

Public toilets (also accessible for disabled people) can be found in the following locations:

  • Philips Stadion (when you have a ticket for the drone experience)
  • Public toilet at Heuvel
  • Or go to one of the GLOW Hosts, see digital map for the locations.
Can I visit GLOW if I’m sensitive to epilepsy?

If you are sensitive to epilepsy, we advise against visiting GLOW. The light artworks regularly feature flickering lights, bright flashes and changing colour patterns. These can trigger an epileptic seizure in people who are sensitive to them. Read more about photosensitivity on epilepsie.nl.

Accessibility

Can I follow the GLOW route by bike or scooter?

The route of GLOW follows the public road as much as possible. We recommend to walk the route, it’s only 5 kilometers. If you do come by bike, take the traffic around you into account.

Is GLOW accessible for wheelchairs?

The GLOW route is fully wheelchair accessible. For more information on (disabled) parking spaces, restrooms, water refill stations, rest areas, and shelters, please refer to the Accessibility Map from the Municipality of Eindhoven.

Stakeholders/residents

I live on the GLOW route and my road is closed, where can I find more information?

Several roads in the city centre will be closed for traffic daily from 18:00 to 00:00. Residents living in the direct vicinity of the event area will receive a letter from GLOW with information on the best way to access your home. Questions? Send an e-mail to info@gloweindhoven.nl

Can I set up a food truck of other point of sale along the GLOW route?

No, this is not permitted. Unless you are an official GLOW Host. 

Can I play/perform music along the GLOW route?

No, this is not permitted.

Can I contribute to GLOW as an entrepreneur?

Yes you can! Visit our partnerships page.

Press information

GLOW has international allure, and that’s something worth writing about. It’s an excellent opportunity for the press to view and discover new artists and light art projects. Members of the press are also welcome at GLOW for reports and interviews.

Questions from the press can be submitted by sending an e-mail to  pers@gloweindhoven.nl, stating which press medium you work for and how you would like to highlight GLOW. Looking for press material? Visit  this page  for press images.
