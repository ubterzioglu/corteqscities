Philips van Lennep Park
The Philips van Lennep Park is a large park in the north of Eindhoven. It's located on the western edge of the city and has several facilities.
15 min. bike
The Philips van Lennep Park is relatively young. It was donated to Eindhoven in 1964. The park is located near the highway. People come to this park to barbeque, play football, tennis or go for a walk.
