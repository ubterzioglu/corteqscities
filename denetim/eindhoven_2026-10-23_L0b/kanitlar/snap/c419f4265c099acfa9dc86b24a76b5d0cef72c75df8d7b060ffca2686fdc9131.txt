Frequently asked questions
Get answers to our most commonly asked questions about Dutch Design Week.
Dates en programme
When does Dutch Design Week (DDW) take place?
DDW takes place from Saturday 17 to Sunday 25 October 2026.
Where can I find the DDW programme?
View the full programme, including all projects and participating designers, here on the programme page.
Is there a special programme for children and young people?
Yes. DDW Young brings together projects from the programme that are particularly interesting for children and young people aged 6 to 18. View the DDW Young & Education programme here.
Can I participate in DDW26?
The call for entries for the upcoming edition of DDW is currently closed.
Locations and Opening Hours
Where does Dutch Design Week take place?
DDW takes place at dozens of locations across Eindhoven. Check the map to see which exhibitions, projects and activities can be found at each location.
What are the opening hours of DDW locations?
Most locations are open daily from 11:00 to 18:00. Some locations have different opening hours, so please check the latest information in the programme before your visit.
Please note: on the first Saturday, the Design Academy Eindhoven Graduation Show opens at 13:00.
Can I leave a location and return later?
Yes. As long as you are wearing a valid DDW wristband, you can leave and return to ticketed locations during opening hours. Please keep in mind that locations can be busy and you may need to queue again.
Please note: Your wristband is valid once it has been secured around your wrist. If you remove or cut it off yourself, it will no longer be valid. You cannot collect a wristband on behalf of someone else.
Are cloakrooms or lockers available?
No, most DDW locations do not have cloakrooms or lockers.

Is there a reduced rate for visitors on a limited budget?
Dutch Design Foundation (DDF) works with Cultuur Inclusief to make DDW accessible to more people. Each year, 500 tickets are available through Cultuur Inclusief for €2.50 for people who are unable to pay the regular ticket price. Would you like to purchase one of these tickets? Please note: Cultuur Inclusief manages a limited number of discount vouchers. Their website explains exactly who is eligible and how to apply. Dutch Design Foundation provides the discount but has no influence over the selection process or allocation of tickets.
Reachability
How do I get to Dutch Design Week?
Find all information about travelling to Eindhoven and getting between the different DDW locations here.
How do I get there by bicycle?
Cycling to Dutch Design Week is a great idea! Eindhoven has numerous bike paths that connect locations, and you can find free, and in some cases even supervised, bike parking.
How do I get there by train?
If you travel to Dutch Design Week by train, you will arrive right in the heart of the design world. The intercity will take you to Eindhoven Central Station in the city centre. Local trains also stop at Strijp-S Station. Check the Journey Planner on ns.nl or the Reisplanner Xtra app from 10 days before your departure.
NS Combi-Ticket
This year, you can again use the NS Combi-Ticket. Are you coming to Dutch Design Week by train? Purchase your ticket through NS Spoordeelwinkel. If you are travelling by train from the Eindhoven region, check the special regional fare applicable to the NS Combi-Ticket.
Read more about the NS Combi-Ticket →
How do I get there by car?
You can reach Eindhoven by car from Amsterdam/Utrecht via the A2, from Rotterdam/Tilburg via the A58, from Antwerp via the A67, and from Arnhem/Nijmegen via the A50.

P+R
There are several parking garages at hotspots like the city centre and Strijp-S, but be aware that it can be busy. A good idea is to park at the various Park + Ride (P+R) locations: park on the outskirts of the city and take a direct bus to Dutch Design Week. There are two options:
P+R Meerhoven, on Noord-Brabantlaan
Bus connection to Strijp-S and Stationsplein locations
P+R Genneper Parken, on Aalsterweg
Bus connection to Stationsplein
Return bus tickets cost €0.50 per person.
How do I get there with coaching and busses?
Large groups coming to Dutch Design Week by coach can use the Kiss & Rides.
There are two clearly marked Kiss & Ride locations, indicated by Dutch Design Week signs. One is at the Flixbus stop opposite the yellow bowling pins on John F. Kennedylaan, and the second is on Achtseweg Zuid, on the other side of Beukenlaan, in Eindhoven.
At the Kiss & Ride location at Achtseweg Zuid, the bus can temporarily park near Strijp-S Station for everyone to disembark. However, we urge you not to leave the bus there. There is ample free parking for coaches further along Achtseweg Zuid.

Frequently asked questions about Design Tours
Do you have a question about the Design Tours?
Find the answer in the frequently asked questions below or contact us at [email protected]
Where do the tours start?
The tours start at the DDW Business Lounge in Area51 on Ketelhuisplein. Mission Tours may also start at Fuutlaan. You will receive the exact starting location with your booking.
Until when can I book a tour?
Custom Tours and Strijp-S Bites & Insights can be booked until Friday 2 October inclusive. All other Design Tours can be booked until 19 October inclusive.
How do I book a Design Tour?
Via the Design Tours booking system:
Step 1
Choose one of the Design Tours. More information about the different tours can be found here.
Step 2
Choose an available date and time slot and select the number of participants.
Step 3
A maximum of 15 people can join each tour. Are you booking for more than 15 people? You will automatically book multiple tours.
This is how it works in the booking system:
Enter the total number of participants in the form.
The system will show how many tours you need to accommodate everyone, initially within the same time slot.
Step 4
Select the language in which you would like to take the tour: Dutch or English.
Would you prefer another language? Email [email protected] and we will see whether we can accommodate your request.
Step 5
Add the tour booking to your basket.
Step 6
Check the summary of your selected tour. Is everything correct? Continue to the booking details.
Step 7
To prepare the quotation, we will ask for your contact details and reference number. You can also email the reference number to us later.
Please note: make sure you enter the correct details in this form. These details will appear on the quotation and invoice.
Step 8
Check the complete summary of your booking and confirm your booking.
Step 9
You will receive a quotation from us. You then have 14 days to accept it. The quotation includes a payment link. You will receive the invoice afterwards.
Are you booking a Custom Tour? Once you have accepted the quotation, we will ask about your organisation’s areas of interest. Our team will use this information to create a tailored tour.
How much does a Design Tour cost?
The price of each tour is listed alongside its description. When booking a tour, you also book admission tickets for Dutch Design Week (DDW). The cost is calculated based on the number of participants. These tickets are valid not only during the tour but throughout DDW, from Saturday 17 to Sunday 25 October.
Bicycle hire is charged separately for Mission Tours. For Strijp-S Bites & Insights, additional costs per person apply for coffee and tea, lunch and drinks.
All prices shown exclude VAT. When you make a booking, you will receive a price overview that includes VAT.
How many people can join a tour?
A maximum of 15 people can join each tour. Are you booking for more than 15 people? You will need to book multiple tours.
This is how it works in the booking system:
Enter the total number of participants in the form.
The system will show how many tours you need to accommodate everyone, initially within the same time slot.
If that time slot is fully booked, the system will search for other available time slots for the remaining tours.

Custom Tours
Custom Tours are tailored to your organisation’s areas of interest and vary in length from 1.5 to 3 hours.
Have you already booked a tour, but would more people like to join?
Additional participants can join, up to a maximum of 15 people per tour.
To add participants, purchase additional standard Dutch Design Week (DDW) tickets through the ticket shop. Make sure these tickets have been exchanged for DDW wristbands before the tour begins.
Please note: you must inform the Design Tours team about any additional participants so that sufficient technical equipment is available when the tour begins. Email [email protected].
Additional participants can be registered until 14 October.
I booked a tour for more people than will be attending. Can I return the unused tickets?
Unfortunately, tickets are non-refundable. Dutch Design Week runs for nine days and the tickets are transferable, so why not make a colleague, friend or family member happy with the remaining tickets?
I have booked a tour but would like to add one or more bicycles to my booking. Is that possible?
Unfortunately, we cannot arrange this. However, bicycles can be hired at several locations during Dutch Design Week (DDW). You can also hire an OV-fiets from Eindhoven Central Station or Strijp-S station.
I would like to book more than 10 tours. Can you help me?
We would be happy to discuss the options with you. Contact us at [email protected].
When do the tours take place?
The tours take place from Saturday 17 to Saturday 24 October inclusive.
Design Tours can be booked in time slots, with a new slot available every half hour. A limited number of tours is available per time slot.
If no tours are available in a particular time slot, the maximum number of tours has already been booked for that time. No additional tours can depart due to the availability of the Design Guides.
How long does a tour last?
The tours last between 1.5 and 4 hours.
In which languages can I book a tour?
Tours are generally offered in Dutch or English. If you would prefer another language, please contact us at [email protected]. We will see whether it is possible to offer the tour in your preferred language.
Some members of my tour group have limited mobility. What should I do?
You can let us know when booking your tour. We will then contact you to ensure the tour is accessible for everyone.
Did you not mention this when booking? Email us at [email protected] to let us know.
Why don’t I receive the Early Bird discount?
Early Bird tickets are intended for individual visitors. Tickets purchased by invoice receive a volume discount instead of the Early Bird discount.
Can I use the hired bicycle for the entire day of the tour?
Unfortunately not. The bicycle is reserved specifically for you and your group for the duration of the Design Tour.
Other questions
Where can I find first aid?
During Dutch Design Week, there is a First Aid post in the Klokgebouw.
For all other locations, the staff/organisation of the respective location should be informed. They can handle simple First Aid cases themselves or refer you to the right person.
In case of an emergency, always contact 112.
Where can I go for lost property?
Found items are kept at the information desk in Klokgebouw. If you have lost something that has not yet been found, please email [email protected] with a clear description of the item.
If your item is found during or after DDW and your contact details are included with it, we will contact you. After DDW, lost property is kept for a maximum of one month.
Are assistance dogs allowed?
Dutch Design Week is fully accessible to assistance dogs. The dog must wear a harness that clearly indicates it is an assistance dog.
Is there a breastfeeding room?
The Klokgebouw has a breastfeeding room. If you'd like to use it, please visit the information desk in the entrance hall (Klokgebouw 50).
What can I do if I cannot wear my wristband for all nine days?
If you are unable to wear your wristband throughout DDW because of work, a hospital stay or another important reason, you can request a personal pass at the service desk in Klokgebouw or at Eindhoven Central Station. This pass will replace your wristband for the remainder of DDW.
Please note: You can only request a pass while your wristband is still secured around your wrist. First exchange your e-ticket for a wristband and then submit your request. Always have your wristband removed by a member of the service team. If you cut it off yourself, it will no longer be valid and a personal pass cannot be issued.
Requests for a personal pass are assessed on a case-by-case basis. Unfortunately, we cannot accommodate every request; the decision of the service team is final.
