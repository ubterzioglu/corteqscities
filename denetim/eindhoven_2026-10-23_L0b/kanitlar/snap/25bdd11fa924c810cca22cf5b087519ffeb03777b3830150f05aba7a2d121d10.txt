Hovenring
The Hovenring is a bicycle roundabout suspended by cables from a pylon above a crossing in one of the connecting roads between Eindhoven and Veldhoven.
20 min. public transport
The Hovenring is a bicycle roundabout suspended by cables from a pylon above a crossing in one of the connecting roads between Eindhoven en Veldhoven. Due to this special, exclusive bicycle roundabut, cyclists and pedestrians can travel unhindered by car traffic from Eindhoven to Veldhoven or from Meerhoven to De Hurk or in any other direction.
