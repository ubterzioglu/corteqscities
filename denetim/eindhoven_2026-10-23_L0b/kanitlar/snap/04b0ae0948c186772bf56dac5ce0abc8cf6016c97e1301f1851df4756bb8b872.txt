Directions
​
By train
Eindhoven Central Station is the nearest train station. From there we are 10 minutes by bike, 15 minutes by bus or a 30 minute walk.
By bike
A 10 minute cycle from the centre of Eindhoven. Please park your bike in front of Koelhuis off the street.
By bus
From the centre of Eindhoven, take a 10 minute bus ride on bus 24 or 320 (Bus to Helmond Station). Get off the bus at Otto Veniusweg bus stop – we’re a short walk from there.
By car
We are located next to the east side of the Eindhoven ring road.
You can park your car free of charge in the area on the street.
