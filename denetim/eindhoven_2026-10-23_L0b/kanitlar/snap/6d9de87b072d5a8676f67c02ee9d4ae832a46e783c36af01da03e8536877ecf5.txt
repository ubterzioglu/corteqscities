Varying fares
The boarding fare for the bus is usually €4. For certain longer lines this is more. Here are the exceptions for Hermes buses. Please note! With an increased boarding fare of, say, €8.00, you need to have a balance of at least €4.00 on the card before checking in on these lines.
The boarding fares apply if you do not check out. If this needs to be corrected, the correct amount can be adjusted at uitcheckgemist.nl (for OV-chipkaart) or OVpay.nl (for debit card/smartphone).

Kilometre fare
If you travel with an OV-chipkaart, you pay a basic fare and a fare per kilometre.The fare for Hermes buses is € 0.202 per kilometre.
Please note: If you travel during off-peak hours with a Dalkorting Noord Brabant subscription on the lines where the increased boarding fare applies, you simply pay a boarding fare of € 4.00.
