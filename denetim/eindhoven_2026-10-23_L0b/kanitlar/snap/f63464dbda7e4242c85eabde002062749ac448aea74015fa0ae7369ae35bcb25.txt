How to get there?
Van Abbemuseum
Stratumsedijk 2
5611 ND Eindhoven
t: +31 40 238 1000
e: info@vanabbemuseum.nl
By bike
There is plenty of space to park your bicycle next to the main entrance at Stratumsedijk. Bicycle parking Heuvel is the closest guarded parking and is a 10-minute walk from the museum. The Van Abbemuseum is connected to the bicycle junction network; bicycle junction 89.
By car
Eindhoven can be reached via the A2, the A50 and the A67. Regardless of which direction you come from, drive in the direction of the city centre. Follow the signs to one of the parking garages in the centre, all within 5 minutes walking distance of the Van Abbemuseum: Q-Park parking Stadhuisplein, Parking Stadskantoor or Q-Park parking Heuvel. Traveling from the south? Park inexpensively at P+R Genneperparken and travel from there by bus, shared bicycle or scooter to the city centre. Information about all public parking places in Eindhoven, the parking fees and opening times can be found on the website of the Eindhoven Municipality.
By train
Every half hour trains from Amsterdam, Utrecht, Den Haag, Rotterdam, Venlo, Heerlen and Maastricht arrive in Eindhoven. Please check schedules via www.ns.nl.
City transport: bus & train
The bus station is located on the north side of the NS railway station Eindhoven Central Station. Several buses stop near the museum, such as stop Bilderdijklaan (1 minute walk), Smalle Haven (3 minutes walk), Stadhuisplein (4 minutes walk), PC Hooftlaan (4 minutes walk) or Keizersgracht (9 minutes walk) to our main entrance at Stratumsedijk 2. Check 9292.nl for the latest travel information.
Wheelchairs & walkers
The Van Abbemuseum is well accessible with wheelchairs, mobility scooters and walkers. The museum has two wheelchairs for visitor use. You can reserve the wheelchairs by sending an email to kassabalie@vanabbemuseum.nl
House rules
Works of art are fragile. To prevent damage, we ask everyone not to bring their coats, bags larger than A3 size, backpacks and umbrellas with them to the museum. A limited number of free lockers are available for storing items. The management of the museum is not liable for loss, damage or theft of personal belongings.
It goes without saying that artworks should not be touched. For safety reasons, keep some distance from the artworks. When visiting with young children, we ask the parents or supervisors to be extra attentive. Running around in the museum is prohibited.
Loud phone calls with a mobile phone are not permitted inside the building.
Smoking is only allowed on the terrace of Van Abbe café.
For wedding reports, please contact info@vanabbemuseum.nl or view the wedding brochure for more information.
For security reasons within the building, the instructions of security personnel must be followed at all times.
Bringing or consuming food and drinks from outside of the museum is not allowed anywhere.
Dogs are not allowed in the museum, with the exception of guide dogs.
During the visit you can use the open wireless network (WIFI), based on Fair Use Policy and entirely at your own risk. The Van Abbemuseum is not responsible for any claims arising from the activities of the users.
