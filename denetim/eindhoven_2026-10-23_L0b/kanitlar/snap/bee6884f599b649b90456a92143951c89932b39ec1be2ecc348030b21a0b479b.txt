This ticket is available to visitors aged 12 to 17 and students with a valid student card. Please bring valid identification or your student card when collecting your wristband.

Is there a family ticket? And what are the costs?
Yes. Groups visiting Dutch Design Week (DDW) with children or young people can purchase a family ticket. The family ticket is available exclusively at the box office during DDW, from Saturday 17 to Sunday 25 October, for €88.00 (valid for a maximum of two adults). The entire group must be present at the ticket desk to collect it.
A family ticket is valid for up to four people, including no more than two adults, in the following combinations:
1 adult with 3 children
2 adults with 2 children
2 adults with 1 child
Does your group fall outside these combinations? Simply purchase individual tickets through our online ticket shop or buy your tickets by debit card at the door.
Age groups:
Children can be included in a family ticket if they are aged between 12 and 17, or if they are students and can present a valid student card.
Children aged 11 and under can visit DDW free of charge. Visitors aged 18 and over who are not students must purchase a regular ticket or can be counted as an adult in one of the family combinations listed above.

Which ticket do I need if I use a wheelchair?
Visitors who use a wheelchair pay the regular ticket price. We do, however, provide one free DDW wristband for a companion. This can be collected at the door at one of the physical ticket sale locations:
Klokgebouw
Design Academy Eindhoven Graduation Show
Strijp TX
Sectie-C
Eindhoven Centraal
Van Abbemuseum
Please note: The ticket desk at Eindhoven Central Station is not easily accessible for wheelchair users or visitors with reduced mobility. Our team will be happy to assist you and will be available on the ground floor. Please approach a member of staff if you need assistance.
Is there a combined train and DDW ticket?
A combination ticket may be available through the NS Spoordeelwinkel, combining a same-day return train journey from your departure station with a visit to DDW.

DDW Student Tickets
What is the price of a ticket for children up to 11 years old?
Children up to 11 years old can visit Dutch Design Week for free.
What is the price of a ticket for children aged 12 to 17?
Children aged 12 to 17, or those with a school or student ID, can visit Dutch Design Week with a discounted DDW Student ticket.
The Student tickets cost €17,50 until 10 October. From 11 October, ticket prices increase to €19,50.
Get your tickets
Please note: Your wristband is valid once it has been secured around your wrist. If you remove or cut it off yourself, it will no longer be valid. You cannot collect a wristband on behalf of someone else.
How to order tickets for pupils?
The ordering process is conducted via the order form. A quote will be sent after the order has been manually checked and all details are correct. The order is final when the quote has been digitally signed. Payment is possible with the payment link on the quote. The invoice will be sent after DDW, payment can also be made when receiving the invoice. Unfortunately, we are unable to process requests via email.
How much do DDW tickets for school classes cost?
We offer a reduced student rate of €17,50 per DDW Student Ticket.
It’s possible for schools to pay via web order with CJP. When ordering tickets, indicate that you want to pay this way. It is possible to pay a portion of the bill through a CJP web order.
Unused tickets are non-refundable. Therefore, we recommend carefully considering the number of DDW tickets you reserve in advance.
How much do DDW tickets for chaperones cost?
When you book tickets for students, you will receive one free DDW regular ticket for a chaperone according to the -5 rule:
20 pupils - 1 free wristband for a chaperone
35 pupils - 2 free wristbands for chaperones
55 pupils - 3 free wristbands for chaperones
75 pupils - 4 free wristbands for chaperones
And so on
In the order form, indicate the total number of chaperones you need. The calculation for the number of free and paid wristbands will be done automatically. Extra DDW tickets cost €29,50 per chaperone.
How will I receive the tickets?
Schools have the option to order tickets in the form of DDW wristbands.
Wristbands can be collected before Dutch Design Week at an agreed time from the Dutch Design Foundation office at Strijp-S, Eindhoven. During DDW, wristbands can be collected from the pick-up point at the Klokgebouw op Strijp-S, Eindhoven. The wristbands must be collected all at once.
The students (and I) are coming to Dutch Design Week by coach, where should we go?
Large groups can use the Kiss & Ride. This is located on Achtseweg Zuid, in Eindhoven, on the opposite side of Beukenlaan, clearly marked with DDW Kiss & Ride signs.
At the Kiss & Ride location, the coach can temporarily park near Station Strijp-S so that all students can disembark. However, we strongly urge that the coach does not remain parked there. There is ample free parking for coaches further down Achtseweg Zuid.
Do schools and educational institutions get tickets on invoice?
As a school or educational institution, you can purchase tickets on invoice. We offer a reduced student rate of €17,50 per ticket. For every 20 students, you will receive one complimentary regular DDW ticket for supervisors.
