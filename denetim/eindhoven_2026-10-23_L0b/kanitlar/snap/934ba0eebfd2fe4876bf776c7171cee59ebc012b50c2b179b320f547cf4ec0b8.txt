Klimcentrum Neoliet Eindhoven-Zuid
At Klimcentrum Neoliet Eindhoven-Zuid there are excellent climbing walls, a rock wall and rope courses. The center is ideally suited for training climbers and cadres. The entire space is for this. But also for an event experience such as parties and arrangements. The center features a large professional restaurant and meeting and teaching rooms. There are approximately 100 routes from 3+ to 7c+. The heights are variable from 11 to a height of 15 meters.
Lots of climbing fun for every climbing level.
