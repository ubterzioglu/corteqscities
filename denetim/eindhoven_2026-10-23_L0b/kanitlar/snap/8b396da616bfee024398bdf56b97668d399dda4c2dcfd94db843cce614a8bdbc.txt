Exchanging a foreign driving licence
Do you have a foreign driving licence and do you live in the Netherlands? In the Netherlands you will be allowed a limited period for driving with a foreign driving licence. After that, you must exchange the driving licence through your municipality. If it’s possible will depend on the country that issued the driving licence. You can always exchange a valid EU / EFTA driving licence.
Conditions for exchange
Please note! You must meet all the conditions.
You have a valid driving licence from the EU / EFTA or from a country with which we have an agreement. An EU/EFTA driving licence is allowed to be expired.
You are registered in the Personal Records Database (BRP) .
You have an EU/EFTA nationality. Not? In that case:
you have a temporary or permanent residence permit (exchanging is not possible if you are waiting for a (new) residence permit), or
you are in the Netherlands under Association Decision 1/80 of the EEC/Turkey Association Council, or
you fall under the Temporary Protection Directive (residence permit 46).
Expat scheme
Are you in the Netherlands for work (highly skilled migrant) and does the expat scheme apply to you? Then you can exchange your driving licence (from any country). Read more in if you are in the Netherlands for work.
Check if your driving licence is suitable
Is your driving licence not from an EU/EFTA country? Then check the list of countries below. Do you have a driving licence from a country that is not on the list, for example a driving licence from America, and you don't apply to the expat scheme? Then you cannot exchange.
You must also have lived in the country for 185 days in the year you obtained the licence. Only then can you exchange if you have a driving licence that is not from an EU/EFTA country.

Previously held a Dutch driving licence?
Do you have a driving licence from a country that has no agreement with the Netherlands? But have you had a Dutch driving licence in the past that was valid after June 30, 1985? If so, it is possible to exchange your driving licence. You will then get the same categories you had on your Dutch driving licence.
Exchanging possible? Please visit your municipality
Start before your driving licence expires
If your driving licence from outside the EU/EEA is about to expire, start the exchange process at your local council. We cannot exchange an expired driving licence. Additional documents, such as a health declaration from the CBR, can be submitted later.
Does the checklist reveal that you can indeed exchange your foreign driving licence? Please visit your municipality and bring the following:
A colour passport photo. Check the requirements.
Your foreign driving licence. Does your licence contain characters that are not used in the Netherlands, such as in Japanese or Greek? Have the licence translated by a certified translator, the embassy or the consulate.
An old Dutch driving licence, if you have one.
A valid Decision on the Evidence Rule from the Dutch tax authority if you are working in the Netherlands and hold a driving licence from outside the EU or EFTA.
A Health Declaration Form from the CBR if you have a driving licence from outside the EU/EFTA. You may need one for licences from within the EU/EFTA as well. For more information, please visit the CBR’s website.
Fill out the application form at the municipality and pay the fee. The fee may vary per municipality.
Your municipality will forward the application and all the documentation to us. Only the municipality can submit the application.
Everything in order? You will receive a letter within 15 working days to inform you that you can pick up your Dutch driving licence at your municipality after 5 working days. If any of the required information is missing, we will inform you in writing as well. This will delay the exchange of your driving licence.
You will not get your licence back.
Expired driving licence from Iceland, Liechtenstein or Switzerland
Do you have an expired driving licence from Iceland, Liechtenstein or Switzerland? Please bring an original statement from the issuing authority stating that they have no objection to the issuance of a Dutch driving licence.
If exchanging your licence is not possible
If you are unable to exchange your foreign driving licence for a Dutch one, you will need to take both the theory and practical tests at the CBR in order to obtain a Dutch driving licence.
Driving without a licence during the exchange
Whether or not you continue to drive during the exchange process, is up to you. You are allowed to operate a vehicle if you have a valid driving licence. If the police stops you while driving, you must be able to present your driving licence. The police may fine you if you cannot comply. The risk of getting stopped by the police is slim as long as you keep to the traffic rules. If you are stopped nonetheless, for example during a roadside check, the police can contact us to verify whether you have submitted an application for exchange and whether you have handed in a valid driving licence. The risk of getting a fine is therefore small, but present. It is up to you to decide whether or not you want to run this risk.
The Fine Database of the Public Prosecutor (OM) states the amount of the fine for failing to produce a driving licence.
Bring your passport or ID
Should you decide to drive, be sure to bring a document that proves who you are (identification), such as a passport or ID.
Insurance
If you have a valid driving licence and have taken out insurance, you are insured. Even if you are unable to present the driving licence at that time. For more information, please contact your insurance company.
Other countries
We advise against driving in other countries during the exchange procedure. They may not be able to verify your information. And the rules and consequences may differ.
Surrendering a category
If you no longer wish to use a category on your driving licence, you may surrender that category. You will need to complete what is known as a waiver form(pdf, 689kb).
Getting a category back
You do not lose your category indefinitely. When renewing your driving licence, the category can be reapplied to your licence. This does require you to complete a Health Declaration Form.
