Philips de Jongh Wandelpark
Philips de Jongh Wandelpark is a park in the north of Eindhoven. Enjoy monumental trees, landscaped walking paths and beautiful light.
15 min. bike
The historical Philips De Jongh Wandelpark is one of the many monuments which Philips left to Eindhoven. It was named after Anton Philips' wife, Anna Philips de Jongh. It's donated to the city of Eindhoven in 1920. The park can be found in the Strijp district, near Landgoed De Wielewaal, the place where the Philips family lived.
