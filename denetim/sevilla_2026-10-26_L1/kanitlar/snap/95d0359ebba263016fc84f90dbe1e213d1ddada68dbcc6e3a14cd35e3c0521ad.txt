Univiaje ticket
It is purchased on board the bus or from the sales clerk at the airport terminal stop or at Customer Service points. It is valid for a single journey.
The Multiviaje Card is also valid.
Once the ticket has been purchased, it will not be refunded under any circumstances, not even if it is lost or stolen.

* Applications in Customer Service Departament at Prado San Sebastián, Ponce de León. Andalucía Av. 11 and municipal district offices.
Return ticket
To be purchased on board the bus or from the sales employee at the airport terminal stop or at Customer Service points. Valid only on the day of purchase.
Once the ticket has been purchased, it will not be refunded under any circumstances, not even if it is lost or stolen.
Non-use of the ticket does not entitle the passenger to a refund.
8 €
* Invalid for transfer from this line to another of our network.
* Applications in Customer Service Departament at Prado San Sebastián, Ponce de León. Andalucía Av. 11 and municipal district offices.
Airport Monthly Card
Nominative card valid for its holder on the Airport Bus and on the ordinary TUSSAM service for 30 calendar days following the first cancellation, with no limit on the number of journeys.
It can be requested through the Tussam website or at any of its Customer Service points.
It can be recharged for new monthly periods through the Tussam website or at its customer service points in Prado San Sebastían, Ponce de León and Avenida Andalucía.
24.60 €
* Invalid for transfer from this line to another of our network.
