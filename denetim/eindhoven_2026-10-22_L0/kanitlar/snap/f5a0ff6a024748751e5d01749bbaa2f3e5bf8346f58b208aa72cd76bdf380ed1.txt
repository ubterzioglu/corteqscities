Strijp-S
Strijp-S is the creative heart of Eindhoven. An innovative place where you as a creative and enterprising person are stimulated to develop yourself. It is a vibrant area thanks to all the initiatives that come about here. Everyone flourishes here. Both young and more renowned companies are located here. They all have a creative streak.
