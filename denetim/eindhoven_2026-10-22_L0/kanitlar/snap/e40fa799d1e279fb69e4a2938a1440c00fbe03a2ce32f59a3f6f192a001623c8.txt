Surprising, innovative, inspiring, unique. A market that has lots of everything. It is visited by the locals on a weekly basis. A market that offers vintage, curiosities, good food, design and technology. Creative and not too stylish. Typical Eindhoven, you might say. A place you visit without a shopping list, even though you're unlikely to leave it empty-handed. Simply because you run into lots of fun, handy, delicious and surprising products.
You’ll find a jewellery designer who creates amazing 3D prints, right next to tasty cheeses and fresh flowers, and a stand filled with fun things to brighten up your city garden. This market's offer changes often. On top of the regulars, you'll find new stands every week.
The Catharina Market takes place at a unique location in the city centre of Eindhoven, every Saturday between 10.00h and 17.00h. And thanks to the many shops, restaurants and bars within walking distance, you can make a day of it.
