PSV Museum
Discover over one hundred years of PSV history!
The PSV Museum provides a wonderful day out for PSV fans. You will discover over one hundred years of PSV history. The Hall of Fame features the greatest players, including Jan van Beveren and samba star Romário. There is a great collection of PSV memorabilia, special photos and unique video footage. With the free audio guide you also get extra information and special sound fragments.
Fun for children
Children will also have a good time, with a touch screen projection globe full of special places, a periscope that allows them to take a look inside the stadium and the Phoxy Stadium where they can play a game of football.
