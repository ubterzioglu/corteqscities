What is OVpay?
OVpay is the new way of paying on public transport. With OVpay, you check in and out contactless. Instead of using an OV-chipkaart , you check in and out with your debit card, credit card, or mobile phone—or with your OV-pas .
More information about OVpay .
How travelling with your debit or credit card works
Check in using your contactless debit card, credit card, or the payment card on your mobile phone.
When you get off, check out using the same card you checked in with.
The cost of your journey is automatically charged to your bank account.
Read more about checking in and out in public transport .
Tip – Take your debit card, credit card, or OV-pas out of your wallet when checking in and out. Or remove it from your phone case. This ensures you check in with the correct card and prevents accidentally using two cards at the same time. Did this happen anyway? Visit the OVpay website to find out how to request a refund.
Things to keep in mind when travelling with your debit card
Checking in and out for one person – You can only check in and out for yourself. It is not possible to travel with multiple people using one card.
Use the same card for check-in and check-out – Always check out using the same card or phone you used to check in.
Discounts and season tickets – Check the transport provider’s website to see whether your season ticket or discount product can also be used with a debit card.
OVpay account – Would you like to set up age-based discounts, travel first class, or view your journey history? You can find all this information and more in your OVpay account.
Frequently asked questions
How do I put an age discount on my debit card and/or OVpas?
Are you under 12 or over 65? Then you can travel with a 34% discount on the bus, tram, and metro. Age discounts can be added via your OVpay account.
Log in to your OVpay account via the OVpay app or via OVpay.nl .
Add your debit card or OV-pas to your account.
Press 'Add age discount' and follow the steps in the app.
Check the OVpay website for more information.
How is the price of travel calculated?
OVpay prices are the same as OV-chipkaart prices when travelling in second class and without a discount or season ticket. Want to know how much your journey will cost? In the 9292 journey planner, you can see how much your journey should cost. Compare this price with the amount withdrawn from your account. It can sometimes take a day for the money to be withdrawn from your account.
Tip – Do you have an OV-chipkaart? Check whether you can already switch to OVpay by using the OVpay Switch Helper .
In the Switch Helper you can see, for example, whether the subscriptions you use on your OV-chipkaart are also available on the OV-pas or for travelling with a debit or credit card.
I forgot to check out with OVpay. How do I get my money back?
Did you miss your check-out? Or accidentally checked in with OVpay while trying to scan your e-ticket? Then you can claim your money back. You will recognise the payment from OVpay on your account by the statement with the reference 'NLOV' followed by 14 digits.
This is how you ask for your money back:
Go to your travel overview on OVpay.nl/en
Follow the step-by-step instructions.
Is OVpay secure?
OVpay is a secure way to pay on public transport. Any payment you make on public transport will be protected, just like with regular debit card payments.
You will recognise the OVpay payment on your bank statement by its description. It always starts with NLOV and then 14 digits. If you still think something is wrong, you can always contact your bank or OVpay customer service .
