Borrowing money from DUO

Information for status holders 2013 Act and 2021 Act

Are you a status holder and are you integrating under the 2013 Act or the 2021 Act? Then this information concerns you.  The Dutch judge ('Raad van State') decided that DUO cannot fine you for taking too long to finish your integration. Neither can DUO require you to repay an integration loan. You don't need to take any action. If you are required to integrate, you need to continue your integration. If anything changes for you, DUO will inform you by a personal letter. Also read the related news item.

If you are integrating, you may be able to borrow money from DUO. For the course and the exam.

Can I borrow?

Are you an asylum status holder and are you required to integrate under the 2021 civic integration act ('Wet inburgering 2021')? Then you cannot borrow from DUO. Your municipality pays for your course costs and 2 exam attempts.

Are you not required to integrate, but do you want to anyway? DUO will check when you were first registered with the municipality. You can borrow until 3 years after. Even if you are from an EU/EER- country Link opent externe pagina in een nieuw tabblad or Switzerland.

Other people that are integrating, can almost always borrow from DUO.

Applying for a loan

  1. Log in to Mijn Inburgering.
  2. Go to: 'Lening aanvragen' (Apply for loan)
Log in to Mijn Inburgeringwith DigId
Log in problems

If you cannot log in to Mijn Inburgering, please use the ‘Aanvraag lening voor cursus en/of examen’ form. You will receive an answer from DUO within 8 weeks.

For what can you borrow?

You can borrow in order to pay for:

  • a course at level B1 or B2
  • a course at level A2
  • a literacy course
  • the exam.

You need to take the course at a school listed on the website zoekinburgerschool.nl Link opent externe pagina in een nieuw tabblad.

No money paid into account

Borrowing money means that DUO pays for your course or exam. The money will not be paid into your bank account. The amount that DUO pays for you equals your loan.

Do I need to repay?

Asylum status holders never need to repay their loan.

If you are not an asylum status holder, sometimes your loan is (partially) cancelled. This means you don’t need to pay back your loan. Or you don’t need to repay the full amount. If you do need to pay back the loan, you also pay interest.

Interest rates

If you borrow money from DUO, you have a debt. As long as you have a debt with DUO, you pay interest on it. During the past years, these were the interest rates per year:
