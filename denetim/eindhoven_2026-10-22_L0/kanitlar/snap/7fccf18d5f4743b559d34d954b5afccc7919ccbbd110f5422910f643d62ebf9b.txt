Philips Historical Products
The development of science and technology has produced many thousands of Philips products. In the exhibition Philips Historic Products, the development of the product groups is shown with sometimes unique, mostly working devices. As a visitor, you'll get a good idea of the historical heritage of Philips since its foundation in 1891.
