Van Abbehuis
In the historic villa in the heart of Eindhoven, you are challenged to look and think differently. With groundbreaking exhibitions, workshops, performances, and lectures, the Van Abbehuis is a place to learn, make, and meet for art lovers, curious visitors, and young creators.
