General Admission Ticket
Discover La Cartuja Stadium with our official guides. This experience will allow you to uncover all the secrets of the current home of Real Betis.
During the tour, you will explore some of the most iconic areas of the stadium. You will visit the first-team dressing room, walk down the tunnel to the pitch, and even sit on the players' benches. You will also see our exhibition area, where you can explore a journey through our more than 118 years of Green and White legend.
Prices
12€ Adult ticket Over 14 years old. Ticket office price: €14.
8€ Soy Bético ticket You must present your Soy Bético card and ID card at the Tour entrance. Ticket office price: €10.
10€ Group ticket For groups of 25 people or more. Check availability and bookings via email: betistour@realbetisbalompie.es

Match Day Tour
Experience the excitement of match day like never before at La Cartuja Stadium. Accompanied by our official guides, you will discover the stadium in the hours leading up to the match, as everything is being prepared for the show to begin. During the tour, you will access exclusive areas at a unique moment.
You will visit the first-team dressing room already set up for the match and walk through the players' tunnel to the pitch. You can also sit on the players' benches and feel like part of the team. Enjoy the stadium in full transformation, surrounded by the anticipation, energy, and unmistakable atmosphere of a Real Betis match day.

Itinerary and access
Access to the tour will take place at La Cartuja Stadium (NOT Benito Villamarín) through Gate 2, the entrance to the Presidential Box.
We recommend arriving early.
People with reduced mobility should notify betistour@realbetisbalompie.es
Useful information
Duration: approximately 50 minutes.
Meeting point: La Cartuja Stadium (NOT Benito Villamarín), Gate 2
Please arrive 10 minutes before the scheduled time.
Punctuality is essential. If you arrive late and the tour has already started at the meeting point, you will not be able to join and will need to purchase a new ticket, which is non-refundable. This new ticket will be subject to availability.
Children must be accompanied by an adult.
Reduced mobility: Activity partially adapted for people with reduced mobility, subject to adjustments to the itinerary. Visitors who require assistance should notify betistour@realbetisbalompie.es and be accompanied
Languages: Spanish and English (other languages on request, not guaranteed).
The visit requires a minimum number of people. Otherwise, the activity may be cancelled
The first team's activities and operational requirements may require the cancellation or rescheduling of tour sessions. In such cases, affected visitors will be informed and offered alternative time slots or a refund.

Preguntas frecuentes
If I buy the ticket online, do I have to go to the ticket office?
No. You must go directly to the entrance, next to the main gate on the preferred side of the stadium.
I have been given tickets for the Tour, what do I do when I arrive at the stadium?
Check that you have been allocated a day and time. If so, you can go directly to the tour entrance.
If I am a member, where do I get my ticket?
Through the website. You can check last minute availability at the stadium entrance desk, next to the tour entrance gate.
From what age do children pay?
During 2026, children born after 1 July 2023 do not pay.
I have a membership card, can someone else use it?
NO. The reduced price is non-transferable
Is there any discount for the Tour?
There is a reduced rate for members, Soy Bético and children under 14 (born after 1 July 2012).
If I am Bético, do I get a discount?
It does not vary from the one that currently appears on the website
What is the maximum capacity that has been established?
Ranges from 30 to 36 persons per shift
Is there access to the toilet during the visit?
No toilet facilities are available throughout the tour and museum visit.
Can an offered date and time be cancelled due to the needs of the Club?
At the time of purchase, it is necessary to provide a telephone number and email address. In the event of a cancellation, the Club staff will offer relocations on the same or other days, at the same or different times, as far in advance as possible
If I am not interested in the new timetable offered, what can I do?
In this case, the Club will reimburse the amount of the ticket for the cancelled timetable.
How can I find out the conditions - price, opening times, minimum and maximum number of visitors - for the group rate?
For more information, please contact recepcion@realbetisbalompie.es
If I have a discount for being a member or Soy Bético, do I have to show my membership card?
The Club may require identification as a member or Soy Bético from anyone who enters the stadium with a ticket at a discounted price.
What happens if I arrive late to the entrance and the gate is already closed?
Access closes 5 minutes after the scheduled start time.
